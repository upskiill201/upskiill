## Where Skillshare Genuinely Excels

Skillshare's format shines in creative disciplines where a tangible finished project is the natural unit of learning — designing a logo, illustrating a scene, editing a short film. Its instructor-led model brings real expertise and a portfolio-building structure that gamified micro-lessons aren't designed to replicate. If your goal is a specific creative project with expert guidance, Skillshare's approach fits that goal directly.

## Where Teyro Takes a Different Approach

Teyro isn't trying to replicate Skillshare's creative-class format — it's solving a different problem: the fact that most self-paced video courses, Skillshare included, depend entirely on personal motivation to sustain multi-week engagement, and personal motivation is exactly the resource that reliably runs out.
