## What can I see?

| Signal | What it tells you |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lesson opens** | Which lessons pull learners in |
| **Quits and last step** | The exact step where people give up |
| **First-try misses** | Questions that confuse most learners |
| **Learner list** | Each learner's progress in your course |

## How do I act on it?

- **Fix the lesson.** If many learners quit at the same step, that step is too long or unclear.
- **Rewrite the question.** A question most people miss first time may be testing the wrong thing.
- **Nudge.** Send a personal nudge to learners who have gone quiet.
- **Cheer.** Celebrate learners who are moving, which keeps them moving.

Nudges and cheers have cooldowns and only reach your own enrolled learners, through their own notification settings — helpful, never spammy.

## Why these numbers and not views?

Views tell you who arrived. Quits and first-try misses tell you why people left — and that is what you can fix. See [why students don't finish my course](/blog/why-students-dont-finish-my-course).
