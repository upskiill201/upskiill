## What Gets Measured

### Mission-Level Analytics

Every mission in your track gets detailed analytics:

- **Completion rate** — What percentage of learners finish this mission?
- **Average time** — How long does it take to complete?
- **Reattempt rate** — How many learners retry this mission?
- **Drop-off point** — Where exactly do learners get stuck?
- **Common errors** — What mistakes do learners make most often?

### Learner-Level Analytics

Each learner's journey is tracked:

- **Start date** — When did they begin your course?
- **Current mission** — Where are they now?
- **Streak length** — How consistently are they learning?
- **Skill progress** — Which skills have they improved?
- **Struggle score** — Where are they experiencing difficulty?

### Cohort Analytics

Compare groups of learners over time:

- **Completion by cohort** — Are newer learners doing better?
- **Skill progression by week** — Is the curriculum working?
- **Engagement trends** — Are learners more or less active over time?

## How to Use Analytics to Improve Your Course

### Identify Problem Missions

If a mission has <50% completion rate or high reattempt rate, it's probably too hard, confusing, or poorly structured. Analytics tell you exactly which missions need work.

### Find Your Best Content

If a mission has >90% completion with low reattempt rate and high engagement, it's working well. Study these missions to understand what resonates with your audience — then replicate that format elsewhere.

### Optimize Drop-Off Points

Drop-off analytics show exactly where learners leave your course. Often, the problem is one mission that's too hard, causing a cascade of drop-offs. Fix that one mission and watch completion improve.

### Understand Your Audience

Different learner cohorts behave differently. Analytics reveal who your learners are (by behavior), what they struggle with, and what they need more of. Use these insights to refine your content and teaching approach.
