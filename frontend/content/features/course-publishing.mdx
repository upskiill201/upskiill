## Why build a course on Teyro?

Because finishing is the problem most courses never solve. Long video courses sell well and stall early. On Teyro, the platform does the part creators can't: every day it gives your learners a reason to come back — a [streak](/features/streaks-and-freeze) to protect, a [league](/features/leagues-leaderboards) to climb, [quests](/features/daily-chests) to finish.

## How are lessons structured?

Every lesson uses the same four steps, which keeps courses consistent and learners on track:

| Step | What you create |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Learn** | The idea — text, code, images or video |
| **Apply** | Practice with instant feedback |
| **Reflect** | A prompt to put it in their own words |
| **Deepen** | Optional extras for curious learners |

## How does pricing work?

| Option | How it works |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Free** | Anyone can take the whole course |
| **Paid** | You set the yearly price; the monthly plan is one sixth of it — a $60 course is $60 a year or $10 a month |
| **Your share** | 70% of every payment, including every renewal |
| **Try first** | Learners get the first two lessons free on paid courses |
| **Coupons** | Discount a learner's first payment; share as a link |

## What else comes with a course?

- a **course community** that learners join after two lessons, with you as admin — see [community](/features/community-qa)
- [analytics](/features/analytics-dashboard) showing where learners stop and which questions they miss
- [payouts](/features/payouts) to your bank or mobile money
