## The Creator's Challenge

### Why Most Courses Fail to Launch

The biggest barrier to course creation isn't knowledge — it's format. Creating a 20-hour video course requires:

- Scripting 20+ hours of content
- Recording high-quality video
- Editing and post-production
- Uploading and organizing on a platform
- Marketing and driving traffic

Each step has a high barrier to entry. Teyro replaces this with a more approachable format: interactive missions that learners can complete in 15 minutes each. A 10-mission course takes a fraction of the time to create — and gets completed 5x more often.

### The "Expertise Trap"

Experts often overestimate what beginners need and underestimate how much guidance they require. Teyro's course publishing tools include templates and guided structure that help creators organize their knowledge into a logical, beginner-friendly progression. You don't need to be an instructional designer — just a clear communicator.

## How Teyro Course Publishing Works

### Step 1: Choose Your Track

Pick the skill or topic you want to teach. Teyro provides templates for popular tracks (coding, business, design, productivity) but you can create any subject.

### Step 2: Build Missions

Instead of recording lectures, you create individual missions:

- **Learn mission** — Introduce a concept interactively
- **Apply mission** — Give learners a problem to solve
- **Reflect mission** — Ask them what they learned
- **Challenge mission** — Push them with a harder problem

Each mission takes 2–5 minutes to create (compared to 15–30 minutes of video for equivalent content).

### Step 3: Organize into a Track

Arrange missions in a logical order. Teyro suggests prerequisite chains based on your track selection. You can customize the order and add prerequisites manually.

### Step 4: Publish and Share

One click publishes your track to the Teyro catalog. You can share it anywhere, and Teyro handles the hosting, updates, and learner management.
