## The Power of Social Comparison

### You Perform Better When Watched

The audience effect is well-documented: people perform better on tasks when they know others can see their performance. Teyro's leaderboards make your learning visible — not to your boss or friends, but to a community of fellow learners. This gentle social pressure keeps you showing up.

### Tiered Leagues Give Everyone a Goal

A flat leaderboard is demoralizing if you're at the bottom. Teyro uses tiered leagues:

- **Bronze** — Starting out, building habits
- **Silver** — Consistent, showing real progress
- **Gold** — Dedicated, above average
- **Platinum** — Elite, consistently high engagement
- **Diamond** — Top performers, learning leaders

Each tier has clear entry criteria. A Bronze learner's goal is to reach Silver. A Silver learner's goal is Gold. This tiered structure gives every learner a realistic next goal, preventing the "I can never catch up" feeling that flat leaderboards create.

### Healthy Competition, Not Toxic Pressure

Teyro's competitive features are designed to motivate, not stress:

- **Leagues reset regularly** — Everyone gets fresh starts
- **Relative ranking** — You see where you are, not just absolute numbers
- **Personal bests** — Your only true competitor is your past self
- **Celebration, not shame** — Climbing ranks feels like winning
