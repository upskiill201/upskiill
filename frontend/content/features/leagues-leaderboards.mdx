## How do Teyro leagues work?

Earning any XP in a week puts you on a board with other learners in your league. Your position updates live after every lesson. Weeks run from Monday to Sunday, on one clock (UTC) for everyone.

## How do I move up?

Finish the week in the promotion zone. When the week ends:

- the **top finishers** move up a league
- the **bottom five** on boards of ten or more move down (never out of Bronze)
- everyone else stays put

## Are there bots?

No. Every learner on your board is a real person. While Teyro is growing, everyone active may share one weekly board; once enough people are learning, it splits into leagues of around 30.

## What if I'm not competitive?

Leagues are optional motivation. Your [streak](/features/streaks-and-freeze) and [daily quests](/features/daily-chests) work fine on their own — but for many people a board of real names is what gets one more lesson done on Sunday night.
