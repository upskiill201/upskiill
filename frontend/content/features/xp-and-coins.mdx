## What is XP for?

XP (experience points) measures effort. You earn it from lessons and right answers, and it does three jobs:

1. fills your **daily goal** (about 5 to 20 minutes' worth — you choose)
2. moves you up the **[weekly league](/features/leagues-leaderboards)**
3. raises your **level** over time

## What are coins for?

Coins are Teyro's currency, and you only get them by learning — from lessons, [daily quests and chests](/features/daily-chests).

| Spend coins on | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Streak freeze** | Covers a missed day |
| **Streak repair** | Saves a broken streak within 48 hours |
| **Heart refill** | All five hearts back, for 120 coins |
| **Power-ups** | For example, a shield that absorbs one mistake |
| **Cosmetics** | Frames and backdrops for your profile |

## Why do some shop items say "locked"?

Some items unlock through learning first, then cost coins. The locked card shows your progress towards it, so it doubles as a goal.

## Are achievements the same as rewards?

No. Teyro has 25 collectible achievements across six families. They are medals for milestones and pay nothing, which keeps the economy honest: coins and XP come from doing the work.
