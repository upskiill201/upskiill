## The Psychology of Game Rewards

### Variable Reward Schedules

B.F. Skinner's research on operant conditioning showed that **variable ratio reinforcement** (rewards delivered unpredictably) creates the strongest and most persistent behavior patterns. This is why slot machines are addictive — and why games are fun.

Teyro applies this principle: you never know exactly when the next XP pop-up will appear, when a bonus coin will drop, or when a surprise reward will trigger. This unpredictability keeps your brain engaged and looking forward to the next session.

### Immediate Feedback Loops

XP and coins provide immediate feedback — you complete a task, you see your XP increase. This tight feedback loop (action → reward) is the same mechanism that makes video games compelling. Your brain learns: "Action = reward → do more." Over time, learning itself becomes the rewarding activity.

### Loss Aversion with Coins

Coins aren't just earned — they can be spent on cosmetic items, streak freezes, or bonus content. Having coins at risk creates another layer of motivation. You work to earn coins and feel motivated to protect your accumulated wealth.
