## What Your Payout History Shows

### Per-Transaction Details

Every payout shows:

- **Date** — When the payment was processed
- **Amount** — Exact dollar amount paid
- **Source** — Which course/track generated the revenue
- **Learner count** — How many learners contributed to this payout
- **Fee breakdown** — Platform fees, taxes (if applicable), net amount

### Summary Statistics

At a glance, your dashboard shows:

- **Total earnings** — Lifetime revenue across all content
- **This period** — Earnings for current week/month
- **Last payout** — When and how much you were last paid
- **Next payout** — Estimated date and projected amount
- **Growth trend** — Your earnings trajectory over time

### Export and Reporting

Your complete payout history can be exported at any time:

- **CSV export** — For accounting and tax preparation
- **PDF reports** — For bank deposits or loan applications
- **Annual summary** — Year-end earnings overview
