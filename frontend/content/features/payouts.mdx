## How do earnings work?

Every paid enrolment adds a line to your earnings ledger in Studio: the amount, your share and the date. The ledger is append-only — corrections are new lines, never edits — and your revenue share is recorded on each transaction, so a later change to your agreement can't rewrite past earnings.

## When is money available?

| Stage | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Pending** | The payment is within its 14-day clearing period |
| **Available** | Cleared and ready to withdraw |
| **Requested** | You asked for a payout; it is being reviewed and processed |
| **Paid out** | Sent to your account |

## How do I withdraw?

Add a payout method in Studio — a **bank account** or a **mobile money** wallet — then request a payout once your available balance reaches **$50**.

## What's my revenue share?

It is set in your creator agreement. For how platforms compare, see [course creator revenue share comparison](/blog/course-creator-revenue-share-comparison), and apply at [teyro.app/teach](/teach).
