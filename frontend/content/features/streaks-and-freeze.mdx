## What counts as a streak day?

Finishing at least one lesson that day, in your own time zone. Teyro keeps a calendar of every day you showed up, so you can watch the habit form.

## What happens on a day I can't learn?

That is what **freezes** are for. If you have one in your bank, it covers the missed day automatically and your streak continues. You can hold up to two at a time. Freezes come from [chests](/features/daily-chests) and the shop, bought with coins you earn.

## What if I lose my streak?

You get a second chance. For **48 hours** after a streak of two or more days breaks, you can **repair** it with coins. Each break can be repaired once — it saves a genuine slip, it is not a way to buy a streak you never had.

## Why do streaks help at all?

Because the hardest part of learning is starting today. A streak turns "I should practise" into "I don't want to lose this". Once you reach 7 days you join the **Streak Society**, and streak milestones earn extra chests along the way.

## Can a streak make things worse?

It can, if it becomes about the number rather than the learning — see [Duolingo streak burnout](/blog/duolingo-streak-burnout). That is why Teyro's daily goal can be as small as about 5 minutes and why freezes exist: keep the streak humane and it keeps working for you.
