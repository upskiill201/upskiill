## The Psychology of Streaks

### Loss Aversion

Behavioral economics shows that humans feel losses roughly 2× more intensely than equivalent gains. A 21-day streak represents 21 units of accumulated progress. Breaking it means losing all 21 units — which feels devastating. This asymmetry makes streak protection a more powerful motivator than streak building.

Teyro displays your streak prominently on the home screen. Every day you complete a mission, your streak grows. The visual representation of your progress — and the fear of losing it — creates a consistent pull to show up daily.

### The "Broken Record" Effect

Once a streak starts, your brain treats it as a continuous commitment. Each passing day reinforces the identity: "I'm someone who learns every day." This identity-based habit is far more durable than goal-based habit ("I want to learn Python"). Teyro's streaks build identity through consistent action.

### The Plateau Problem

Around week 3, most learners hit a motivation plateau. Streaks help push through this because breaking the streak feels like a loss. The milestone celebrations at 7, 14, 30, 100, and 365 days provide extra fuel during the plateau — moments of recognition that keep you going.

## Freeze Protection: The Safety Net

### Why Freezes Exist

Without protection, one bad day = broken streak = "It's all over" = giving up. This is the most common failure pattern in habit formation. Teyro's freeze protection prevents this catastrophic spiral by giving you safety valves.

### How Freeze Works

When you can't complete a mission for any reason (illness, travel, emergency), Teyro offers a freeze option:

1. **Freeze a day** — Skip today without breaking your streak
2. **No streak reset** — Your streak continues as if you completed the day
3. **Limited freezes** — You get a few freeze tokens per month, preventing abuse while protecting against real life
4. **Renewal** — Freeze tokens refresh monthly, ensuring ongoing protection

This system respects that life happens while maintaining the psychological benefits of consistency. You never feel guilty for missing a day, but you still maintain your streak.
