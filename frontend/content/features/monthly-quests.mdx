## The Anatomy of a Monthly Quest

### One Monthly Goal

Each month you choose (or are recommended) one primary learning goal. This is your quest. Examples:

- **"Complete the Python Basics track"** — 60 daily XP target
- **"Master Business English communication"** — 80 daily XP target
- **"Build 3 real-world projects"** — 100 daily XP target

The target is calibrated to your level. Beginners get lower targets; advanced learners get higher ones. The key: **one goal. One month. Clear finish line.**

### Milestone Sub-Quests

Your monthly quest contains 3 milestone checkpoints that break the month into motivating phases:

1. **Warm-Up (Day 10)** — Reach ⅓ of your target days → Unlock a bonus reward
2. **Halfway Hero (Day 20)** — Reach ⅔ of your target days → Unlock a bigger reward
3. **Monthly Champion (Day 30)** — Hit your full target → Unlock the grand finale reward + badge

Each milestone is a celebration moment. Teyro runs a full celebration animation (the Celebration Engine) when you hit each one — confetti, sound effects, and a reward drop. This creates dopamine hits at 10, 20, and 30 days instead of one vague finish line at "whenever."

### Daily XP Targets

Your daily target is the number of XP you need to earn per day to stay on track for your monthly goal:

- **20 XP/day** → 12 target days (40% of month) — Warm-Up tier
- **50 XP/day** → 15 target days (50% of month) — Mid-tier
- **100 XP/day** → 18 target days (60% of month) — Advanced
- **200 XP/day** → 20 target days (67% of month) — Expert

These targets are achievable. A 15-minute mission earns 15–25 XP. That means even one short mission per day is enough for lower tiers — and two missions puts you comfortably in the mid-tiers.

## The Psychology of Monthly Challenges

### Why 30 Days Is the Magic Number

Behavioral research shows that 21–30 days is the sweet spot for habit formation. Shorter (7 days) doesn't create enough identity change. Longer (90 days) creates overwhelm and decision fatigue. A 30-day challenge:

- **Is short enough** to commit to without overthinking
- **Is long enough** to create a genuine habit shift
- **Has a clear endpoint** — which creates urgency and focus
- **Allows for a fresh start** — monthly resets prevent "I already failed" spirals

### Milestone Motivation

The ⅓ / ⅔ / full milestone structure leverages the **goal gradient effect** — people accelerate effort as they approach a target. When you're at Day 12 and realize you're ⅓ of the way there, motivation spikes. The next milestone feels achievable, so you push for it. Each milestone crossed makes the next one feel closer.

### Celebration as Reinforcement

Teyro's Celebration Engine runs visual celebrations at every milestone — and after every daily quest completion. These aren't empty animations; they're **reinforcement events** tied to real progress. The brain associates "completing a mission" with "feeling celebrated," which creates a positive feedback loop that makes showing up enjoyable, not obligatory.

### Step 4: Monthly Reset

At month's end, your quest results are finalized:

- **Completed** — Hit all milestones, earned all rewards
- **Partially completed** — Hit some milestones, keep earned rewards
- **Available to claim** — Past months stay claimable forever (no expiry)
