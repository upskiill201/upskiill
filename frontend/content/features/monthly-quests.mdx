## How does the Monthly Challenge work?

Each month has a target number of learning days. A day counts when you finish at least one lesson. Your progress bar fills as the days add up, and it shows up in the after-lesson screens so you always know how close you are.

## What are the milestones?

The bar has checkpoints. Pass one and a chest opens right on the bar — coins, hearts, freezes or boosts, like [daily chests](/features/daily-chests). Finish the month and you earn its **badge**, which is different every month and stays on your profile.

## How is this different from a streak?

| | Streak | Monthly Challenge |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Measures** | Days in a row | Days in the month |
| **Missed day** | Needs a freeze or repair | Just catch up later in the month |
| **Reward** | Streak chests, Streak Society | Milestone chests and a month badge |

The two work together: the [streak](/features/streaks-and-freeze) keeps today honest, and the challenge keeps the month on track even after a slip.

## Who is it for?

Anyone who wants a bigger goal than "one lesson today". It pairs well with a [30-day skill challenge](/blog/30-day-skill-challenge-app).
