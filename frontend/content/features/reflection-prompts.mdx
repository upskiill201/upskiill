## The Illusion of Completion

### Finishing ≠ Learning

The most common learning trap is confusing "I finished the lesson" with "I learned the material." Teyro marks a mission as complete only after you've passed the reflection check — not just when you've clicked through all the pages. This distinction matters because completion without reflection is the leading cause of "I studied this but can't remember it."

### Why Your Brain Forgets

Your brain is a filtering system, not a recording device. It decides what to keep based on signals you send. If you just consume information without pausing to process it, your brain treats it as ephemeral — something to discard. Reflection is the signal that tells your brain: "This matters, keep it."

### The Reflection Gap

Most platforms either don't have reflection at all, or they offer a generic "Congratulations!" screen. Teyro's reflection prompts are specific to exactly what you just learned. They're not generic — they're targeted questions about the specific concepts in your current mission.

## The Science of Reflection

### Retrieval Practice

Reflection is retrieval practice — you're pulling information out of your memory rather than pushing it in. Retrieval is the single most powerful learning technique because it strengthens the neural pathways that store the information. Every time you retrieve a memory, the memory becomes stronger and more accessible.

### Elaborative Interrogation

When you answer "Why does this work?" or "How does this connect to what I already know?", you're engaging in elaborative interrogation — connecting new information to existing knowledge networks. This creates richer, more durable memory traces than isolated facts.

### Metacognition

Reflection prompts also build metacognition — your ability to think about your own thinking. "Where did I struggle?" is a metacognitive question that helps you identify blind spots. Learners with strong metacognitive skills learn faster and more effectively over time. Teyro's reflection prompts build this skill naturally.
