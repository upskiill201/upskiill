## What is the reflection step?

Every Teyro lesson has four steps: **Learn, Apply, Reflect, Deepen**. Reflect is the short pause after practice where you put the idea in your own words — what it is, when you would use it, what tripped you up.

## Why bother reflecting?

Because recognising an idea and understanding it feel the same from the inside. Clicking the right answer can come from pattern-matching; explaining *why* it is right cannot. Reflection is a quick check that catches the difference while it is cheap to fix.

It also helps later. Ideas you have explained once are easier to pull back up than ideas you only saw.

## How long does it take?

Under a minute in most lessons. It is designed to be short enough that you do it every time, not an essay you skip.

## What comes after?

An optional **Deepen** step for when you want more on the topic. Then the lesson's rewards: XP, your [streak](/features/streaks-and-freeze), and progress on your [daily quests](/features/daily-chests).
