## How do I get Teyro on my phone?

Go to [teyro.app/start](/start) on your phone and press the button. On Android, your browser installs Teyro in one tap. On iPhone, Teyro shows you the Add to Home Screen step. Either way you end up with the Teyro icon next to your other apps.

## Why not an app store app?

Installing from the browser is faster — no store page, no account, no large download — and updates arrive instantly, without waiting for a store review. You still get what matters from an app:

| | Teyro on your Home Screen |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Icon on your Home Screen** | Yes |
| **Opens full screen, instantly** | Yes |
| **Works offline** | Yes, once installed |
| **Streak reminders** | Yes, if you allow notifications |
| **Same progress on laptop** | Yes, one account everywhere |

## How do reminders work?

Teyro asks about reminders during setup and at most twice more, after a finished lesson — never on every visit. If you say yes, it nudges you on days you haven't practised yet, before the streak runs out.

## Where does phone learning fit?

Anywhere you would otherwise scroll: a commute, a queue, a lunch break. See [learning a skill during your commute](/blog/learn-a-skill-during-your-commute) for ideas.
