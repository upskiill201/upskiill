## The Mobile Learning Advantage

### Micro-Moments Are Everywhere

The average smartphone user checks their phone 96 times per day. Each of those checks is a potential learning moment. Teyro's mobile app turns micro-moments into micro-lessons — 15 minutes of focused learning during any spare pocket of time.

### One-Handed Design

Mobile learning only works if the app is actually usable on a phone. Teyro's mobile interface is designed for one-handed use: large touch targets, swipe-based navigation, and minimal scrolling. You can complete a mission with one hand while holding coffee with the other.

### Push Notifications That Work

Most app notifications are ignored. Teyro's smart notifications are timed to your most active learning windows and include specific calls to action ("Your streak is at 12 days — mission ready"). This creates a nudge that's hard to ignore without feeling spammy.

## Offline Learning: No Internet Required

### Download Missions for Offline Use

You can download your next few missions over Wi-Fi and complete them anywhere — flights, subways, rural areas, or places with poor connectivity. Offline missions sync automatically when you reconnect.

### Progress Syncs When Connected

Your streak, XP, and progress are always preserved. If you complete missions offline, your results sync the moment you reconnect. No progress is ever lost — offline or online, your streak continues.

### Cross-Device Continuity

Start a mission on your phone during your commute, finish it on desktop at your desk. Teyro syncs everything across devices. Your streak and progress are device-independent — they follow you, not your device.
