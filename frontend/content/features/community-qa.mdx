## What is a course community?

A space attached to each course where learners post questions, share what they built and cheer each other on. The course creator runs it, so answers often come from the person who wrote the lesson.

## Why only after two lessons?

Because the best communities are full of people doing the work. Two lessons is a small bar, but it means everyone inside has started — and it is the moment the community welcomes you in.

## What can I do there?

- ask a question when a lesson doesn't click
- share a win or something you built
- answer someone else's question (explaining is great practice)
- see classmates' profiles and progress

## How do friends work?

Follow people to see their streaks and profiles, and you will meet them on the [weekly league](/features/leagues-leaderboards). A friend's streak is a surprisingly strong reason not to break yours.

More on why this matters: [learn a skill with friends](/blog/learn-a-skill-with-friends-app).
