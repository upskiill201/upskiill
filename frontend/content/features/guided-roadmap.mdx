## The Decision Fatigue Problem

### Choice Overload

Barry Schwartz's "paradox of choice" research shows that more options lead to less satisfaction and less action. When you open a learning platform with 500 courses, you're overwhelmed. When you see a clear "next step" — a single mission designed for where you are — you act.

Teyro reduces your options to exactly one: the next mission on your roadmap. Not the next possible mission. The RIGHT next mission for your specific situation.

### The "What Now?" Problem

Completing a lesson without knowing what comes next creates a gap. This gap fills with doubt ("Am I doing the right thing? Should I try something different? Am I behind?"). Guided roadmaps close this gap instantly — you always know the next step, so doubt never gets room to grow.

### Sequential vs. Random Learning

Learning is cumulative. Each concept builds on the previous one. A roadmap ensures you're building in the right order — not jumping randomly between topics. Teyro's roadmap is designed by experts to ensure prerequisite knowledge is built before advanced concepts are introduced.
