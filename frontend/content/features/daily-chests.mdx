## What are daily quests?

Three small goals that change every day, one at each difficulty. They are built from things you do anyway — finish lessons, get answers right, hit an accuracy target, spend a few minutes learning — and they never repeat the same kind of goal twice in one day.

Finishing a quest opens a chest right there, no extra screens.

## How many chests can I get?

| Chest | How you get it |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Quest chests** | One for each of the three daily quests |
| **Daily chest** | Unlocked by your first lesson of the day |
| **Streak chests** | At 3, 7, 14, 21 and 30 days, then every 30 days |

A red dot on the Quests tab tells you when one is ready.

## What can a chest give me?

The reward is drawn when you open it:

- **coins** to spend in the shop
- **hearts**
- a **streak freeze**
- an **XP boost**

If you already have full hearts or a full freeze bank, the chest gives coins instead, so nothing is wasted.

## Is this just a slot machine?

Chests are a bonus on top of learning, not the point of it. You earn them by doing lessons, they never cost money, and the biggest rewards — your [streak](/features/streaks-and-freeze), your [league](/features/leagues-leaderboards), your skills — come from showing up.
