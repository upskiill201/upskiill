## The Magic of Daily Rituals

### Anticipation Is the Reward

Behavioral research shows that the anticipation of a reward can be more pleasurable than the reward itself. The dopamine release happens during the anticipation phase — not just when you receive the reward. Daily chests create a daily ritual of anticipation: "What will I get today?"

This is the same mechanism that makes morning coffee pleasurable — it's not just the caffeine, it's the ritual of making it and anticipating the first sip. Teyro's daily chest is the learning equivalent: the ritual of opening and the surprise inside.

### Variable Rewards Create Obsession

Slot machines work because of variable ratio reinforcement — the reward is unpredictable but regular. Sometimes you get common loot, sometimes you get rare items. This unpredictability keeps you coming back. Daily chests use the same principle: every session is a chance at something special.

Teyro's chest system is calibrated so most days give you useful but common rewards (XP boost, small coin bonus), occasionally you get premium items (exclusive themes, bonus streak protection), and rarely you get legendary rewards (mega XP bonus, rare cosmetic items). This distribution mirrors game economies and keeps engagement high.

### Daily Rituals Beat Willpower

Habits tied to rituals outperform habits tied to willpower. "Open the chest" is a ritual. "Study hard" is willpower. When learning is framed as a daily ritual with a celebratory moment, it becomes automatic. You don't need motivation to open your chest — and you don't need motivation to learn what's inside.
