The first offer is rarely the final one.

## Direct answer

To make a counteroffer, thank them, restate your interest, propose specific terms with a reason and invite discussion. Be polite, clear and ready to discuss trade-offs.

## Script

"Thank you for the offer. I am excited about the role. Based on my experience and market research, I was hoping for a salary of X. Is there flexibility?"

## Tips

1. Research a fair range.
2. Counter with a number slightly above your target.
3. Give a reason.
4. Be willing to discuss other terms.
5. Get the final agreement in writing.
