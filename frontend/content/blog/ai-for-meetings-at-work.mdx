AI can reduce the admin around a meeting, not replace the thinking.

## Direct answer

Use AI before a meeting to draft an agenda and brief, during it to transcribe (with consent), and after it to summarise decisions, list action items and draft follow-up emails. Always check the output and follow your organisation's rules on recording and data.

## Before

"Draft a 30-minute agenda for a project check-in covering progress, risks and decisions needed. Keep it to four items."

## During

Use an approved transcription tool and tell participants.

## After

"From this transcript, list decisions, action items with owners and deadlines, and write a short follow-up email."

## Checklist

1. Consent and policy.
2. Accurate names and numbers.
3. Send within a day.
4. Track actions in your task tool.
