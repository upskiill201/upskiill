A form needs two parts: the page people see and something that receives the data.

## Direct answer

An HTML form collects the information, but you need a backend or a form service to receive it. Static sites can use a form service or the hosting platform's form handling. Add labels, validation and spam protection.

## Basic HTML

```html
<form action="https://your-form-service.example/submit" method="POST">
  <label for="name">Name</label>
  <input id="name" name="name" required>

  <label for="email">Email</label>
  <input id="email" name="email" type="email" required>

  <label for="message">Message</label>
  <textarea id="message" name="message" required></textarea>

  <button type="submit">Send</button>
</form>
```

## Options for receiving it

- A form service that emails you submissions.
- Your host's built-in forms.
- Your own server endpoint.

## Good practice

1. Label every field.
2. Explain what you do with the data.
3. Add spam protection.
4. Show a clear success message.
5. Do not log sensitive data.
