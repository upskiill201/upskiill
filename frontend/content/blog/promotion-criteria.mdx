Do not guess what the next level needs. Ask.

## Direct answer

To get promoted, find out the criteria for the next level, compare your current skills and results, plan to close gaps and gather evidence of impact. Agree on goals and timelines with your manager and review progress regularly.

## Steps

1. Ask: "What would I need to show to be considered for the next level?"
2. Get written criteria if possible.
3. Compare with your achievements.
4. Take stretch projects.
5. Document results.
6. Review every quarter.
