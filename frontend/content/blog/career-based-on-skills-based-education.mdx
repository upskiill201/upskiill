The global workforce is undergoing a seismic shift. For decades, a 4-year university degree was considered the only ticket to white-collar employment. Today, soaring tuition costs, rapid technological turnover, and the rise of generative AI have made a **career based on skills-based education** the fastest, most cost-effective path to high-income employment. With gamified micro-learning platforms like [Teyro](/onboarding/0), you can master the exact technical and business skills top employers demand in just 15 minutes of daily practice.

Companies like Google, Microsoft, Apple, and Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) have dismantled formal degree requirements in favor of **skills-first hiring**—evaluating candidates on what they can actually build, solve, and execute.
