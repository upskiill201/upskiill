A new purchase can lift your mood for a moment. The question is what happens next.

## Direct answer

Retail therapy means shopping to feel better. A small, planned treat can be fine. It becomes a problem when you shop regularly to escape stress, spend money you do not have or feel guilty afterwards. Healthier alternatives address the feeling directly.

## Signs it is becoming a problem

- You shop when stressed, sad or bored.
- You hide purchases or feel guilty.
- You spend beyond your budget.
- You buy things you never use.

## What to try instead

1. **Name the feeling** before opening a shopping app.
2. **Change your state:** walk, stretch, shower, or call someone.
3. **Create a treat budget** for planned fun spending.
4. **Use a wish list** and review it after a week.
5. **Seek support** if shopping feels out of control.

## A kinder approach

Occasional treats are part of life. The goal is choice, not perfection.
