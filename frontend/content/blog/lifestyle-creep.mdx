Many people earn more every few years and feel no richer. Lifestyle creep is usually why.

## Direct answer

Lifestyle creep is when your spending slowly rises with your income, so you're no better off despite earning more. It's easy to miss because each upgrade feels small and reasonable. The fix is to decide in advance what happens to a raise, automate savings and treat upgrades as choices.

## Examples

- Moving to a pricier flat after a raise
- Upgrading the car or phone every time
- Takeaways becoming weekly, then daily
- Subscriptions piling up
- "Treats" that become routine

## Why it's hard to notice

Each step is small. Friends and colleagues spend similarly. And "I deserve it" feels true.

## How to avoid it

1. **Split every raise.** For example, save half, enjoy half.
2. **Automate savings first.** See [how to stop overspending](/blog/how-to-stop-overspending).
3. **Upgrade slowly.** Wait a few months before raising your standard.
4. **Spend on what you value,** not what's expected. See [needs vs wants](/blog/needs-vs-wants-examples).
5. **Review annually.** What did I add this year? Was it worth it?

## A quick check

If your income rose 20% in three years, did your savings rate rise too?
