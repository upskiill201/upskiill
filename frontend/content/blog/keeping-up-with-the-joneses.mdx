You see their holiday. You do not see their credit card statement.

## Direct answer

"Keeping up with the Joneses" means spending to match what other people have. Comparison pushes you to buy things you may not value, often on credit. Break the cycle by defining your own goals, limiting accounts that trigger envy and focusing on your own progress.

## Why it is expensive

- Status spending rarely gives lasting satisfaction.
- You compare your inside with others' highlights.
- Debt can hide the true cost of someone's lifestyle.

## What to do

1. **Write your own goals** so you know what you are aiming for.
2. **Unfollow or mute** accounts that make you want things.
3. **Track net worth,** not purchases.
4. **Celebrate your progress.**
5. **Find friends** who value similar things.
