A bot in the meeting changes the room. Handle it well.

## Direct answer

Use an approved AI note taker, tell participants at the start, turn it off for sensitive topics and verify the summary before sharing. Be aware of where recordings and transcripts are stored and who can access them.

## Etiquette

1. Announce it at the start.
2. Allow people to opt out where possible.
3. Do not record confidential items.
4. Share the notes with attendees.
5. Correct errors.

## Setup checklist

- Approved by your IT or security team.
- Data storage and retention understood.
- Access limited to attendees.
- Language and accent accuracy tested.
