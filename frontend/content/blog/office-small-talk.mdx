Small talk is how work relationships start.

## Direct answer

At work, keep small talk light and positive: weekends, food, hobbies, projects and local events. Join groups by listening first and adding a relevant comment. Avoid gossip, complaints about colleagues and sensitive subjects.

## Safe topics

- Weekend or holiday plans.
- Food and coffee spots.
- Shows, books and podcasts.
- Hobbies and sport.
- Industry news.

## Tips

1. Ask open questions.
2. Remember small details and follow up.
3. Keep it brief if people are busy.
4. Include remote colleagues.
