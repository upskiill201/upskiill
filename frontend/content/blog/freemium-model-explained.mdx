Free users are a cost and a marketing channel.

## Direct answer

Freemium gives users a free version and charges for upgrades. It works when the free tier shows value, the paid tier solves clear extra needs and costs per free user are low. Design the upgrade path carefully and measure conversion.

## Design questions

1. What makes users successful for free?
2. What do power users need that is worth paying for?
3. What is the cost of a free user?
4. How will free users discover the upgrade?

## Risks

- Too much free value.
- Too little to hook users.
- Support cost for free users.
