A number that tells you whether things are going well.

## Direct answer

KPI stands for key performance indicator. It is a measurable value that shows how well you are doing against an important goal, such as monthly sales or customer satisfaction. Pick a few and track them regularly.

## Examples

| Area | KPI |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sales | monthly revenue |
| Support | average response time |
| Marketing | conversion rate |
