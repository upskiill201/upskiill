### Module 1: High-Speed Ingestion & Keyboard Trimming
Mastering keyboard shortcuts (`Cmd/Ctrl + B` razor, `Q/W` ripple trim) allows you to assemble rough cuts 3x faster than mouse-dependent editors.

### Module 2: Broadcast Audio Engineering
Dialogue must peak cleanly between `-6 dB` and `-12 dB`, with background music ducked to `-24 dB` and atmospheric noise filtered below 100Hz. Master studio-grade dialogue in our [how to do a voiceover for video masterclass](/blog/how-to-do-a-voiceover-for-video).

### Module 3: Color Grading & Scopes
Learn to read Waveforms, Vectorscopes, and Parade graphs to balance exposure and skin tone lines accurately across different camera sensors.
