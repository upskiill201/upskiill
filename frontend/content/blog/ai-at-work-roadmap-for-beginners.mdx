You don't need to become an AI expert. You need a handful of habits that make everyday work faster and better.

## Direct answer

A good beginner roadmap for AI at work is: understand what AI can and can't do, learn to write clear prompts, apply them to two or three tasks you do every week, check the results carefully, protect confidential data, then share what works. Practice on real tasks beats reading about tools.

## The roadmap

### 1. Understand the basics
AI chatbots predict useful text. They are fast and fluent but can be wrong. See [how AI works in simple terms](/blog/how-does-ai-work-in-simple-terms).

### 2. Learn to prompt
State the role, task, context and format. Add an example. See [ChatGPT prompts for beginners](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-beginners).

### 3. Pick three real tasks
Examples: summarise meeting notes, draft a reply, create a weekly plan.

### 4. Check everything
Facts, numbers, tone. You own the result.

### 5. Stay safe
Know your employer's rules. See [ChatGPT at work](/blog/chatgpt-at-work).

### 6. Build workflows
Save prompts that work, and reuse them.

### 7. Share and improve
Show a colleague what worked. Keep learning one new use per month.

## Free ways to start

- Use an approved AI tool for one task this week
- Follow a short free course
- Keep a notes file of prompts that work

## Mistakes to avoid

- Trying a tool once and giving up on a vague answer
- Trusting output without checking
- Pasting private data
