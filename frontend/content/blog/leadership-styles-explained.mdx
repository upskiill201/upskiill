Good leaders flex their style.

## Direct answer

Common leadership styles include authoritative (sets direction), democratic (seeks input), coaching (develops people), pacesetting (leads by high standards), affiliative (builds harmony) and servant (puts the team first). The best leaders choose the style to fit the situation and the people.

## Quick guide

| Style | Works well when | Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Authoritative | a clear vision is needed | can feel top-down |
| Democratic | you need buy-in | slow decisions |
| Coaching | developing people | takes time |
| Pacesetting | skilled, motivated team | burnout |
| Affiliative | repairing trust | avoids hard feedback |
| Servant | building long-term teams | may be slow in a crisis |

## How to adapt

1. Read the situation: urgency, skill and trust.
2. Ask what the team needs.
3. Change style when circumstances change.
