A good opening sets the range and the tone.

## Direct answer

Make an opening offer after researching a fair range. Choose a number that is ambitious but defensible, explain why and invite discussion. Leave room to concede and be ready to justify it.

## Script

"Based on comparable offers and my experience, I am looking at X. I would love to hear your thoughts."

## Tips

1. Research before you speak.
2. Round numbers can signal flexibility. Precise numbers can signal research.
3. Never apologise for the number.
4. Stay calm if they push back.
