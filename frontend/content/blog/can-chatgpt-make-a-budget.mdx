AI can write a first draft of a budget in seconds. It cannot know your life, and it can get the maths wrong. Here is **how to use ChatGPT or any AI assistant to make a budget**, safely, with a prompt you can copy.

## Direct answer: can ChatGPT make a budget?

Yes. Give it your take-home income, your fixed costs, your goals and any rules, and ask for a table where income minus spending equals zero. Then check every figure yourself and adjust to your real spending. Do not share account numbers, passwords or personal identifiers.

## What to give it

- Monthly **take-home** income
- Fixed costs: rent, utilities, phone, insurance, minimum debt payments
- Rough everyday costs: groceries, transport
- Goals: an emergency buffer, a trip, paying off debt
- Any rule: for example, a split like the [50/30/20 rule](/blog/50-30-20-rule-budget)

## A copy-paste prompt

> I earn $2,400 a month after tax. My rent is $800, utilities $150, phone $50, transport $150 and I pay a $100 minimum on a loan. I spend about $350 a month on groceries. I want to save $300 a month and keep some fun money. Build me a monthly budget as a table, make the total exactly equal my income, and tell me which categories I could trim if I run short. Show your arithmetic.

Asking it to show its arithmetic helps you check the totals.

## What to check

- Does **income minus total planned spending equal zero**?
- Are the numbers yours or did it invent costs you do not have?
- Is each fixed cost correct?
- Is there a buffer, or is every unit assigned with no room for surprises?

## What never to share

Account or card numbers, passwords, your full legal name with your address, and any identifier a bank would ask for. Use round numbers and categories instead.

## What AI is good for

- Turning your numbers into a draft table
- Comparing methods (zero-based vs 50/30/20)
- Suggesting categories you forgot
- Explaining a term or role-playing a hard conversation about money

## What AI cannot do

- Track your real spending
- Know your local prices or taxes
- Replace the weekly habit of checking your budget

## A workflow that works

1. Track a week of real spending (see [how to budget for beginners](/blog/how-to-budget-for-beginners)).
2. Ask AI for a draft and paste it into a [monthly budget template](/blog/monthly-budget-template).
3. Fix the numbers that are wrong.
4. Review weekly.
