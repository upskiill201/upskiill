Introverts often excel at the part of conversation that matters: listening.

## Direct answer

Introverts can handle small talk by preparing a few questions, arriving early when it is quieter, aiming for one or two good conversations rather than many, focusing on listening and allowing time to recharge afterwards.

## Strategies

1. **Prepare three questions.**
2. **Arrive early.**
3. **Aim for one-to-one chats.**
4. **Use your listening skill.**
5. **Set a goal** such as meeting two people.
6. **Take breaks.**
7. **Leave when you are ready.**

## Reframe

Small talk is the doorway to deeper conversation, not the point itself.
