Advice helps. Advocacy opens doors.

## Direct answer

A mentor talks with you and gives advice. A sponsor speaks about you in rooms where decisions are made and recommends you for opportunities. Both help your career. Earn a sponsor by delivering excellent work, sharing your ambitions and building trust with senior leaders.

## Comparison

| | Mentor | Sponsor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Role | advise | advocate |
| Seniority | any experienced person | usually senior |
| Gives | guidance | opportunities and visibility |

## How to earn a sponsor

1. Deliver consistently.
2. Make your goals clear.
3. Be reliable on stretch tasks.
4. Say thanks and show results.
