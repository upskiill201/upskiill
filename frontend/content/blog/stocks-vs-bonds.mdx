They are two of the main building blocks of investing.

## Direct answer

A stock represents part ownership in a company, with returns coming from price growth and dividends. A bond is a loan you make to a government or company, which pays interest and returns the original amount at maturity. Stocks generally offer higher potential returns with more volatility. Bonds are usually steadier but not risk-free.

## Comparison

| | Stocks | Bonds |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| What you get | Ownership | Loan repayment plus interest |
| Typical return source | Price growth, dividends | Interest |
| Typical volatility | Higher | Lower |
| Main risks | Market and company risk | Credit, interest rate and inflation risk |

## How they work together

When stocks fall, bonds sometimes hold up better, which can smooth results, though this is not guaranteed.
