Prices move. Knowing that in advance makes it easier to cope.

## Direct answer

Volatility describes how widely and quickly an investment's price changes. High volatility means large swings in both directions. It is a normal part of markets. You can manage it by investing for the long term, diversifying and not holding money you need soon in volatile assets.

## What causes it

- News about the economy or companies.
- Interest rate changes.
- Investor mood and uncertainty.

## How to cope

1. **Match investments to your timeline.**
2. **Diversify.**
3. **Avoid checking prices constantly.**
4. **Stick to your plan** unless your circumstances change.
5. **Keep an emergency fund** so you do not sell in a downturn.
