## 2. Touch Typing at 100+ Words Per Minute

Watching someone type without looking at the keyboard at blazing speeds looks like magic. More importantly, doubling your typing speed saves you over 30 hours of computer time every year.

* **Best Practice Sites:** Monkeytype, Keybr, and TypeRacer.
* **Daily Goal:** 10 minutes of disciplined blind touch typing every morning.

## 3. The Memory Palace Technique (Method of Loci)

Imagine being able to memorize a list of 50 items, a 20-minute speech, or an entire deck of cards in minutes. Ancient Roman orators used the **Memory Palace technique**, anchoring concepts to familiar mental locations in their home.
