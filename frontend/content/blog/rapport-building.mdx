Connection makes communication easier.

## Direct answer

Build rapport by being warm, using people's names, asking open questions, finding common ground and listening with real curiosity. Remember details and follow up on them next time.

## Habits

1. Smile and be present.
2. Use their name.
3. Ask open questions.
4. Find shared interests.
5. Listen and respond to what they said.
6. Remember details.
