Second-hand shopping can be fun and affordable, if you know what to look for.

## Direct answer

To thrift well, make a list, check items for damage, try clothes on and focus on quality fabrics and brands you know. Buy items that last, such as furniture, books and kitchenware. Avoid safety-critical items like helmets and car seats. Visit often because stock changes.

## Before you go

- Write a list so you do not leave with bargains you do not need.
- Set a budget.
- Wear clothes that are easy to try on.

## What to check

- **Clothes:** seams, zips, stains, holes and odours.
- **Furniture:** wobble, damage, pests.
- **Electrical items:** ask to test them.
- **Books and games:** missing pages or pieces.

## Good second-hand buys

Coats, jeans, kitchen tools, shelves, books, bikes and children's clothes that will be outgrown fast.

## Make the most of it

Learn basic repairs such as sewing a button, or swap handles on furniture. Resell items you no longer wear.
