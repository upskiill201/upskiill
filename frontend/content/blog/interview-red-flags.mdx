You are interviewing them too.

## Direct answer

Common red flags are vague role descriptions, interviewers who are late or disrespectful, high turnover, unrealistic expectations and pressure to decide quickly. Ask direct questions, look for patterns and trust your judgement.

## Signs

- The role changes as you talk.
- Nobody can say how success is measured.
- Negative talk about former employees.
- Pay or terms are unclear.
- Pressure to accept immediately.

## Questions to ask

- Why is this role open?
- How long have people in this team stayed?
- What would a typical week look like?
