One of the most mocked words in business, with a real idea underneath.

## Direct answer

Synergy means that combined effort produces more than the parts alone, as in "1 + 1 = 3". In business it is used for mergers and teamwork. Because it is often vague, replace it with the specific benefit: what improves, by how much.

## Examples

- "The merger creates synergy" often means savings from shared costs.
- "Our teams have good synergy" means they work well together.

## Plain alternatives

| Instead of | Say |
| :--- | :--- |
| We need more synergy | We should work together on X to save time |
| Synergies from the merger | Savings and shared strengths we expect |
