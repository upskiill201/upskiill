Your habits are your loudest message.

## Direct answer

Leading by example means modelling the behaviour and standards you want from others. Be reliable, respectful, prepared and honest about mistakes. People copy what they see, so consistent actions build trust and influence.

## 10 behaviours

1. Keep your promises.
2. Arrive prepared.
3. Be on time.
4. Listen without interrupting.
5. Give credit to others.
6. Admit mistakes quickly.
7. Ask for feedback.
8. Treat everyone respectfully.
9. Stay calm under pressure.
10. Keep learning.

## A test

Would you be happy for your team to behave exactly as you do?
