Money back on purchases sounds like a free win. It is only a win if the purchase was planned.

## Direct answer

Cashback apps return a small percentage of what you spend at partner retailers. They work when you use them on things you were already going to buy. They can backfire if the offers tempt you to spend more than you intended or to buy things you did not need.

## How they work

Retailers pay the app a commission for sending you a sale. The app passes a share back to you, usually as credit you can withdraw once you reach a threshold.

## Using them wisely

- Only use offers on purchases you planned.
- Ignore "limited time" pressure.
- Check how long cashback takes to be approved.
- Read the privacy policy to see what data is collected and shared.
- Track your total spend, not just the cashback earned.

## The real test

Ask yourself: "Would I buy this at full price?" If not, a 5 percent return does not make it a saving.
