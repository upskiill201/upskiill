## Unit 1: Communication Skills (Core Breakdown)

* **The 7 Cs of Communication:** Clear, Concise, Concrete, Correct, Coherent, Complete, and Courteous.
* **Barriers to Communication:** Physical barriers (noise, distance), linguistic barriers (jargon), cultural barriers, and emotional barriers.
* **Nonverbal Communication:** Facial expressions, gestures, posture (kinesics), and personal space (proxemics).

---

## Unit 2: Self-Management & Stress Reduction

Self-management is the ability to regulate your emotions, thoughts, and behaviors effectively across different situations:

1. **SMART Goals:** Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.
2. **Stress Management Techniques:** Deep breathing, regular physical exercise, prioritizing high-impact tasks, and getting 8 hours of sleep.

---

## Unit 3: Information & Communication Technology (ICT)

* **Operating System (OS):** The software that manages computer hardware and software resources (e.g. Windows, Linux, Android).
* **Keyboard Shortcuts:** `Ctrl+C` (Copy), `Ctrl+V` (Paste), `Ctrl+Z` (Undo), `Ctrl+S` (Save). Learn more in [how to learn basic computer skills](/blog/how-to-learn-basic-computer-skills).
* **Cyber Hygiene:** Using strong alphanumeric passwords and running anti-virus scans.

---

## Unit 4 & Unit 5: Entrepreneurship and Green Skills

* **Entrepreneurship:** An innovator who takes calculated financial risks to launch a business solving customer pain points (see [entrepreneurial skills guide](/blog/entrepreneurial-skills-guide)).
* **Green Economy:** An economy that reduces environmental risks and ecological scarcities while promoting social equity.
