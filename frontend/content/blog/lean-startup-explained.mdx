## Direct answer

Lean startup is a method of running small experiments to learn quickly. You build a minimum version, measure how customers respond and learn whether to persevere or pivot. It reduces waste by testing assumptions early.

## The loop

1. **Build** the smallest test.
2. **Measure** what people do.
3. **Learn** and decide.

## Example

Instead of building an app, create a landing page and see how many people sign up for the waiting list.

## Key ideas

- Validated learning.
- Minimum viable product.
- Pivot or persevere.
