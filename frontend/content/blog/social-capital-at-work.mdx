Who you can count on, and who can count on you.

## Direct answer

Social capital is the goodwill and trust you build through relationships. You build it by helping others, keeping promises, sharing information and showing real interest. You spend it when you ask for support. Keep the balance healthy.

## Ways to build it

1. **Help first,** without keeping score.
2. **Be reliable.**
3. **Share useful information.**
4. **Learn about colleagues' goals.**
5. **Keep in touch** outside of urgent requests.
6. **Thank people** specifically.

## Spend it wisely

Make clear, small requests and explain why. Return the favour.
