Not every job opening begins with an advert.

## Direct answer

To tap the hidden job market, tell your network what you are looking for, ask for introductions, follow companies you like, contact hiring managers with a short, specific message and attend events. Combine this with applying to advertised roles.

## Steps

1. List 20 target companies.
2. Follow them and their teams.
3. Reach out to people with a specific, short ask.
4. Share what you can offer.
5. Follow up politely.

## Message example

"Hi [name], I admire your team's work on X. I am a [role] with experience in Y. Are you hiring or could you suggest who I should speak to?"
