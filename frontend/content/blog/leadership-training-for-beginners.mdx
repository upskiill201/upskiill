Leadership training ranges from a free ten-minute video to a course costing thousands. Here is how to choose as a beginner, and what to expect.

## Direct answer

Choose leadership training that teaches practical situations (one-on-ones, feedback, delegation, hard conversations), has you practise, and fits your schedule. Start with a free or low-cost option, check what the free version really includes, and do not pay for a certificate unless you need one. Practise at work between lessons.

## What a good beginner course covers

1. **Leading one to one:** regular conversations that build trust.
2. **Goals and expectations:** making clear what good looks like.
3. **Delegation:** handing over outcomes with the right support.
4. **Feedback and praise:** specific, timely, kind.
5. **Hard conversations:** raising problems early and calmly.
6. **Leading under pressure:** staying steady when things go wrong.

## Free vs paid

| | Free | Paid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Good for | Trying a topic, building the habit | Structured depth, feedback, recognised names |
| Watch out for | Free first module only, ads, no practice | Price, long time commitment, passive videos |
| Check | What is included after the free part | Refund policy, cancellation, what you get |

Many providers have a free tier or a free first chapter. Terms change, so check them yourself.

## Do certificates matter?

Some employers like seeing a completed course, but most care about evidence: have you led anything? Use training to build skills, then show results. ## Ways to learn leadership

- **Short online courses:** best for fitting around work.
- **Your employer:** many offer first-time manager programmes. Ask.
- **Books and videos:** good for ideas, weaker for practice.
- **A mentor:** someone who leads well and will talk through real situations.
- **Practice:** lead a small project, volunteer for a team, organise something.

## How to choose

1. Does it cover practical situations or only theory?
2. Can you practise or reflect after each lesson?
3. Is it short enough to finish?
4. What does the free version include?
5. Does it match your situation (new manager, team lead, no title)?

## Questions to ask before you pay

- Can I try before I buy?
- What happens when the trial ends?
- Is the certificate extra?
- Will I still have access afterwards?
