Initiative is noticing, then doing something sensible about it.

## Direct answer

Take initiative by noticing a problem or opportunity, proposing a small, useful action, checking with the people affected and following through. Keep your manager informed so your effort is seen and aligned.

## How to do it

1. **Notice:** what frustrates people or wastes time?
2. **Propose:** "I noticed X. Could I try Y?"
3. **Start small** and share results.
4. **Check scope** and permissions.
5. **Follow through** and report back.

## Examples

- Create a shared checklist for a repeated task.
- Offer to onboard the next new hire.
- Spot a recurring error and suggest a fix.
