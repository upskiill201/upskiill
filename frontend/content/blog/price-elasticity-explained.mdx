If you raise the price, how many customers do you lose?

## Direct answer

Price elasticity of demand is the percentage change in quantity demanded divided by the percentage change in price. Elastic goods see big changes in demand when price moves. Inelastic goods, such as essentials, change less. Test price changes carefully to learn.

## Example

If you raise the price by 10 percent and sales fall by 5 percent, elasticity is −0.5, so demand is inelastic and revenue likely rises.

## How to use it

1. Test small changes.
2. Watch sales, not just price.
3. Consider different customer groups.
