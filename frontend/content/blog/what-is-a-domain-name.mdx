A domain is the address, hosting is the house.

## Direct answer

A domain name is your website's address, like `example.com`. You register it through a domain registrar, usually for a yearly fee, and point it to your hosting. Choose a short, memorable name with a common extension such as `.com`, and avoid hyphens and tricky spellings.

## How to choose

1. **Keep it short** and easy to say.
2. **Avoid hyphens and numbers.**
3. **Check spelling** by saying it to a friend.
4. **Check the name** is not a trademark and the social handles are free.
5. **Choose the right extension.** `.com` is familiar. Country and new extensions can also work.

## How to register

1. Search for the name at a registrar.
2. Check the yearly price and renewal price.
3. Turn on privacy protection if offered.
4. Enable auto-renew so you do not lose it.
5. Point it to your host using DNS records.

## Domain vs hosting

| | Domain | Hosting |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| What it is | the address | where files live |
| Paid | yearly | monthly or yearly, or free tiers |
