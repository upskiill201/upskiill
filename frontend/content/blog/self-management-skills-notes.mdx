## 1. Stress Management: Causes, Symptoms & Remedies

Stress is the body's physical and mental reaction to challenging circumstances. When unmanaged, it leads to chronic burnout and cognitive fatigue:

* **Stress Management Techniques:**
  1. **Physical Exercise & Yoga:** Releases endorphins and lowers cortisol.
  2. **Meditation & Deep Breathing:** Activates the parasympathetic nervous system within 60 seconds.
  3. **Nature Walks & Hobbies:** Gives the brain's prefrontal cortex time to recharge.

## 2. Setting SMART Goals for Maximum Achievement

Vague goals like *"I want to study more"* always fail. Use the **SMART formula**:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 THE SMART GOAL ARCHITECTURE                 │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  S ──> SPECIFIC: "I will master SQL database joins."        │
│  M ──> MEASURABLE: "Solve 25 interactive SQL challenges."   │
│  A ──> ACHIEVABLE: "Dedicate 15 minutes every morning."     │
│  R ──> RELEVANT: "Needed for data analyst internship."      │
│  T ──> TIME-BOUND: "Complete all challenges within 14 days."│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. Time Management & The Eisenhower Matrix

Separate your daily tasks into four quadrants:

1. **Quadrant 1 (Urgent & Important):** Critical crises and immediate deadlines $\rightarrow$ **Do First**.
2. **Quadrant 2 (Not Urgent but Important):** Daily skill building on Teyro, exercise, strategic planning $\rightarrow$ **Schedule & Protect**.
3. **Quadrant 3 (Urgent but Not Important):** Interruptions and non-essential notifications $\rightarrow$ **Delegate/Automate**.
4. **Quadrant 4 (Not Urgent & Not Important):** Mindless social media doom-scrolling $\rightarrow$ **Eliminate**.
