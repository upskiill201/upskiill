Numbers are felt as well as calculated.

## Direct answer

Psychological pricing uses perception effects: charm prices ending in 9, round numbers for premium goods, price anchoring and bundles. Test them with real customers because effects vary by product and audience.

## Common tactics

| Tactic | Example |
| :--- | :--- |
| Charm pricing | 19.99 |
| Round pricing | 20 for premium items |
| Bundle pricing | three for the price of two |
| Anchoring | premium plan beside standard |

## Tips

1. Test, do not assume.
2. Be honest.
3. Keep it consistent with your brand.
