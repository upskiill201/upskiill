## 1. Evidence-Based Study & Memory Techniques

Stop highlighting textbooks and cramming the night before an exam. Elite students use cognitive science:

* **Active Recall:** Testing yourself with flashcards and practice exams (read [spaced repetition: how to study smarter](/blog/spaced-repetition-how-to-study-smarter)).
* **The Feynman Technique:** Explaining complex formulas in simple language (learn more in [how to improve our learning skill](/blog/how-to-improve-our-learning-skill)).
* **Pomodoro Focus Blocks:** 25 minutes of deep focus followed by a 5-minute break.
