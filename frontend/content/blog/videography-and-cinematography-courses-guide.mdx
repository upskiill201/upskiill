### Pillar 1: The Exposure Triangle & Shutter Angle
- **The 180-Degree Shutter Rule:** When shooting at 24fps for cinema, set your shutter speed to $1/50\text{s}$ (twice the frame rate) to produce natural human motion blur.
- **Aperture Depth of Field:** Using wide apertures ($f/1.8 - f/2.8$) creates shallow depth of field with creamy background bokeh, isolating subjects from distractions.
- **Native Dual ISO:** Understanding your sensor's base ISO levels eliminates shadow noise in low-light environments.

### Pillar 2: The 3-Point Lighting System
Lighting separates amateur home video from cinematic artistry:
1. **Key Light:** The primary light source positioned 45 degrees from the subject's face at eye level.
2. **Fill Light (or Negative Fill):** Soft bounce or dimmed light on the opposite side to control shadow contrast.
3. **Rim / Backlight (Hair Light):** Positioned behind the subject to create edge separation from the background.

## Videography vs Cinematography: Which Career Path is Right for You?

Understanding the commercial distinction helps you choose the right training path:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────┐
│         VIDEOGRAPHY PATH              │         CINEMATOGRAPHY PATH           │
├───────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Solo operator / Run-and-gun crews   │ • Large crew (Gaffer, Grip, ACs, DP)  │
│ • Weddings, events, corporate promos  │ • Narrative films, music videos, ads  │
│ • Natural light & portable LED wands  │ • Heavy diffusion, HMI & studio grids │
│ • Fast turnaround (1-2 week delivery) │ • Extended pre-production & color lab │
│ • Gear: Mirrorless + Gimbal + Lav Mic │ • Gear: Cinema rigs + Anamorphic Glass│
└───────────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

If your immediate goal is freelancing and earning commercial income fast, videography training offers a much shorter path to revenue. If your goal is feature films or high-end agency commercials, cinematography diplomas provide valuable set etiquette.
