You negotiate more than you think: pay, rent, deadlines, chores, prices. Learning the basics changes the outcomes.

## Direct answer

Negotiation is reaching an agreement that works for both sides. The core skills are preparing (knowing your goal, minimum and alternative), listening, asking good questions, communicating clearly, looking for trades that help both sides and staying calm. Beginners get better fastest by practising on small everyday asks.

## Skills and examples

| Skill | Example |
| :--- | :--- |
| Preparation | Researching typical rent before talking to a landlord |
| Listening | Asking a client what matters most, then summarising |
| Clear asking | "I'd like a 10% discount for paying upfront." |
| Creative trades | Longer contract for a lower price |
| Staying calm | Taking a pause when the other side pushes |
| Knowing your limit | Saying no when the deal doesn't work |

## 8 tips

1. **Prepare:** goal, minimum, alternative.
2. **Ask:** most people never do.
3. **Make the first offer** when you have good research.
4. **Ask questions** to learn what they need.
5. **Trade, don't just give in:** "If I do X, can you do Y?"
6. **Stay polite and firm.**
7. **Use silence.**
8. **Get it in writing.**

## A simple plan

1. What do I want, ideally?
2. What's the least I'd accept?
3. What happens if we don't agree?
4. What might they want?
5. What can I offer that costs me little?
6. What will I say first?
