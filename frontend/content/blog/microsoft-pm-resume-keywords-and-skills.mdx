## 1. The Core Microsoft PM Pillars You Must Demonstrate

### 1. Customer Obsession (The "Why")
Microsoft's culture under Satya Nadella places **Customer Obsession** above all else. Your resume must prove that you conduct qualitative user interviews, observe real customer friction, and translate empathy into product roadmaps.

### 2. Telemetry and Data-Driven Experimentation (The "How Much")
Microsoft PMs live in data. You need to show that you do not just launch features—you instrument telemetry, define success metrics (North Star metrics), and iterate based on statistical feedback loops.

* **Data Practice:** Sharpen your database querying with [SQL games for practice](/blog/sql-games).

### 3. Technical Credibility (The "How")
While you do not write production code daily, you must be able to debate architectural trade-offs with Principal Software Engineers. Familiarize yourself with Python and cloud concepts in [Duolingo for Python](/blog/duolingo-for-python).
