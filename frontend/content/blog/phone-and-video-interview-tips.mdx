More first rounds now happen on the phone or on camera. The skills are the same as in person, plus a little tech preparation.

## Direct answer

For phone and video interviews, choose a quiet place, test your tech in advance, have your CV, the job post and notes in front of you, speak clearly and slightly slower than normal, and show energy: smile, even on the phone. On video, look at the camera when you answer, light your face from the front and use a tidy background.

## Phone interviews

- Take the call somewhere quiet with good signal.
- Have a glass of water, your CV and the job post.
- Stand or sit upright; it improves your voice.
- Smile when you speak. People can hear it.
- Don't interrupt; pauses are fine.
- Ask about next steps at the end.

## Video interviews (Zoom, Teams, Meet)

1. **Test first:** camera, microphone, internet and the app link.
2. **Light:** face a window or lamp; avoid a bright light behind you.
3. **Background:** plain and tidy, or a simple blur.
4. **Camera at eye level:** stack books under a laptop if needed.
5. **Look at the camera** when answering, not at yourself.
6. **Close other apps** and silence notifications.
7. **Join a few minutes early.**

## What to wear

Dress as for an in-person interview, from head to toe. It helps your mindset.

## If something goes wrong

- Internet drops: rejoin, then message the contact.
- Noise: apologise briefly and move on.
- You lose your train of thought: pause, sip water and ask to repeat the question.
