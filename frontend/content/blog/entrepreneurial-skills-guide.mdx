## 1. Customer Discovery & The "Mom Test"

Most first-time founders make the mistake of asking: *"Do you think this is a cool idea?"* Friends and family will always say yes to be polite.

* **The Mom Test Rule:** Never ask people what they might do in the future. Ask about their past behavior:
  * *"When was the last time you struggled with [Problem]?"*
  * *"How much did you spend trying to fix it?"*
  * *"What did you hate about the current solution you tried?"*

---

## 2. Rapid Prototyping: Build Minimum Viable Products (MVPs)

Do not spend six months building a complex platform. Validate demand in 7 days:

1. **The Concierge MVP:** Manually perform the service behind the scenes before automating it with code.
2. **Landing Page Smoke Test:** Build a clean Figma/Next.js landing page with a waitlist button to gauge real click-through interest.
3. **No-Code Automation:** Use Zapier and Airtable to connect tools before building custom software (see [future digital skills for 2026 checklist](/blog/future-digital-skills-for-2026-checklist)).

## 3. Financial Runway & Cash Flow Management

A business fails when its bank account hits zero. Founders must master basic financial modeling:

* **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Total sales & marketing spend divided by new customers acquired.
* **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** Total gross profit a customer generates across their relationship.
* **The 3:1 Golden Ratio:** Your LTV must be at least 3x higher than your CAC for a venture to be scalable.
