Saving is delayed gratification in action.

## Direct answer

Delayed gratification means waiting for a bigger reward rather than taking a smaller one now. In money terms, it is saving for a goal instead of spending today. You can build it with clear goals, automation, rewards along the way and by making temptations harder to reach.

## Techniques

1. **Set a vivid goal** and put it where you see it.
2. **Automate saving** so it happens before you spend.
3. **Use waiting rules** such as 24 hours or 30 days.
4. **Break goals into milestones** and celebrate each one.
5. **Remove triggers** like saved cards.

## A note on fairness

Waiting is easier with stable income. If money is tight, small rewards can matter. Plan some fun money so the process is sustainable.
