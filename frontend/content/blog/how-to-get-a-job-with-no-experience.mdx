"Entry level, 2 years experience required" is one of the most annoying sentences in job hunting. The way through is to prove your ability in other ways. Here is how.

## Direct answer

To get a job with no experience, look for entry-level or trainee roles, turn what you have already done (school, volunteering, projects, family work) into skills on your CV, create small proof of your ability, tell people you are looking and apply to many roles while tailoring each application. Employers hire people who look reliable and able to learn.

## 9 steps

1. **Pick a target.** Choose one or two kinds of entry role, so your CV can match them.
2. **List what you already have.** Group projects, clubs, volunteering, caring for family, selling things, helping at a family business. These are real.
3. **Name the skills.** Reliability, communication, teamwork, problem solving, basic software.
4. **Build small proof.** A tiny project, a volunteer day, a short course with a practical task. See [self-taught skills that get you hired](/blog/self-taught-skills-that-actually-get-you-hired).
5. **Write a clear CV.** See [how to write a CV with no experience](/blog/how-to-write-a-cv-with-no-experience).
6. **Tell your network.** Many entry jobs never reach job boards. See [how to network for a job](/blog/how-to-network-for-a-job).
7. **Apply widely, tailor each.** Mirror the words in the job post.
8. **Prepare for interviews.** Have short true stories ready. See [first job interview tips](/blog/first-job-interview-tips).
9. **Say yes to a foot in the door.** A first job, even not perfect, becomes your experience.

## Jobs that often take beginners

| Area | Examples |
| :--- | :--- |
| Service | Retail, cafes, restaurants, hotels |
| Support | Customer service, helpdesk, admin |
| Practical | Warehouse, delivery, cleaning, care assistant |
| Trainee schemes | Apprenticeships, graduate programmes, internships |

Entry requirements differ by country and employer, so read each post closely.

## If you have no degree

Many roles care more about reliability and a willingness to learn. Look for apprenticeships, trainee programmes and skills-based hiring, and show proof of skills instead of a certificate.

## Working from home

Some remote entry jobs exist, such as support or data entry. Never pay to get a job, never share bank details before a real contract and be suspicious of "easy money" offers.
