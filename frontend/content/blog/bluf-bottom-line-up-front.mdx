Readers should not have to hunt for the point.

## Direct answer

BLUF means starting with your conclusion, decision or request, then giving the supporting detail. It saves readers time and makes your message clear. Use it in emails, reports and meetings.

## Before and after

**Before:**
"Hi, I have been looking at last quarter's numbers and there are a few things I wanted to mention about the budget..."

**After:**
"We need an extra 5,000 for the launch. Here is why, and what I recommend."

## How to apply

1. Write your main point in one sentence.
2. Put it first.
3. Add three supporting points.
4. End with the next step.

## Email template

**Subject:** Decision needed by Friday: venue for the offsite
**Body:** I recommend Venue B. It fits our budget and has availability. Please approve by Friday. Details below.
