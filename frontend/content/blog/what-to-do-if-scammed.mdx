If it happened to you, you are not alone, and you are not foolish. Scammers are professionals.

## Direct answer

If you've been scammed, contact your bank or payment provider straight away, stop all contact with the scammer, change passwords on affected accounts, keep evidence, report it to the platform and your country's fraud authority, and watch for follow-up scams. The sooner you act, the better your chances of limiting the damage.

## First 24 hours

1. **Call your bank** using the number on your card.
2. **Stop paying or replying.**
3. **Secure your accounts:** change passwords and enable two-factor authentication.
4. **Keep evidence:** screenshots, messages, receipts, account numbers.
5. **Report it** to the platform, the police or fraud service in your country.
6. **Warn friends and family** if they could be targeted.
7. **Watch for recovery scams,** where fraudsters offer to get your money back for a fee.

## Look after yourself

Scams cause stress and shame. Talk to someone you trust, and seek support services if you need them.
