## 1. Core Responsibilities of an SDF

### Conducting Corporate Skills Audits
An SDF conducts detailed surveys, manager interviews, and competency assessments to determine where employee performance bottlenecks exist—such as digital literacy, project management, or AI automation.

### Developing the Workplace Skills Plan (WSP)
The WSP is a formal document detailing what training interventions the company will provide over the coming financial year. It balances internal onboarding with external accredited certifications.

### Managing Training Grants and Compliance
In many regulatory environments (such as South Africa's SETA framework or UK apprenticeship levies), submitting an approved WSP and ATR allows employers to recover significant portions of their skills development levies.
