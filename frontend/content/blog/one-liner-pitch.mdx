If you cannot say it in one sentence, you may not have clarity yet.

## Direct answer

A one-liner pitch states what you do, for whom and the benefit in one clear sentence. Use the formula "We help [audience] [result] by [method]" and test it by asking someone to repeat it back.

## Examples

- "We help small teams share files securely without IT support."
- "I help startups turn customer feedback into product decisions."

## Steps

1. Write ten versions.
2. Remove jargon.
3. Pick the clearest.
4. Test with three people.
