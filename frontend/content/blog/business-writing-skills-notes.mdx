## Direct Answer: The 7 Cs of Business Writing at a Glance

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 THE 7 Cs OF BUSINESS WRITING                │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  1. CLEAR       ──> Plain words, unambiguous purpose        │
│  2. CONCISE     ──> Maximum meaning in minimum words        │
│  3. CONCRETE    ──> Specific data & facts over generalities │
│  4. CORRECT     ──> Flawless grammar, formatting & data     │
│  5. COHERENT    ──> Logical structural flow between ideas   │
│  6. COMPLETE    ──> Answers all questions with clear next step
│  7. COURTEOUS   ──> Respectful, professional & empathetic   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 1. The BLUF Framework (Bottom Line Up Front)

Executives receive over 120 emails a day. If your key takeaway is buried in paragraph four, it will be missed:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 THE EXECUTIVE BLUF EMAIL FORMAT             │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Subject: [Action Required] Q3 Marketing Budget Approval    │
│                                                             │
│  Hi Team,                                                   │
│                                                             │
│  BLUF: We are requesting approval for a $15,000 Q3 budget   │
│  reallocation to increase paid search acquisition by 25%.   │
│                                                             │
│  Key Highlights:                                            │
│  • CAC decreased by 18% in Q2 pilot testing.                │

│  • Zero impact on existing engineering expenditures.        │
│                                                             │
│  Next Step: Please reply with approval by Thursday 5 PM.    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Formal Memorandum (Memo) Structure

A memo is an internal organizational broadcast used to document procedural updates:

1. **Header Block:** `TO:`, `FROM:`, `DATE:`, `SUBJECT:`.
2. **Opening Paragraph:** State the exact reason for the memo in 2 sentences.
3. **Body Paragraphs / Bullet Points:** Group background facts and policy details under bold subheadings.
4. **Action Close:** Specify who must do what, and by what deadline.

## 3. Editing for Brevity: Cutting the "Fluff"

* *Wordy:* "Due to the fact that we experienced an unexpected drop in revenue..."
* *Concise:* "Because revenue dropped unexpectedly..."
* *Wordy:* "In the event that the client makes an inquiry regarding pricing..."
* *Concise:* "If the client asks about pricing..."
