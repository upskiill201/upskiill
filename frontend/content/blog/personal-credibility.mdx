Credibility is built slowly and lost quickly.

## Direct answer

Personal credibility is built from competence, reliability, honesty and consistency. Do what you say, be open about limits, communicate early about problems and keep improving your skills. Over time, people trust your word and your judgement.

## Habits

1. **Keep promises, even small ones.**
2. **Say "I do not know"** when true, and find out.
3. **Share bad news early.**
4. **Prepare thoroughly.**
5. **Be consistent** across situations.
6. **Give credit.**

## After a mistake

Own it, fix it, explain what changes and follow through.
