Wanting something and buying it do not have to happen on the same day.

## Direct answer

The wish list method means adding items to a list instead of buying immediately. You wait a set time, then review the list and only buy what you still want, can afford and have a plan to use. It works because many urges fade within days.

## How to set it up

1. **Create a list** in a notes app or on paper. Include the item, price and date.
2. **Set waiting periods:** 24 hours for small items, 7 days for medium, 30 days for large.
3. **Review on a schedule,** such as every Sunday.
4. **Ask questions:** Do I still want it? Do I have something like it? Can I afford it from this month's budget?
5. **Cross off what you skip** and note the money saved.

## Tips

- Add a note on why you want it.
- Keep a "treat" budget for planned buys.
- Share the list with a friend for accountability.
