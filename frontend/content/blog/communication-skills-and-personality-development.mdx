## 1. Verbal Communication: The Art of Articulate Speech

The way you speak shapes how the world perceives your intelligence:

* **Eliminate Filler Words:** Silence is far more authoritative than "um" or "you know." When thinking, simply pause and breathe.
* **The Power of Vocal Pitch:** Avoid upward inflection (raising your pitch at the end of statements as if asking for permission). Make declarative statements with downward tonal resolution.
* **Expanded Lexicon:** Reading quality literature conditions your brain to choose precise words naturally (see [lsrw skills in English communication](/blog/lsrw-skills-english-communication)).

---

## 2. Nonverbal Presence & Grounded Body Language

Over 70% of human communication is communicated before you speak a single word:

1. **The 3-Second Eye Contact Rule:** When speaking to someone, hold warm eye contact for 3 to 4 seconds before naturally looking away—never dart your eyes around the room.
2. **Open Torso Posture:** Keep shoulders pulled back and down, uncross your arms, and expose your chest to signal non-defensiveness.
3. **The Slow Walk:** Rushed movements signal anxiety; smooth, unhurried movements signal executive authority.

## 3. Emotional Intelligence: Making Others Feel Important

As Dale Carnegie famously wrote in *How to Win Friends and Influence People*: *"You can make more friends in two months by becoming interested in other people than you can in two years by trying to get other people interested in you."*

* **Remember and Use Names:** A person's name is to that person the sweetest sound in any language.
* **Set Healthy Boundaries:** True charisma comes with self-respect (read [refusal skills definition and examples](/blog/refusal-skills-definition-and-examples)).
