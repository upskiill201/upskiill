AI built into the place where your notes already live.

## Direct answer

Notion AI adds writing, summarising, brainstorming and organising features inside Notion pages and databases. It can draft text, summarise long pages, pull out action items and help answer questions about your workspace. Review its output and check Notion's current plans and data policies.

## Good uses

- Summarise a long document.
- Turn rough notes into a clear outline.
- Extract tasks from meeting notes.
- Rewrite for tone or length.
- Search and ask questions across a workspace, where available.

## Prompt tips

- State the audience and format: "Write a friendly update for the team, under 100 words."
- Paste the source text you want it to use.
- Ask for options, then edit.

## Cautions

- Check facts and figures.
- Be mindful of sensitive information.
- Follow your organisation's policies.
