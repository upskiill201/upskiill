A warm introduction beats a cold application.

## Direct answer

To get a referral, find people at the company, build a genuine connection, share the role and your resume, explain why you fit and make it easy for them to say yes. Always be polite and follow up.

## Message template

"Hi [name], I am applying for [role] at [company]. My experience in [skill] matches the requirements, and I have attached my resume. Would you be comfortable referring me, or sharing any advice?"

## Tips

1. Do not ask strangers for a favour on first contact. Start with advice.
2. Make it easy: give links and a summary.
3. Thank them and update them.
