Groceries are flexible, which makes them a good place to save.

## Direct answer

Set a grocery budget by averaging your last three months of food spending, then aim a little lower. Divide it by weeks, track every shop and adjust. Reduce costs with a meal plan, a shopping list, unit price comparison, own-brand products and less waste.

## How to set the number

1. Add up three months of grocery spending from bank statements.
2. Divide by three to get an average.
3. Subtract 5 to 10 percent as a target.
4. Divide by 4 for a weekly amount.

## Tools that cut costs

- **A list** based on a meal plan.
- **Unit prices,** not shelf prices.
- **Own-brand basics.**
- **Frozen produce** to reduce waste.
- **Eating before shopping.**
- **Using up leftovers** and checking dates.

## Track it

Write down each shop or use a notes app. If you overspend one week, adjust the next. After a month, review whether the target was realistic.
