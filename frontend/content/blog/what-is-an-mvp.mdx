Build the smallest thing that teaches you something.

## Direct answer

An MVP (minimum viable product) is the smallest version of your product that solves the core problem for early users. Define one problem, one user and one main feature, build it quickly, put it in front of real people and learn what to do next. AI tools can speed up the build, but not the learning.

## How to define it

1. **Who** is the user?
2. **What problem** do they have?
3. **What is the one thing** your product must do?
4. **What can you leave out** for now?

## Build it

- Use AI builders or no-code tools for a first version.
- Fake what you can (manual work behind the scenes).
- Avoid extras like settings pages and perfect design.

## Learn from it

- Watch people use it.
- Ask what they expected.
- Measure one number, such as sign-ups or repeat use.
