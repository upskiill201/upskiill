## Direct answer

Retirement accounts are accounts designed to hold long-term savings, often with tax benefits. Types and rules vary by country, but the common ideas are the same: save regularly, take any employer match, understand fees, and invest according to your time horizon and risk tolerance.

## Common types

- **Workplace pensions or plans** where your employer helps you save.
- **Individual retirement accounts** you open yourself.
- **Government pension systems** that pay in later life.

## Key ideas

1. **Start early** so compounding has time.
2. **Take any employer match.** It is extra pay.
3. **Watch fees,** which reduce long-term returns.
4. **Diversify** rather than putting all savings in one asset.
5. **Check the rules** on withdrawals and taxes in your country.

## Where to learn more

Your government's pension or tax website and a licensed adviser can explain rules for your situation.
