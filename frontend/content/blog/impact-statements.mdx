Duties describe a job. Impact describes you.

## Direct answer

An impact statement describes the result of your actions, such as "Cut report preparation time by 30 percent by creating a template." Use the formula: action, what you did, result. Quantify where you can.

## Examples

| Duty | Impact |
| :--- | :--- |
| Answered customer emails | Reduced average response time from 24 to 8 hours |
| Managed social media | Grew followers by 40 percent in six months |
| Prepared reports | Built a template that saved the team four hours a week |

## Tips

1. Start with strong verbs.
2. Add numbers or scale.
3. Keep each to one line.
