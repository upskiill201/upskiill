Someone near you probably has the thing you need sitting unused.

## Direct answer

A Buy Nothing group is a neighbourhood community where people give away items for free and ask for things they need, with no payment or swapping. Search for local groups on social platforms or community apps, read the rules, then post what you can give or what you are looking for.

## What people share

Furniture, baby items, kitchen tools, clothes, books, plants, and sometimes services like lifts or help moving.

## Etiquette

- Follow each group's rules.
- Reply promptly and be on time for pickups.
- Say thank you.
- Pay it forward when you can.
- Describe items honestly, including any flaws.

## Safety tips

- Use public or well-lit pickup points when possible.
- Do not share more personal information than needed.
- Bring a friend for larger items or evening pickups.

## Related ideas

Local swap events, repair cafes and library-of-things schemes work the same way. See [frugal living tips](/blog/frugal-living-tips) for more.
