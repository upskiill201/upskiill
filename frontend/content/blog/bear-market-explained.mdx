Bear markets feel awful in the moment and are normal over long investing lives.

## Direct answer

A bear market is a prolonged period when prices fall around 20 percent or more from recent highs, often alongside pessimism and economic worries. A bull market is the opposite. During a bear market, long-term investors usually focus on their plan, their emergency fund and avoiding panic.

## Common causes

- Economic slowdowns
- Rising interest rates
- Financial crises
- Major shocks

## How to cope

1. **Remind yourself of your time horizon.**
2. **Keep an emergency fund** so you are not a forced seller.
3. **Rebalance if your plan says so.**
4. **Avoid borrowing to invest.**
5. **Limit news checking.**
