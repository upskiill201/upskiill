Authority is easier. Influence takes skill.

## Direct answer

To lead peers, build trust first: be reliable, listen, help others succeed and share credit. Make requests clear, link them to shared goals and invite input instead of giving orders. Be honest about what you do not know and follow through on promises.

## Habits that work

1. **Deliver on your own work** before asking others to change.
2. **Ask questions** and listen.
3. **Offer help** before asking for it.
4. **Make decisions easy:** give options and a recommendation.
5. **Share credit** generously.
6. **Address problems privately** and calmly.

## Awkward situations

- **A peer ignores your request:** ask what is in the way.
- **You disagree:** focus on the goal, not the person.
- **Competition:** look for ways both can win.
