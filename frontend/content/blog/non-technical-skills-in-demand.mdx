## 1. Problem Framing: Finding the Right Question

Average employees rush to solve the first problem presented to them. Elite professionals practice **root cause analysis**:

* **The "5 Whys" Framework:** Asking "Why did this occur?" five times to drill past surface symptoms down to the structural breakdown.
* **First Principles Thinking:** Stripping an issue down to its most basic, undeniable truths and reasoning upwards from there.
