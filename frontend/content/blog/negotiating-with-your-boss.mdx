Your manager is a person with limits and goals. Work with them.

## Direct answer

To negotiate with your boss, prepare your case with evidence, decide your target and fallback, pick a calm moment, link your request to their goals and listen. If the answer is no, ask what would change it and agree on a follow-up.

## Steps

1. **Define the ask.**
2. **Collect evidence:** results, feedback, market data.
3. **Frame it** around shared goals.
4. **Ask clearly.**
5. **Listen** and explore options.
6. **Agree next steps** in writing.

## Script

"I have delivered X and Y this year. I would like to discuss a pay increase to reflect that. What would you need to see?"
