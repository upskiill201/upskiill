Crypto moves fast and is hard to reverse, which is why scammers like it.

## Direct answer

Common crypto scams include fake exchanges and apps, giveaways that ask you to send crypto first, phishing sites that steal wallet keys, rug pulls where developers drop a project and run off with funds, and fake investment platforms. Never share your recovery phrase and double-check every link and address.

## Types to know

- **Giveaway scams:** "send 1, get 2 back".
- **Phishing:** fake wallet or exchange pages.
- **Rug pulls:** new tokens abandoned after funds are raised.
- **Impersonation:** fake support or celebrity endorsements.
- **Fake platforms:** show fake profits, block withdrawals.

## Safety habits

1. Use well-known platforms and verify the web address.
2. Never share your seed phrase or private keys.
3. Turn on two-factor authentication.
4. Test with small amounts.
5. Ignore unsolicited investment advice.
