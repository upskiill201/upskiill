Paying in four instalments feels small. Several of them together may not be.

## Direct answer

Buy now pay later lets you split a purchase into instalments, often interest-free if you pay on time. It can help with planned spending but can encourage impulse buying, create several overlapping payments and bring fees or interest if you miss one.

## How it works

1. You choose a provider at checkout.
2. You pay a part now and the rest in scheduled payments.
3. You may be charged late fees or interest if you miss a payment.

## Risks

- **Overspending:** it makes pricey items feel cheap.
- **Tracking:** several plans at once are hard to manage.
- **Fees:** late fees can add up.
- **Returns:** refunds can be slow.

## Use it only if

- You were going to buy the item anyway.
- You can afford every payment from your current budget.
- You have set up reminders or automatic payments.
- You are only using one or two plans at a time.
