## 1. Digital & Technical Logistics Skills

As supply chains digitize, employers place premium value on warehouse workers who are comfortable with digital tools:

* **Warehouse Management Systems (WMS):** Knowing how to log receipts, transfer bin locations, and close shipping manifests in systems like SAP or Oracle.
* **Spreadsheet Inventory Tracking:** Mastering basic Excel data entry, filtering, and row pinning (see [how to alphabetize in Excel](/blog/how-to-alphabetize-in-excel) and [how to pin a row in Excel](/blog/how-to-pin-a-row-in-excel)).

Recruiters review hundreds of generic resumes stating *"responsible for moving boxes."* Transform your bullet points with numbers:
