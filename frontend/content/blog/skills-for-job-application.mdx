## 1. How to Beat the ATS (Applicant Tracking System) Keyword Filter

Most enterprise companies use ATS software (like Greenhouse, Lever, or Workday) that ranks applicants based on keyword density.

### 3 Rules for ATS Optimization:
1. **Mirror the Job Description:** If the job listing asks for "Relational Database Management (SQL)", do not just write "Databases"—write "Relational Database Management (SQL)".
2. **Use Standard Headings:** Stick to clean headers like `Technical Skills`, `Professional Experience`, and `Education`.
3. **Quantify with Numbers:** Every skill bullet point should follow Google's X-Y-Z formula: *"Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]."*
