## 1. Core Clinical Competencies Every MA Must Master

### Accurate Vital Signs Assessment
Accurate manual blood pressure measurement (listening for Korotkoff sounds) remains a gold-standard diagnostic skill. Even a 5 mmHg recording error can alter a patient's hypertension management plan.

### Phlebotomy & Specimen Handling
Mastering the Order of Draw (Blood culture bottles $\rightarrow$ Light blue $\rightarrow$ Red/Gold $\rightarrow$ Green $\rightarrow$ Lavender $\rightarrow$ Gray) prevents cross-contamination of tube additives and ensures reliable laboratory diagnostics.

### Infection Control and Aseptic Technique
Complying strictly with OSHA and CDC universal precautions, sterile field setup, and proper autoclaving of surgical instruments protects both patients and clinical staff.

## 2. Administrative & Healthcare IT Skills

* **Electronic Health Records (EHR):** Charting Chief Complaints (CC), recording medication reconciliations, and routing laboratory results.
* **Insurance Prior Authorization:** Communicating with health insurers to secure pre-approval for specialized procedures and medications.
* **Medical Billing Basics:** Understanding ICD-10 diagnostic codes and CPT procedural codes to prevent insurance claim denials.

---

## 3. Patient Psychology and De-escalation

Patients visiting a medical clinic are often anxious, in physical pain, or frightened by potential diagnoses:

1. **Active Listening:** Allow patients to finish describing their symptoms without interrupting.
2. **Empathetic Body Language:** Sit at eye level with the patient rather than standing over them with a clipboard.
3. **Clear Patient Education:** Explain discharge instructions in plain language without intimidating medical jargon (see [benefits of reading communication skills and social engagement](/blog/benefits-of-reading-communication-skills-social-engagement)).
