A customer who searches for you and finds nothing may go elsewhere. A simple website fixes that.

## Direct answer

A small business website needs a clear page that says what you do and for whom, how to contact or book you, where you are, and proof you are good (photos, reviews). Start with one to four pages. You can build it yourself with a website builder or with HTML and CSS, then make sure it works on phones.

## Pages to include

1. **Home:** what you do in one sentence, and a clear button to contact or book.
2. **Services or products:** what you offer and prices or "from" prices.
3. **About:** who you are, a photo, why customers trust you.
4. **Contact:** phone, email, address, map, opening hours, a form.

## What makes it work

- A clear headline
- Good photos of your work
- Customer reviews, with permission
- Fast loading
- Easy to use on a phone. See [what is responsive web design](/blog/what-is-responsive-web-design)

## Build or buy

| Option | Best for | Trade-off |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Website builder | Quick, no coding | Less control, upgrade costs |
| Build it yourself with code | Learning and control | Takes time |
| Hire a designer | Complex needs, little time | Cost |

If you want the build-it-yourself route, see [how to make a website for beginners](/blog/how-to-make-a-website-for-beginners).

## Cost

Free options exist, but costs vary by what you need: domain, builder plan, shop features. We don't list prices because they change, so compare current plans.

## Getting found

- Put your business name, town and service in page titles.
- Create a Google Business Profile.
- Keep details consistent across the web.
- Ask happy customers for reviews.

## Selling online

Add a payment link or shop tool. Check fees and delivery rules for your area.
