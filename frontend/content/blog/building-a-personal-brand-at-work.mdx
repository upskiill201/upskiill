People talk about you when you are not in the room. Guide what they say.

## Direct answer

To build a personal brand at work, choose two or three strengths you want to be known for, deliver on them consistently, help others, share your results and build relationships across teams. Be authentic and keep your word.

## Steps

1. **Pick your focus.**
2. **Deliver consistently.**
3. **Share progress** in updates and meetings.
4. **Help others.**
5. **Ask for feedback.**
6. **Be visible** appropriately.
