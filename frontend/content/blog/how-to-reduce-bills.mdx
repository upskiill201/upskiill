Bills repeat every month, so small cuts add up. Once you fix one, the saving keeps coming.

## Direct answer

To reduce bills, list every recurring cost, compare providers, negotiate or switch plans, cancel anything unused and cut usage where you can. Start with the biggest ones: housing, energy, insurance, phone and internet. Review them once or twice a year.

## 12 ways

1. List all recurring payments from your bank statements.
2. Cancel subscriptions you do not use.
3. Compare phone plans, especially if you are out of contract.
4. Compare broadband and ask what your provider can offer.
5. Compare insurance when it renews.
6. Switch energy provider or tariff if you can.
7. Lower energy use: lights, heating, standby devices.
8. Pay annually if it is cheaper and you can afford it.
9. Ask for loyalty or retention deals.
10. Bundle services only if the total is cheaper.
11. Use free alternatives for entertainment.
12. Set a calendar reminder to review each bill.

## What to say when you call

"I have been a customer for a while and I am reviewing my bills. Is there a cheaper plan or promotion available for me?" Stay polite and be ready to switch if the answer is no.
