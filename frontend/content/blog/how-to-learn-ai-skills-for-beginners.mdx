## 1. Explaining AI to Beginners (How Does AI Actually Learn?)

At its core, modern AI (Machine Learning) does not "think" like a human brain—it is a **mathematical pattern recognition engine**:

```
[Traditional Programming] ──> Rules + Data = Answers
[Machine Learning AI]     ──> Data + Answers = Rules (Model Weights)
```

1. **The Training Phase:** The AI reads billions of words and adjusts billions of tiny numerical dials (weights) until it can predict the next word in a sentence with high accuracy.
2. **Inference:** When you type a prompt, the model uses those fine-tuned weights to generate the most statistically probable and contextually relevant response.
