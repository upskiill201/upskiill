Two people on the same income can spend very differently. Values explain why.

## Direct answer

Value-based spending means directing your money to what you care about most, such as family, learning, travel or health, and cutting spending that does not add value. You start by naming your values, then check each spending category against them.

## Step by step

1. **List your top three to five values.**
2. **Review last month's spending** and mark each item as high, medium or low value.
3. **Cut or reduce low-value items.**
4. **Give high-value categories a proper budget.**
5. **Review every quarter** as your life changes.

## Example

Someone who values learning might spend more on courses and books and less on clothing. Someone who values time with family might choose a smaller home near relatives over a bigger one farther away.
