Returns that look too steady and too high are a warning.

## Direct answer

A Ponzi scheme is a fraud that pays existing investors using money from new investors rather than real profit. It collapses when new money dries up. Warning signs include guaranteed high returns, unclear strategies, pressure to reinvest and difficulty cashing out.

## How it works

1. The operator promises high, steady returns.
2. Early investors get paid, which builds trust.
3. They recommend it to others.
4. New money pays old investors.
5. When recruitment slows or many withdraw, it collapses.

## Red flags

- Guaranteed or unusually steady returns.
- A secret or vague strategy.
- Unlicensed sellers.
- Pressure to bring in friends.
- Delays in withdrawing money.

## Protect yourself

- Check the seller and product with your country's financial regulator.
- Ask how returns are generated.
- Never invest money you cannot afford to lose.
- Get independent advice.
