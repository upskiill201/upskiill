Executives want the answer first and the detail on demand.

## Direct answer

When communicating with executives, lead with your conclusion or request, give the impact in numbers, offer options and a recommendation and keep supporting detail ready. Be concise, honest about risks and clear about the decision you need.

## A simple format

1. **Headline:** the point in one sentence.
2. **Why it matters:** impact on goals, cost, risk.
3. **Options:** two or three, with a recommendation.
4. **Ask:** what you need and by when.
5. **Backup:** detail available if wanted.

## Example

"Recommend delaying the launch two weeks to fix the payment bug. This protects our launch-day revenue and costs one extra sprint. Approval needed by Thursday."
