## Why XP and Streaks Work (When Done Right)

XP works because it converts an abstract, slow-moving process — getting better at a skill — into a concrete, immediate number that goes up after every single action. Your brain registers "+15 XP" as a real reward signal even though nothing externally changed; that's not a flaw, it's the entire mechanism that makes daily practice feel worthwhile before the skill itself has visibly improved.

Streaks work through the opposite mechanism: loss aversion. A visible daily-use counter reframes skipping a day from a neutral non-event into something you're actively losing. Combined, XP pulls you forward and streaks push you not to stop — which is precisely why apps using both together tend to sustain daily active use far longer than apps using neither.

## What Separates a Good XP System From a Shallow One

Not all XP systems are equal. Watch for these differences:

- **Granularity:** XP awarded per micro-action (finishing a step within a lesson) beats XP awarded only at the end of a full lesson — more frequent rewards sustain engagement better.
- **Meaningful levels:** XP that unlocks genuinely new content or skill tiers is more motivating than XP that just accumulates into a number with no consequence.
- **Honest difficulty scaling:** Some apps inflate XP for trivial actions to manufacture a sense of progress — a red flag if the actual skill isn't advancing at the same rate the number is.
