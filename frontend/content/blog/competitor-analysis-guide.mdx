Know what customers can choose instead of you.

## Direct answer

To analyse competitors, list direct and indirect options, compare what they offer, their prices and positioning, read customer reviews to find complaints and gaps and decide how you will be different. Update it regularly.

## Steps

1. List competitors.
2. Capture offers and prices.
3. Read reviews.
4. Spot patterns and gaps.
5. Decide your angle.
