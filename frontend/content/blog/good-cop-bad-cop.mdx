When one person is tough and the other helps you out, ask who benefits.

## Direct answer

In good cop, bad cop, one negotiator makes harsh demands while another seems reasonable, nudging you to accept the "reasonable" offer. Respond by recognising the pattern, staying focused on your interests and objective criteria and taking a break if needed.

## How to respond

1. **Notice** the shift in tone.
2. **Do not reward pressure.**
3. **Return to criteria:** "What standard are we using to judge this?"
4. **Take time:** "I will get back to you tomorrow."
5. **Stay respectful.**
