Financial independence is about choices, not luxury.

## Direct answer

Financial independence means your assets produce enough income to cover your living costs, so work becomes optional. Work out your yearly spending, set a savings target, and build it through regular saving and investing. Rules of thumb exist, but they are estimates and carry risk.

## First steps

1. **Know your annual spending.**
2. **Save a fixed share of income.**
3. **Clear high-interest debt.**
4. **Build an emergency fund.**
5. **Invest regularly** in a diversified way suited to your risk and time horizon.
6. **Raise income** through skills and career growth.

## Common rule of thumb

A target of around 25 times annual spending comes from the idea of withdrawing about 4 percent a year. It is a rough guide that depends on markets, costs and how long you need the money to last.
