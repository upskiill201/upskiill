With limited money, order matters.

## Direct answer

A common order is: cover essentials, build a small emergency fund, pay minimums on all debts, clear high-interest debt, build a fuller emergency fund, then save and invest for long-term goals while keeping some money for enjoyment. Adjust it to your situation.

## A simple sequence

1. **Essentials** such as housing, food, utilities and transport.
2. **A starter emergency fund** to avoid new debt from small shocks.
3. **Minimum payments** on all debts.
4. **High-interest debt,** using the [avalanche](/blog/debt-avalanche-method) or [snowball](/blog/debt-snowball-method) method.
5. **A fuller emergency fund.**
6. **Long-term saving and investing.**
7. **Other goals and fun.**

## Make it yours

Employer pension matching, a mortgage deposit or a tight job market may change your order. If in doubt, speak to a qualified adviser.
