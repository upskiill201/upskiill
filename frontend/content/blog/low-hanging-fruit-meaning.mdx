Reach the apples you can reach first.

## Direct answer

Low-hanging fruit are the easiest tasks or opportunities that give a quick benefit for little effort. They are good for momentum, but do not let them crowd out important longer-term work.

## Examples

- Fixing a confusing form that causes support tickets.
- Cancelling unused software subscriptions.
- Updating an outdated page that gets lots of traffic.

## Plain alternatives

- Quick wins
- Easy improvements
- Simple fixes first
