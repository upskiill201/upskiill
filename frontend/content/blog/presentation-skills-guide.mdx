## 1. Structuring the Narrative Arc (The 3-Act Formula)

Every unforgettable presentation follows the timeless structure of classical drama:

```
[Act 1: The Status Quo & The Threat] ──> Establish what is, and what will go wrong if nothing changes
                 │
                 ▼
[Act 2: The Breakthrough & Evidence] ──> Introduce your solution with 3 concrete data proofs
                 │
                 ▼
[Act 3: The Call to Action (CTA)]    ──> Paint the glorious future and demand a specific next step
```

* **The 10/20/30 Rule:** Guy Kawasaki's standard for pitch presentations: **10 slides**, **20 minutes max**, **30-point minimum font size**.

---

## 2. Vocal Modulation and the Power Pause

The most charismatic speakers use silence as a weapon:

1. **The Power Pause:** Before delivering your most critical number or conclusion, pause for 2 full seconds. This commands instant silence and focus in the room.
2. **Eliminating Filler Words:** Whenever you feel the urge to say "um" or "like," simply close your mouth and take a breath.
3. **Vocal Pacing:** Speak at 130–150 words per minute, slowing down during complex explanations (learn more in [how to learn business skills](/blog/how-to-learn-business-skills)).

## 3. Handling Hostile Q&A Like a Seasoned Executive

When tough questions arise, never get defensive. Use the **Listen-Validate-Answer (LVA)** method:

* **Listen:** Let the questioner finish completely without interrupting.
* **Validate:** *"That is a critical point regarding our Q3 delivery timeline..."*
* **Answer:** *"Here is the exact mitigation protocol we have in place..."*
