Every concession should buy something.

## Direct answer

Plan your concessions in advance, rank them by cost to you and value to the other side and trade them for something in return, using "if you, then I" phrasing. Make later concessions smaller to signal you are near your limit.

## Phrases

- "If you can commit to a two-year term, I can offer a lower rate."
- "I could do that if we agree on delivery by the 15th."

## Tips

1. List what you can give and want.
2. Trade, do not give.
3. Make concessions smaller over time.
4. Keep a record.
