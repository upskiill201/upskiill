Who did what? Active voice answers it.

## Direct answer

In active voice, the subject performs the action: "The team approved the plan." In passive voice, the subject receives it: "The plan was approved by the team." Prefer active voice for clear, direct writing, and use passive when the actor is unknown or unimportant.

## Examples

| Passive | Active |
| :--- | :--- |
| The report was written by Sam. | Sam wrote the report. |
| Mistakes were made. | We made mistakes. |
| The server was restarted. | Ops restarted the server. |

## When passive is fine

- The doer is unknown: "The window was broken."
- The action matters more: "Your order has been shipped."
- Diplomacy: "The deadline was missed" (use sparingly and honestly).

## How to fix it

1. Find "was", "were", "been" plus a verb.
2. Ask who did it.
3. Move that person to the front.
