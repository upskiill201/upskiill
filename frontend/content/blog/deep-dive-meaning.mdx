A summary tells you what. A deep dive shows you why.

## Direct answer

A deep dive is a detailed examination of a topic, such as a session dedicated to understanding a problem or analysing data. Ask for one when you need more than a summary. A plain alternative is "a closer look".

## Running one

1. State the question.
2. Gather data beforehand.
3. Set a time limit.
4. End with decisions and actions.

## Examples

- "Let us do a deep dive on why churn rose last quarter."
