## Direct answer

A neural network is made of layers of simple units. Each unit takes inputs, multiplies them by weights, adds them up and passes the result through a function. By adjusting the weights to reduce errors on training data, the network learns patterns such as recognising images or predicting text.

## The pieces

- **Input layer:** receives the data.
- **Hidden layers:** transform it step by step.
- **Output layer:** gives the prediction.
- **Weights:** numbers that are adjusted during training.

## How it learns

1. Make a prediction.
2. Measure the error with a loss function.
3. Adjust weights using [gradient descent](/blog/gradient-descent-explained).
4. Repeat over many examples.

## Where they are used

Image recognition, speech, translation and language models.
