A friendly way to say "let us speak briefly".

## Direct answer

To touch base is to make brief contact to share updates or check in. For example, "Let us touch base on Friday" means "Let us have a short chat on Friday." You can say "check in" or "catch up" for the same idea.

## Examples

- "I will touch base with the client tomorrow."
- "Can we touch base after the meeting?"

## Plain alternatives

- Check in
- Catch up
- Have a quick chat

## Tip

Add a purpose: "Let us touch base on Friday to confirm the launch date."
