Projects fail from people problems more often than technical ones.

## Direct answer

Stakeholder management means identifying who is affected by or can influence your work, understanding what they care about and keeping them informed and involved at the right level. Map them by influence and interest, plan communication and handle resistance with listening.

## Simple steps

1. **List stakeholders.**
2. **Map by influence and interest.**
3. **Decide engagement:** high influence and high interest means manage closely.
4. **Plan communication:** what, how often, how.
5. **Check in** and adjust.

## Grid

| | Low interest | High interest |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High influence** | keep satisfied | manage closely |
| **Low influence** | monitor | keep informed |
