A tiny difference that can feel big.

## Direct answer

Charm pricing sets prices just below a round number. Studies and retailer experience suggest it can boost sales because people focus on the left-most digit. Test it for your audience, and consider round prices for premium or trust-focused products.

## When to use

- Value-oriented products.
- Online stores where price is compared.

## When to avoid

- Luxury or service brands where trust and quality matter more.

Run a simple A/B test and compare conversion and revenue.
