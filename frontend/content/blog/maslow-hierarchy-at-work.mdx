## Direct answer

Maslow's hierarchy suggests people have layers of needs: physiological, safety, belonging, esteem and self-actualisation. At work this maps to fair pay and conditions, job security, team connection, recognition and growth. Use it as a checklist for what your team may be missing, not as a rigid rule.

## At work

| Level | Workplace example |
| :--- | :--- |
| Physiological | fair pay, rest, safe conditions |
| Safety | job security, clear expectations |
| Belonging | team connection, inclusion |
| Esteem | recognition, respect, status |
| Self-actualisation | growth, purpose, challenge |

## How to use it

1. Check the basics first.
2. Ask what is missing for each person.
3. Do not assume everyone is motivated by the same level.

## Limits

Research has not confirmed a strict order. People can seek growth while still worried about security.
