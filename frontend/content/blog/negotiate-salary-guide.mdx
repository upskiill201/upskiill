Many people leave money unclaimed because they do not ask.

## Direct answer

Negotiate salary after receiving an offer. Thank them, express enthusiasm, state a researched number with reasons and discuss other benefits if they cannot move on pay. Stay polite, calm and flexible.

## Scripts

**Responding to an offer:**
"Thank you, I am excited about the role. Based on my research and experience, I was expecting X. Is there flexibility?"

**If they cannot move on salary:**
"Could we look at a sign-on bonus, extra leave or a six-month review?"

**If they ask early:**
"I would like to understand the role first. What range has been budgeted?"

## Mistakes

- Accepting immediately.
- Giving an exact current salary where not required.
- Making ultimatums.
