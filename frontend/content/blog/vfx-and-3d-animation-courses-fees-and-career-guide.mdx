## The 3D Animation & VFX Production Pipeline

Every major studio—from Pixar and Industrial Light & Magic to Epic Games—organizes its workflow into three distinct phases:

```
[The Universal 3D / VFX Studio Pipeline]
                       │
  ┌────────────────────┼────────────────────┐
  │                    │                    │
  ▼                    ▼                    ▼
[Phase 1: Pre-Production] [Phase 2: 3D Production] [Phase 3: Post-Production]
  • Concept Art & Moodboard • 3D Polygonal Modeling  • Multi-Pass Compositing
  • Storyboarding & Animatics• UV Unwrapping & Texture• Rotoscoping & Paint Prep
  • Script & Shot Planning   • Character Rigging     • Matchmoving (3D Track)
                             • Character Animation   • Color Grading & Master
                             • Lighting & Render (Cycles)
```

### 1. Modeling, Sculpting & Texturing
- **Polygonal Modeling:** Building objects, environments, and mechanical props using vertices, edges, and faces in Blender or Maya.
- **Digital Sculpting:** Sculpting organic anatomy, monsters, and characters with millions of polygons in ZBrush or Blender Sculpt mode.
- **PBR Texturing:** Applying Physically Based Rendering materials (Roughness, Metallic, Normal maps) in Substance 3D Painter.

### 2. Rigging and Character Animation
- Creating an internal bone skeleton (rig) with Inverse Kinematics (IK) and Forward Kinematics (FK) controls.
- Applying the **12 Principles of Animation** (Squash & Stretch, Anticipation, Timing, Arc of Motion) to bring 3D characters to life.

### 3. Visual Effects (FX) & Compositing
- Simulating natural physics: fire, smoke, water, explosions, and cloth dynamics using Houdini or Blender Mantaflow.
- Merging live-action footage with 3D CGI passes using nodal compositing software (Foundry Nuke or DaVinci Fusion).
