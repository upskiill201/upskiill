The **best careers for 2027** sit where demand is growing and AI makes people more productive rather than replacing them: data, cybersecurity, software, AI, healthcare and some skilled trades. This guide uses public projections, not guesses, and shows the skills each path needs, many of which you can start on [Teyro](/courses/data-science-for-beginners) in minutes a day.

## Direct answer

The best careers for 2027 include **data scientist**, **information security analyst**, **software developer**, **AI and machine learning engineer**, **healthcare roles**, **clean-energy technicians** and **managers who understand technology**. US Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) projections for 2024 to 2034 show data scientists growing about 34%, information security analysts about 29% and software developers about 16%.

## What the data says

- **BLS, 2024 to 2034 projections:** among the fastest-growing jobs are wind turbine service technicians (about 50%), data scientists (about 34%) and information security analysts (about 29%). Healthcare roles also fill much of the fastest-growing list.
- **World Economic Forum, *Future of Jobs Report 2025*:** employers expect about 170 million jobs to be created and about 92 million displaced by 2030, with AI, data and cybersecurity skills growing fastest.

Projections are estimates. Use them to spot direction, then check job listings where you live.

## High-demand jobs in 2027

| Career | Why demand is growing | Key skills |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Data scientist or analyst | Every organisation runs on data | SQL, Python, statistics |
| Information security analyst | Attacks are growing and more automated | Networking, security, risk |
| Software developer | Every business needs software | A language, Git, systems design |
| AI or machine learning engineer | Companies are building AI into products | Python, ML, evaluation |
| Healthcare roles | Ageing populations | Specialist training |
| Clean-energy technician | New wind and solar capacity | Trade training, safety |
| Tech-savvy manager | Teams need leaders who understand AI | Leadership, communication |

## How to choose your career path for 2027

1. **List what you enjoy.** Problem solving, people, building, caring, persuading.
2. **Match it to a growing field.** Use the table above as a starting point.
3. **Test it cheaply.** Take a short course or build one small project before committing.
4. **Build proof.** Projects and real results beat certificates alone.
5. **Get hired.** Learn the job-hunting skills too. See [how to get a job in 2027](/blog/how-to-get-a-job-in-2027).
