Net worth starts with two columns: what you own and what you owe.

## Direct answer

An asset is something you own that has value or can generate income. A liability is money you owe. Subtract liabilities from assets to find your net worth. Building wealth usually means growing assets and reducing liabilities over time.

## Examples

| Assets | Liabilities |
| :--- | :--- |
| Savings and cash | Credit card balances |
| Investments | Student loans |
| Property you own | Mortgage |
| Business value | Car loans |
| Pension | Unpaid bills |

## A caution on cars and homes

Whether something is an asset in practice depends on its value and costs. A car may lose value and cost money to run, even though it is something you own.

## How to use it

1. List your assets with current values.
2. List your liabilities with balances.
3. Subtract to find net worth.
4. Track it every few months.
