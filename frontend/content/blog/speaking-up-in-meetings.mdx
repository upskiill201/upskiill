Good ideas that stay in your head help nobody.

## Direct answer

To speak up in meetings, prepare one or two points beforehand, speak early before the conversation hardens, use a clear opener like "I have a suggestion", build on others' ideas and stay calm if interrupted. You can also share ideas in writing before the meeting.

## Tips

1. **Prepare a point** and a question.
2. **Speak in the first ten minutes.**
3. **Use signposts:** "Two quick points."
4. **Reference others:** "Building on Sam's idea..."
5. **Handle interruptions:** "Let me finish this point."
6. **Follow up in writing.**

## If you are not invited to speak

Ask the chair for a moment: "Could I add something?"
