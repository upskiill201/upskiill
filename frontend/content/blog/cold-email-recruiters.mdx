Be brief, specific and useful.

## Direct answer

Email a recruiter with a clear subject, a short introduction, two specific reasons you fit and a small ask. Attach your resume and follow up once after a week.

## Template

**Subject:** Junior data analyst with SQL and Python

Hi [name],
I am a data analyst with two years of experience in SQL and dashboards. I noticed your opening for [role]. In my last role I built a reporting tool that saved the team five hours a week. I have attached my resume. Could we talk for 15 minutes this week?
Thanks,
[your name]

## Tips

1. Personalise each message.
2. Keep it under 120 words.
3. Proofread.
