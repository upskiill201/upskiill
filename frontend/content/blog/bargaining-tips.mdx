Haggling is a friendly game with rules.

## Direct answer

To bargain well, know the typical price, start with a polite lower offer, bundle items, stay friendly and be ready to walk away. Respect local customs, as not every place welcomes haggling.

## Tips

1. Know the value.
2. Ask "Is that the best price?"
3. Offer a reasonable counter.
4. Bundle purchases.
5. Pay in cash where appropriate and safe.
6. Be willing to walk away.
7. Say thank you.
