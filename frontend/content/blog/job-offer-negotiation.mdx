An offer is the start of a conversation.

## Direct answer

Review the whole offer: pay, benefits, flexibility, role and growth. Thank them, ask for time, research the market and negotiate politely, with reasons and options. Get the final terms in writing before accepting.

## Checklist

- [ ] Salary and bonus
- [ ] Leave and benefits
- [ ] Hours and flexibility
- [ ] Role and reporting line
- [ ] Training and growth
- [ ] Start date
- [ ] Probation terms

## Script

"Thank you. I am excited about the role. Could I have until Friday to review? I would also like to discuss the salary and start date."
