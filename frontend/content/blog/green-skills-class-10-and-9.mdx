## 1. The 3 Pillars of Sustainable Development

Sustainable development balances three interconnected pillars:

```
                  SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT
                             │
     ┌───────────────────────┼───────────────────────┐
     ▼                       ▼                       ▼
1. Economic Growth      2. Social Inclusion     3. Environmental
• Decent livelihoods    • Equal opportunities   • Resource conservation
• Innovation & infra    • Quality education     • Zero emissions
```

* **The Brundtland Report (1987):** First formally defined sustainable development as *"development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs."*

---

## 2. The 4Rs Waste Management Hierarchy

1. **Refuse:** Say no to disposable plastic bags, single-use cups, and excessive packaging.
2. **Reduce:** Lower your consumption of electricity, fossil fuels, and fresh water.
3. **Reuse:** Repurpose glass jars, shipping boxes, and clothing before discarding.
4. **Recycle:** Segregate organic, plastic, glass, and electronic waste so materials can be reprocessed into raw assets.
