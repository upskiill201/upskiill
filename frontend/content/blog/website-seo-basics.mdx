SEO is a set of habits, not a trick.

## Direct answer

For basic SEO, give every page a unique title and description, use headings in order, write helpful content, make the site fast and mobile friendly, add descriptive alt text, create a sitemap, and submit your site to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

## 12 basics

1. **Unique title** for each page, around 60 characters.
2. **Meta description** that earns the click.
3. **One H1** and clear subheadings.
4. **Helpful content** that answers real questions.
5. **Mobile-friendly design.**
6. **Fast loading** pages.
7. **Descriptive URLs.**
8. **Alt text** on images.
9. **Internal links** between related pages.
10. **A sitemap** at `/sitemap.xml`.
11. **HTTPS.**
12. **Search Console** set up and monitored.

## What not to do

- Copy content from other sites.
- Stuff keywords.
- Buy links.
