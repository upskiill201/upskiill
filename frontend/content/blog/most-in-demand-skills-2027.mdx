The **most in-demand skills in 2027** fall into two groups: technical skills that build and run AI-powered systems, and human skills that AI can't do well. Employers want people who have some of both. This guide pulls together what the data actually says and shows how to start learning each skill on [Teyro](/courses/data-science-for-beginners).

## Direct answer

The most in-demand skills for 2027 are **AI and machine learning**, **data analysis**, **cybersecurity**, **software development**, **cloud computing** and **technology literacy**, plus **analytical thinking**, **creative thinking**, **adaptability**, **leadership** and **communication**. The tech skills get you hired. The human skills get you promoted.

## What the data says

Two public sources are worth knowing:

- **World Economic Forum, *Future of Jobs Report 2025*.** Based on a survey of more than 1,000 large employers, it lists AI and big data, networks and cybersecurity, and technology literacy as the fastest-growing skills. It expects about 170 million new jobs and about 92 million displaced by 2030.
- **US Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2024 to 2034 projections.** Data scientists (about 34%), information security analysts (about 29%) and software developers (about 16%) are all projected to grow much faster than the average job.

Nobody can predict 2027 exactly, but these trends have been consistent for several years.

### Why AI skills top the list

Most companies are trying to use AI, and few have enough people who know how. That includes developers who can build with AI and non-technical staff who can use it well. Our guide to [which AI skills are most in demand](/blog/which-ai-skills-are-most-in-demand) breaks this down by role.

### Why data skills keep growing

Every app, shop and service now produces data. People who can clean it, query it and explain what it means are needed in marketing, finance, health and operations, not only in tech.

## How to build in-demand skills in 2027

1. **Pair skills.** One tech skill plus one human skill makes you stand out. Data plus communication is a strong combination.
2. **Learn in small daily sessions.** Spaced practice helps memory more than cramming.
3. **Show your work.** Projects prove skills faster than certificates alone.
4. **Keep updating.** In-demand tools change, but the fundamentals, like logic, data and clear thinking, last.
