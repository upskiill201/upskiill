Projects teach more than tutorials.

## Direct answer

Good beginner machine learning projects use small, clean datasets and a clear question. Start with classification on the iris dataset, predict house prices with regression, build a spam filter and classify handwritten digits. Document your process and results clearly.

## 10 ideas

1. **Iris flower classifier** with a decision tree.
2. **House price prediction** with linear regression.
3. **Spam detector** using text features.
4. **Handwritten digit recognition.**
5. **Customer churn prediction.**
6. **Movie recommender** using similarity.
7. **Titanic survival prediction.**
8. **Sentiment analysis** of reviews.
9. **Customer segmentation** with clustering.
10. **Credit risk model,** with attention to fairness.

## How to do each

1. Define the question.
2. Explore and clean the data.
3. Split into training and test sets.
4. Build a simple baseline first.
5. Try one improvement at a time.
6. Evaluate with the right metrics.
7. Write up what you learned.
