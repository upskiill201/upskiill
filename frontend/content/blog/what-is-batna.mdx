Your power in a negotiation is mostly what you can do if you say no.

## Direct answer

BATNA is your best alternative if you do not reach an agreement. Knowing it tells you the lowest deal worth accepting and gives you confidence to walk away. Improve it by developing options before you negotiate, such as a second job offer or another supplier.

## How to work it out

1. List what you could do if talks fail.
2. Estimate the value of each.
3. Pick the best one. That is your BATNA.
4. Compare any offer against it.

## Example

You have a job offer at 50,000 and another at 55,000. Your BATNA for the first is 55,000, so you need a better offer than that to accept the first.

## Tips

- Think about the other side's BATNA too.
- Do not reveal a weak BATNA.
