Less stuff means less to buy, store, clean and replace.

## Direct answer

Minimalism is keeping what adds value and letting go of what does not. Owning less usually means spending less: fewer impulse buys, fewer things to maintain and a clearer sense of what you really need. Start small and do not replace one habit with another.

## How to start

1. **Pick one area,** such as your wardrobe.
2. **Remove what you do not use** and sell or donate it.
3. **Add a rule:** one in, one out.
4. **Pause before buying** to ask whether it replaces something or fills a gap.
5. **Reinvest the savings** in goals.

## Watch out

- Buying new organisers and "minimalist" products can become another spending habit.
- Do not throw away items you will need to repurchase soon.
