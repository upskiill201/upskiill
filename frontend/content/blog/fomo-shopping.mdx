"Only 2 left!" is designed to make you stop thinking.

## Direct answer

FOMO shopping means buying because you are afraid of missing out on a sale, a trend or something your friends have. Countdown timers, low stock warnings and social media posts create urgency. Slow down, check whether the deal repeats, and decide using your budget, not the clock.

## Common triggers

- Flash sales and countdown timers
- "Low stock" notices
- Influencer posts and unboxings
- Friends talking about a trend

## How to say no

1. **Pause 24 hours.** Real needs survive a day.
2. **Ask three questions:** Do I need it? Is it in my budget? Would I buy it at full price?
3. **Check the price history** or whether the offer returns.
4. **Unfollow or mute** accounts that make you want things.
5. **Celebrate skipping it.** Move the money to savings.

## Reframe

You are not missing out. You are choosing what to spend your money on.
