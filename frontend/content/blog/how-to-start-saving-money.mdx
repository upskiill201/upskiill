The hardest part of saving is starting. These steps make it simple.

## Direct answer

To start saving, learn what you spend, set one clear goal with a date, open a separate savings account, automate a small transfer on payday and increase it when you can. Begin with an amount you can sustain. The habit matters more than the amount at the start.

## Step by step

1. **See your numbers.** One month of spending. See [how to budget for beginners](/blog/how-to-budget-for-beginners).
2. **Pick a goal.** "$500 buffer by March."
3. **Open a separate account** so it's not mixed with spending.
4. **Automate.** A transfer on payday.
5. **Start small, then increase** by a little every few months.
6. **Protect it.** Treat it like a bill.
7. **Celebrate milestones.**

## What to save for first

| Order | Goal |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | A small buffer. See [emergency fund for beginners](/blog/emergency-fund-for-beginners) |
| 2 | Paying down expensive debt |
