The interview is the same. The setting is different.

## Direct answer

For a video interview, test your camera, microphone and internet, set up good lighting, choose a tidy background, dress professionally, look at the camera and keep notes nearby. Have a backup plan if your connection fails.

## Checklist

- [ ] Test the link and software.
- [ ] Light your face from the front.
- [ ] Use a plain background.
- [ ] Wear headphones if helpful.
- [ ] Close other apps.
- [ ] Have your resume and notes.
- [ ] Join five minutes early.

## During

Speak clearly, smile, avoid distractions and nod while listening.
