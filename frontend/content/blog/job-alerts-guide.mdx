Let the jobs come to you.

## Direct answer

Create alerts on job boards and employer career pages for your target titles, skills and locations. Check them daily, apply quickly with a tailored application and keep refining your keywords.

## Steps

1. List 5 to 8 target job titles.
2. Add key skills and locations.
3. Set daily or weekly emails.
4. Act on new matches within a day or two.
5. Adjust keywords monthly.
