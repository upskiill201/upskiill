The best way to **save money in 2027** is to make saving automatic and spending a little harder. You don't need a big income or a complicated spreadsheet. You need a simple budget, a few habits and a way to stick with them. This guide covers 15 that work, and you can practise them in Teyro's [Budgeting for Beginners](/courses/budgeting-for-beginners) course.

## Direct answer

To save money in 2027: **automate a transfer on payday**, **build a simple budget**, **keep an emergency fund**, **cut subscriptions you don't use**, **wait before buying**, and **review your spending every week**. Start small. Saving a little every month beats a big plan you abandon in February.

## Build the foundation

### 1. Know where your money goes

Track every expense for 30 days. Most people are surprised by where the money actually goes.

### 2. Use a simple budget

The 50/30/20 rule is a good start: about 50% of take-home pay for needs, 30% for wants and 20% for savings and debt. Adjust the split to your life. Read our guide to the [50/30/20 rule](/blog/50-30-20-rule-budget).

### 3. Pay yourself first

Set up an automatic transfer to savings on payday. What you don't see, you don't spend.

### 4. Build an emergency fund

Aim for a small starter fund first, then build toward a few months of essential costs. It stops surprises from turning into debt.

## Spend less without feeling poor

### 5. Cancel unused subscriptions

List every recurring payment and cancel anything you haven't used in a month.

### 6. Use a waiting rule

Wait 24 to 48 hours before any unplanned purchase. Many urges pass. [How to Stop Impulse Buying](/courses/how-to-stop-impulse-buying) teaches more tricks like this.

### 7. Separate needs from wants

Ask: do I need this, or do I want it right now? [Needs vs. Wants](/courses/needs-vs-wants-making-better-spending-decisions) makes this decision faster.

### 8. Plan meals

Food is often the easiest budget line to cut. Plan the week and shop with a list.

### 9. Remove saved cards

Making checkout slower makes impulse buys rarer.

### 10. Unsubscribe from sale emails

Fewer reminders of what you could buy means fewer purchases.

## Grow what you save

### 11. Pay down expensive debt

High-interest debt usually costs more than savings earn. Many people pay it down before investing.

### 12. Learn the basics of risk

Before investing, understand what can go wrong. [How to Understand Investment Risk](/courses/how-to-understand-investment-risk) covers the basics.

### 13. Watch out for scams

"Guaranteed returns" and urgent requests for money are warning signs. See [How Scams and Financial Fraud Work](/courses/how-scams-and-financial-fraud-work).

### 14. Increase your income

Saving has a limit. Earning more doesn't. A new skill or a [side hustle](/blog/side-hustle-ideas-2027) can make the biggest difference.

### 15. Review every week

Spend ten minutes each week checking your budget. Small corrections stop small problems from growing.

## Make it a 2027 goal you keep

Pick two or three habits from this list, not all fifteen. Attach them to a fixed day, track your progress and review monthly. See [goals for 2027](/blog/goals-for-2027) for a simple system.
