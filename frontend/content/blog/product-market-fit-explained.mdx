When customers pull the product from you.

## Direct answer

Product-market fit is when a product clearly satisfies a market need, shown by retention, referrals and customers who would be very disappointed if it disappeared. Without it, focus on learning from customers and refining your product before scaling.

## Signs

- Customers return repeatedly.
- People tell others unprompted.
- Sales do not need heavy persuasion.

## A simple survey

Ask users: "How would you feel if you could no longer use this?" Many teams look for a large share saying "very disappointed".
