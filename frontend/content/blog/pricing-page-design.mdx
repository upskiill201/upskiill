A pricing page should answer "What do I get and what does it cost?" in seconds.

## Direct answer

A good pricing page shows plan names and prices clearly, highlights a recommended plan, lists what is included in simple terms, answers common questions and has an obvious call to action. Keep it honest and easy to compare.

## 10 tips

1. Limit to three or four plans.
2. Use clear names.
3. Highlight one recommended plan.
4. Show monthly and yearly options honestly.
5. List key features, not every feature.
6. Add FAQs.
7. Show a guarantee or trial if you offer one.
8. Make the button clear.
9. Use simple language.
10. Test and improve.
