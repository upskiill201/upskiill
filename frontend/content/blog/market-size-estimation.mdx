Is the market big enough?

## Direct answer

Estimate market size with a bottom-up method: count the customers you can reach, multiply by what they would pay per year and sanity-check with top-down data. Use real numbers, not wishful percentages.

## Example (bottom-up)

- 5,000 small bakeries in your region.
- You can reach 20 percent in two years: 1,000.
- Price: 300 per year.
- Revenue potential: 300,000 per year.

## Steps

1. Define the customer precisely.
2. Count how many exist.
3. Estimate who you can reach.
4. Multiply by price.
5. Compare with top-down data.
