The shape of your question decides the shape of the answer.

## Direct answer

Open-ended questions start with words like what, how, why or tell me about, and invite a fuller answer. Use them to start and deepen conversations. Follow up with "What was that like?" or "Say more".

## Closed vs open

| Closed | Open |
| :--- | :--- |
| Did you have a good weekend? | What was the best part of your weekend? |
| Do you like your job? | What do you enjoy most about your work? |
| Is it busy? | What has been keeping you busy lately? |

## Tips

1. Ask one question at a time.
2. Pause and listen.
3. Follow up on what they said.
4. Avoid "why" when it may sound accusatory. Try "what led to...".
