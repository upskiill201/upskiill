## Direct answer

Grammar checkers and AI writing assistants catch spelling, grammar and clarity problems and suggest rewrites. Use them as a second pair of eyes, review every suggestion and keep confidential text out of unapproved tools. Learn from the corrections to improve your own writing.

## What they do well

- Typos and basic grammar.
- Consistency of tone.
- Shortening wordy sentences.

## Cautions

- Suggestions can change your meaning.
- Tone suggestions can flatten your voice.
- Privacy: check policies.

## Best practice

1. Write first.
2. Run the tool.
3. Accept only what you agree with.
4. Re-read the final version.
