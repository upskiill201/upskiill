## 2. Stealth-Mode Productivity: The Power of Quiet Mastery

One of the biggest psychological traps for ambitious learners is **premature social validation**. When you announce on social media that you are "learning to code" or "launching a startup," your brain receives cheap dopamine before you have written a single line of code.

### The Stealth Mastery Formula:
1. **Work in the Dark:** Spend 30 to 60 days practicing your craft in total silence.
2. **Build Proof of Work:** Create tangible GitHub projects or designs before showing anyone.
3. **Emerge with Results:** Let your completed projects make the noise for you.
