First impressions shape how quickly people become productive.

## Direct answer

Good onboarding covers logistics before day one, a welcoming first week with clear expectations, introductions and a buddy, and regular check-ins through the first 90 days. Share goals, tools and how success is measured.

## Checklist

**Before day one**
- Accounts, equipment and access ready.
- Welcome message and schedule.

**Day one**
- Welcome, tour and introductions.
- Assign a buddy.
- Explain the team's goals.

**First week**
- Role expectations and first small tasks.
- Training and tools.
- A short check-in each day.

**First month**
- A 30-60-90 plan.
- Feedback conversation.
- Meet key stakeholders.
