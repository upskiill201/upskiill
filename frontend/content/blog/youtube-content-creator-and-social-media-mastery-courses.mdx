```
  │                    │                    │
  ▼                    ▼                    ▼
[Stage 4: Multi-Stream Monetization] ───> AdSense + Sponsorships + Digital Course / SaaS
```

### Stage 1: Design Packaging BEFORE Scripting
Never spend 20 hours writing and filming a video without knowing how you will package it. Professional creators design the **Thumbnail and Title first**. If you cannot create a thumbnail concept that creates intense curiosity, the video will not get clicked regardless of production value.

### Stage 2: The 5-Second Visual Stakes Hook
The first 5 seconds determine 80% of a video's success:
- Never begin with animated logos, channel intros, or personal greetings.
- Immediately confirm the promise of the thumbnail and title within the first sentence.
- Show physical motion or introduce an unexpected visual element.
