## What Does Unacademy's English Speaking Course Actually Cover?

- **Pronunciation guidance** — accent and clarity-focused lessons

The content is generally well-structured and taught by dedicated educators, which is a real strength over unmoderated free YouTube content. The gap is the same one affecting almost every English-learning video course: **watching someone speak English is not the same as practicing speaking it yourself.**

## The Core Problem: Passive Video Doesn't Build Speaking Fluency

This is the most important thing to understand before enrolling in any spoken English course, Unacademy included: **fluency is a motor and recall skill, not just a knowledge skill.**

You can understand every grammar rule perfectly and still freeze mid-sentence in a real conversation, because speaking fluently requires practiced retrieval under time pressure — not just passive recognition of correct grammar. Video lectures teach the "what." Only active practice — speaking out loud, repeatedly, in short structured bursts — builds the "how."

This is backed by second-language acquisition research: **active production practice** (speaking, writing) produces measurably stronger retention and fluency gains than passive input (listening, reading) alone.
