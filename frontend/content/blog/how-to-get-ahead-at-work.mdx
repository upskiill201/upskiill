Getting ahead is not about working the longest hours. It is about being useful, visible and growing in the right direction.

## Direct answer

To get ahead at work, deliver reliably, learn what your manager and organisation need most, solve problems beyond your job description, build good relationships, and make your results visible. Plan the next step, ask for feedback and learn skills that matter for the role you want.

## Core habits

### Be reliable
Do what you say, on time, and tell people early when plans change.

### Understand priorities
Ask your manager: "What would make the biggest difference this quarter?"

### Solve problems, not just tasks
Notice recurring issues and suggest fixes with a plan.

### Build relationships
Colleagues, people in other teams, and people above you. Be helpful and curious.

### Make results visible
Share short updates, keep a win list and say thank you in public.

### Keep learning
Pick one skill each quarter. Using AI tools well is now one of them; see [how to use AI at work](/blog/how-to-use-ai-at-work).

## Your first 90 days

1. Listen and learn the work and the people.
2. Ask what good looks like.
3. Deliver a small early win.
4. Agree goals with your manager.

## Moving up

When you're ready, see [how to ask for a promotion](/blog/how-to-ask-for-a-promotion) and [career growth tips](/blog/career-growth-tips).

## Mistakes

- Waiting to be noticed
- Taking on everything without priorities
- Ignoring feedback
- Complaining instead of proposing
