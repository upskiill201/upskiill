A model that memorises the textbook may fail the exam.

## Direct answer

Overfitting is when a machine learning model fits the training data too closely, including its noise, and so performs badly on new data. You spot it when training accuracy is high but test accuracy is much lower. Reduce it with more data, simpler models, regularisation and cross-validation.

## A picture

Imagine fitting a curve through 10 points. A wiggly curve that hits every point is overfitted. A smooth curve that follows the trend generalises better.

## Signs

- High training score, low test score.
- Performance gets worse on new data as the model grows more complex.

## Fixes

1. **Get more data.**
2. **Simplify the model.**
3. **Regularise** to penalise complexity.
4. **Use cross-validation.**
5. **Stop training early.**
6. **Remove noisy features.**

## Underfitting is the opposite

A model too simple to capture the pattern. Both fail to generalise.
