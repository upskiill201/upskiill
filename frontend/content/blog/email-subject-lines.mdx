The subject is the first, and sometimes only, thing read.

## Direct answer

A good subject line is specific, short and action-oriented. Put key information first and say if action is needed and by when. Avoid vague words like "Hi" or "Question" and avoid spammy phrases.

## Formulas

- **Action + deadline:** "Approve budget by Friday"
- **Topic + detail:** "Project Atlas: launch date moved to 14 May"
- **Question:** "Can you attend the 3pm review?"
- **FYI:** "FYI: new expense policy"

## Before and after

| Before | After |
| :--- | :--- |

| Meeting | Moving Monday's meeting to 3pm |
| Request | Need your sign-off on invoice 1042 by Thu |
