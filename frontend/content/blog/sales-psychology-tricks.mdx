Shops are designed so you buy more than you planned.

## Direct answer

Retailers use predictable psychology to influence what you buy. Examples are anchoring (a high "was" price), scarcity ("only 2 left"), decoy pricing, charm pricing like 9.99, free shipping thresholds and store layouts that route you past tempting items. Knowing them helps you resist.

## 8 common tricks

1. **Anchoring:** a high "original" price makes the sale price look great.
2. **Scarcity:** low stock or countdowns create urgency.
3. **Decoy pricing:** a middle option is priced to make a bigger one look like a deal.
4. **Charm pricing:** 9.99 feels cheaper than 10.
5. **Free shipping thresholds:** you add items to qualify.
6. **Bundles:** you buy more to "save".
7. **Store layout:** essentials at the back, treats near the till.
8. **Social proof:** "most popular" or "people also bought".

## How to stay in control

- Make a list and a budget.
- Compare unit prices.
- Look for the real price history.
- Walk away and decide later.
