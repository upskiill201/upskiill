## The 5 Core Modules of a Practical E-Accounting Syllabus

A truly job-ready computerized accounting curriculum must bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world tax compliance:

```
[The 5-Module E-Accounting & Taxation Mastery Pipeline]
                       │
  ┌────────────────────┼────────────────────┐
  │                    │                    │
  ▼                    ▼                    ▼
[Module 1: Bookkeeping] [Module 2: GST Practical] [Module 3: TDS & Payroll]
  • Chart of Accounts    • GSTR-1 (Outward Sales)  • Section 194C, 194J, 194I
  • Voucher Entry        • GSTR-3B (Tax Payment)   • Form 24Q & 26Q Filing
  • Bank Reconciliation  • Input Tax Credit (2B)   • PF & ESI Statutory Deductions
  │                    │                    │
  ▼                    ▼                    ▼
[Module 4: ITR Filing] [Module 5: Advanced Excel & Reporting]
  • ITR-1 (Salaried)     • VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP & Pivot Tables
  • ITR-4 (Presumptive)  • Cash Flow & Profit & Loss Statement
  • AIS / 26AS Match     • Financial Ratio Analysis & MIS Reports
```

### 1. Computerized Bookkeeping (TallyPrime & Cloud ERP)
Master chart of accounts creation, purchase/sales invoicing, inventory batch tracking, debit/credit notes, and automated **Bank Reconciliation Statements (BRS)** to ensure bank balances match accounting ledgers down to the cent.

### 2. Practical GST Return Filing
- **GSTR-1:** Uploading B2B (business-to-business) and B2C sales invoice summaries.
- **GSTR-2B Reconciliation:** Matching supplier invoices with Input Tax Credit (ITC) to prevent fraudulent tax deductions.
- **GSTR-3B:** Calculating net tax liability after offsetting eligible ITC and executing tax payments through online challans.

### 3. Income Tax Return (ITR) Filing & Annual Information Statement (AIS)
Learn to analyze Form 26AS, AIS (Annual Information Statement), and TIS (Taxpayer Information Summary) to verify dividend income, stock capital gains, and salary TDS before submitting ITR-1, ITR-2, and ITR-4 filings.
