Landlords often prefer a good tenant to a vacancy.

## Direct answer

To negotiate rent, research comparable listings, highlight your track record as a tenant, ask early, propose a specific figure and offer something in return such as a longer lease. If they say no, ask for other benefits.

## Script

"I have enjoyed living here and always paid on time. I have seen similar places listed at X. Would you consider X for a 12-month renewal?"

## Steps

1. Research comparable rents.
2. Time it well, before the renewal date.
3. Make your offer in writing.
4. Offer value: longer lease, on-time payment.
5. Get agreements in writing.
