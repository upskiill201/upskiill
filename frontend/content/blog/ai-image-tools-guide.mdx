## Direct answer

AI image tools generate or edit pictures from text descriptions. Describe the subject, style, lighting and composition, then refine. Check each tool's licence and terms for commercial use, avoid imitating living artists' work or private individuals, and be clear when an image is AI-generated where it matters.

## Prompt recipe

"A cosy home office at golden hour, wide shot, soft natural light, watercolour style, no text."

## Uses

- Mock-ups and concept art.
- Social graphics.
- Presentation visuals.
- Removing backgrounds and clean-ups.

## Things to consider

- **Licence:** read the terms.
- **Accuracy:** AI images can contain odd details and wrong text.
- **Ethics:** avoid misleading images of real people or events.
- **Disclosure:** label AI-generated content where appropriate.
