People rarely act on vague hints.

## Direct answer

A call to action is a clear, specific request that follows your pitch or message, such as "Could we book 20 minutes on Thursday?". Make it easy, specific and time-bound.

## Examples

| Weak | Strong |
| :--- | :--- |
| Let me know. | Could you reply by Friday with your preferred date? |
| Maybe we can talk. | Could we have a 15-minute call on Tuesday at 10? |
| Check it out. | Try the demo at the link and tell me what you think. |

## Tips

1. One ask per message.
2. Make it easy: link, time, one click.
3. Say why it benefits them.
