## 1. The #1 Rule of Self-Defense: Situational Awareness & De-Escalation

The most skilled fighters know that the best fight is the one that never happens:

1. **Environmental Scanning:** Always be aware of exits, body language, and potential hazards in unfamiliar surroundings.
2. **Verbal De-escalation:** Maintain calm, assertive posture with open palms at chest level (the "interview stance") to create a natural defensive guard.
3. **Run When Possible:** Self-defense is about surviving and getting home safely, not proving ego.

---

## 2. Solo Home Drills to Build Muscle Memory

While partner sparring is essential, you can build vital movement mechanics at home:

* **Shadowboxing (3 Rounds of 3 Mins):** Practice stance transitions, 1-2 punch combinations (Jab, Cross), and slipping punches in front of a mirror.
* **Footwork Line Drills:** Move forward, backward, and lateral without crossing your feet or compromising your center of gravity.
* **Jump Rope Conditioning:** 10 minutes of jump rope builds the calf endurance and rhythm required for dynamic footwork.
