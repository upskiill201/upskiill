Growth happens just outside your comfort zone.

## Direct answer

A stretch assignment is a project that stretches your skills. Ask for one by linking it to a team need and your development goals, agree support and checkpoints and share results. Choose projects with visibility and learning.

## How to get one

1. Identify skills to build.
2. Spot a team problem you could help with.
3. Propose it to your manager.
4. Agree support and success measures.
5. Report results.

## Examples

- Leading a small project.
- Presenting to leaders.
- Training a new colleague.
