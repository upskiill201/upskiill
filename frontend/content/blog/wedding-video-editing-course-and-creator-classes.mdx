## The 5-Stage Wedding Video Editing Workflow

High-end wedding films do not follow strict chronological order; they are structured like emotional mini-movies built around vows, toasts, and letters. Follow this five-stage editing pipeline:

```
[500GB Multi-Camera Card Dumps]
              │
              ▼
[Stage 1: Multi-Cam Sync & Culling] ───> Timecode sync Ceremony, Speeches & Audio
              │
              ▼
[Stage 2: Audio Spine Assembly]    ───> Select poignant vow & toast soundbites
              │
              ▼
[Stage 3: B-Roll Emotional Montage] ──> Layer bridal prep, first look & drone shots
              │
              ▼
[Stage 4: Color Grading & Skin Tones] ─> Apply romantic warm film LUTs & balance
              │
              ▼
[Stage 5: Final Master Export]      ───> 4K ProRes & Web-Optimized Client USB
```

### Stage 1: Audio-First Narrative Architecture
Never start by laying down video clips. Start with spoken audio:
1. Listen through the ceremony vows, groom's letter reading, and reception speeches.
2. Cut out the most emotionally resonant 3 to 5 soundbites (e.g., *"From the moment I met you in college..."*).
3. Place these speech snippets across your audio timeline as the narrative anchor points.

### Stage 2: Music Selection and Beat Mapping
License two complementary tracks: an acoustic, emotional ballad for the vows and build-up, and an energetic indie pop/rock track for the reception celebration. Drop timeline markers on the major snare hits and string swells.

### Stage 3: Visual Pacing & B-Roll Weaving
Weave your visual footage over the audio anchor points:
- During the emotional vow audio, cut to intimate morning preparations, first looks, and parent reactions.
- During high-energy reception beats, utilize smooth speed ramps and dynamic dance floor clips.

## Screenplay & Scriptwriting Frameworks for Modern Video Creators

Whether writing a 10-minute YouTube video essay or a cinematic short film, professional storytelling relies on the timeless **Three-Act Structure**:

```
The Universal 3-Act Creator Story Arc:
Tension / Stakes
   ▲                                              [Climax: The Big Reveal]
   │                                                         ▲
   │                                     [Act II: Friction] / \
   │                    [Inciting Incident]     /──────────/   \
   │ [Act I: The Hook]        /────────────────/                \── [Act III: Resolution]
   └─────────────────────────/──────────────────────────────────────────────── Timeline
```

### 1. Act I: The Irresistible Hook (First 10–15% of Script)
- **The Core Problem:** Establish the central stakes immediately (e.g., *"Why 90% of online courses fail—and the psychology behind the 10% that work"*).
- **The Inciting Question:** Introduce the burning question that the viewer must stay until the end to discover.

### 2. Act II: The Obstacle and Exploration (60–70% of Script)
- Break the thesis down into 3 escalating proof points or narrative challenges.
- Introduce counter-arguments, surprising data, or dramatic real-world case studies.

### 3. Act III: The Resolution and Actionable Takeaway (15–20% of Script)
- Deliver the climactic insight that ties the whole narrative together, concluding with a clear call-to-action.
