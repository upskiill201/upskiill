Frugal living is not about misery. It is about getting more of what you value from the money you have.

## Direct answer

Frugal living means spending intentionally: paying for things that matter to you and cutting waste on things that do not. Start by tracking spending, planning meals, lowering recurring bills, buying second-hand where it makes sense and waiting before non-essential purchases.

## 15 tips

1. Track every expense for 30 days.
2. Plan meals and shop with a list.
3. Cook at home more often.
4. Cancel subscriptions you do not use.
5. Compare prices for insurance, phone and internet yearly.
6. Buy second-hand for furniture, clothes and tools.
7. Repair before replacing.
8. Use the library for books, films and sometimes tools.
9. Wait 48 hours before non-essential purchases.
10. Use cash or a set limit for fun money.
11. Turn off lights and watch energy use.
12. Walk, cycle or share rides when practical.
13. Batch errands to save fuel.
14. Look for free local events.
15. Automate a small saving each payday.

## The trap to avoid

Saving 5 on something you do not need is not saving. It is spending. Frugal people compare value, not just price.
