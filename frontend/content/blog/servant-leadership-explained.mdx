The leader works for the team, not the other way round.

## Direct answer

Servant leadership means leading by serving: listening, removing obstacles, developing people and sharing credit. The leader's success is measured by how well the team does. It still involves clear goals and accountability.

## Principles

- Listen first.
- Show empathy.
- Build people's skills.
- Share power and credit.
- Remove obstacles.
- Be accountable and clear.

## Example behaviours

1. Ask "What is getting in your way?"
2. Take time to coach.
3. Speak up for the team to higher management.
4. Give credit publicly, take responsibility privately.

## Limits

In a crisis, quick decisions may be needed. Balance serving with clear direction.
