## 1. Mastering Google Sheets for Real-World Data

Spreadsheets are the universal engine of commerce. Applied digital skills teaches you to move beyond manual calculators:

* **Basic Formulas:** `=SUM()`, `=AVERAGE()`, `=COUNTIF()`, and percentage calculations.
* **Organizing Rows:** Learn [how to alphabetize in Excel & Sheets](/blog/how-to-alphabetize-in-excel).
* **Freezing Headers:** Learn [how to pin a row in Excel & Sheets](/blog/how-to-pin-a-row-in-excel).
* **Data Querying:** Take your spreadsheet logic further by exploring [SQL games for practice](/blog/sql-games).

---

## 2. Collaborative Document Design with Google Docs

Modern teams work asynchronously across continents. Understanding cloud collaboration prevents version confusion:

1. **Suggesting Mode & Comments:** Providing constructive peer review without overwriting primary copy.
2. **Version History & Restore:** Rolling back document changes safely.
3. **Structured Headings:** Using `Heading 1` and `Heading 2` for automatic table of contents generation.

## 3. Digital Citizenship and Internet Safety

Applied digital literacy includes responsible online engagement:

* **Evaluating Online Sources:** Spotting misinformation and validating author credentials (see [ATL skills guide](/blog/atl-skills-guide)).
* **Securing Google Accounts:** Setting up 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) and managing cloud folder sharing permissions (read [Security+ certification prep](/blog/security-plus-certification)).
