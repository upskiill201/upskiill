## How to Verify Legitimacy of Any Learning App Yourself

This is the part that matters more than anything we say about ourselves — a framework you can apply to Teyro or any other app you're evaluating:

**1. Does it have a working free tier or trial?** A legitimate product with real confidence in what it offers usually lets you try before you pay. Be wary of anything that demands payment upfront with no way to sample the actual experience first.

**2. Is pricing transparent and easy to find?** Hidden or hard-to-find pricing, or pricing that only appears after you've entered payment details, is a red flag across any product category, not just learning apps.

**3. Is there real information about who's building it?** A findable team, company information, and a real support channel matter more than slick marketing copy. Anonymous operators with no accountability trail are worth extra scrutiny.

**4. What do independent reviews say, not just testimonials on the site?** Testimonials curated by the company are marketing, not evidence. Look for reviews on independent platforms, app stores, or genuine social discussion.

**5. Does a real 10-minute test match the marketing claims?** This is the single best test for any learning app. Sign up, do an actual lesson, and honestly assess whether what you experienced matches what the landing page promised. If there's a gap, that tells you what you need to know regardless of anything else.
