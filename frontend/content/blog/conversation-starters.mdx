The first line is easier with a few ready examples.

## Direct answer

Good conversation starters are open questions or comments about the shared situation: the event, the setting, the work or a recent experience. Listen to the answer and follow up with curiosity.

## At work

1. What are you working on at the moment?
2. How did you end up in this team?
3. What is the best thing you have read or watched lately?
4. What has been the highlight of your week?

## At events

5. What brought you here?
6. Have you been to one of these before?
7. What did you think of that talk?

## Anywhere

8. Any good recommendations for [food, shows, travel]?
9. What are you looking forward to this month?
10. What do you do to unwind?

## Keep it going

- Follow up with "What was that like?"
- Share something small about yourself.
- Remember names and details.
