Good persuasion helps people say yes for their own reasons.

## Direct answer

Persuade ethically by understanding your audience's goals, explaining how your idea helps them, backing it with evidence and a short story, offering clear options and making a specific ask. Be honest about trade-offs and respect their decision.

## A simple structure

1. **Listen** to their concerns first.
2. **Link** your idea to their goals.
3. **Show evidence:** data, examples, customer stories.
4. **Offer options** and a recommendation.
5. **Make a clear ask.**
6. **Handle objections** with curiosity.

## Example

"You mentioned customers wait too long for answers. A shared inbox could cut that by half. Could we try it for two weeks?"
