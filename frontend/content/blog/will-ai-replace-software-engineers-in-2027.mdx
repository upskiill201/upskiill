**Will AI replace software engineers in 2027?** No, but it is changing what they do. AI tools now write a lot of routine code, so the valuable part of the job is moving toward deciding what to build, designing systems and checking what AI produces. The same pattern is showing up in many other jobs. Learning to work with AI, for example with Teyro's [Working with AI Coding Agents](/courses/working-with-ai-coding-agents), is the best protection.

## Direct answer

AI will not replace software engineers in 2027, but engineers who use AI well will have an edge over those who don't. US Bureau of Labor Statistics projections still show software developer jobs growing about 16% from 2024 to 2034. What changes is the work: less typing code, more designing, reviewing, testing and owning results.

## What the data says about AI and jobs

- **World Economic Forum, *Future of Jobs Report 2025*:** employers expect about 170 million jobs to be created and about 92 million displaced by 2030, a net gain of about 78 million. The report expects about 22% of jobs to be disrupted in that period.
- **US Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2024 to 2034 projections:** software developers (about 16%), information security analysts (about 29%) and data scientists (about 34%) are all projected to grow faster than average.

Neither source says AI will wipe out tech jobs. Both say the mix of tasks is shifting.

## What AI is good at in software

- Writing boilerplate and repetitive code
- Explaining unfamiliar code
- Drafting tests and documentation
- Turning a clear spec into a first draft

## What AI still struggles with

- Understanding what the business actually needs
- Designing systems that stay reliable as they grow
- Debugging problems that span many parts of a system
- Security, privacy and the consequences of mistakes
- Taking responsibility when something breaks

These are the skills that make an engineer valuable in 2027.

## What jobs will AI replace by 2027?

AI replaces **tasks** faster than **jobs**. Here is how different kinds of work are being affected:

| Type of work | How AI affects it |
| :--- | :--- |
| Data entry and routine processing | Heavily automated |
| Basic customer support | Partly automated, humans handle hard cases |
| First-draft writing and summaries | Faster, humans edit and decide |
| Routine reporting | Automated, humans explain and act |
| Software development | Changed: more design and review |
| Skilled trades and hands-on care | Least affected |
| Leadership, sales, negotiation | Least affected, AI supports |

For a longer list, read [what jobs will AI replace](/blog/what-jobs-will-ai-replace) and [skills that won't be replaced by AI](/blog/skills-that-wont-be-replaced-by-ai).

## How software engineers stay valuable in 2027

1. **Learn to direct coding agents.** Write clear tasks, set boundaries and review the output.
2. **Get strong at fundamentals.** Data structures, systems design and debugging matter more when AI writes the first draft.
3. **Own testing and quality.** Being the person who knows if the code is actually right is a lasting skill.
4. **Understand the business.** Engineers who can talk to customers and product teams decide what gets built.
5. **Communicate.** Explaining trade-offs clearly is now a core engineering skill.

## Should beginners still learn to code?

Yes. AI makes it easier to start, because you can get help instantly. But you still need to understand code to know when the AI is wrong.
