A readability tool is a mirror, not a boss.

## Direct answer

Readability tools such as Hemingway Editor flag long sentences, complex words, adverbs and passive voice, and estimate a reading grade. Use them to spot problems, then decide for yourself. Aim for clear sentences, not a perfect score.

## How to use it

1. Paste your draft.
2. Fix very hard sentences first.
3. Replace complex words where simpler ones work.
4. Review passive voice.
5. Keep technical terms if your reader needs them.

## What to ignore

- Suggestions that remove needed nuance.
- Adverbs that add meaning.
