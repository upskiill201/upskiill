Your personal details are valuable to criminals.

## Direct answer

Identity theft is when someone uses your personal information, such as your name, ID number or bank details, to commit fraud. It can happen through phishing, data breaches, stolen mail or oversharing online. Protect yourself with strong unique passwords, two-factor authentication and careful handling of personal documents.

## How it happens

- Phishing emails and texts
- Data breaches
- Stolen mail or documents
- Public Wi-Fi attacks
- Oversharing on social media

## Protect yourself

1. Use a password manager and unique passwords.
2. Enable two-factor authentication.
3. Shred sensitive documents.
4. Check bank statements and credit reports regularly.
5. Be careful about what you share online.
6. Freeze your credit if it is available where you live.

## If you are a victim

Contact your bank and lenders, change passwords, report to the police or fraud service and monitor your credit report.
