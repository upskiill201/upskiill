## 1. Why Adobe Premiere Pro is the Core NLE

While CapCut and DaVinci Resolve are gaining ground, MrBeast's team continues to rely on **Adobe Premiere Pro Productions**:

* **Collaborative Productions Feature:** Allows 5 to 10 editors to work on the exact same project simultaneously without overwriting project files.
* **Dynamic Link to After Effects:** Enables animators to update lower-thirds and visual graphics in real-time on the Premiere timeline without rendering intermediate video files.
