Investors back people and plans they believe in.

## Direct answer

A good investor pitch tells a clear story: the problem, your solution, why now, evidence it works, how you make money, who is on the team and what you need. Be honest about risks and ready for hard questions.

## Structure

1. Hook and problem.
2. Solution and demo.
3. Market and timing.
4. Traction.
5. Business model.
6. Team.
7. The ask and use of funds.

## Tips

- Practise out loud with feedback.
- Know your numbers.
- Admit what you do not know.
- Follow up quickly after meetings.
