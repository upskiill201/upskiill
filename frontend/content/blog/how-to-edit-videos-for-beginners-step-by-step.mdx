Video editing is the structured process of assembling, trimming, and refining raw video and audio clips into a finished visual narrative. The universal workflow follows six stages: organizing media, creating a rough cut, pacing fine edits, inserting graphics and image overlays, balancing audio levels, and exporting high-bitrate video.

## The 6 Essential Steps for Editing Any Video

Every professional editor—from Hollywood film suites to top YouTube creators—relies on a predictable multi-pass timeline structure. If you are wondering *video kaise edit karte hain* (how video editing is actually done), follow this standardized six-stage pipeline:

```
[Raw Footage & Assets]
        │
        ▼
[Step 1: Ingest & Tagging]  ───> Organize into Bins (A-Roll, B-Roll, SFX, Music)
        │
        ▼
[Step 2: Rough Assembly]   ───> Cut the narrative backbone; remove filler pauses
        │
        ▼
[Step 3: Visual Overlays]  ───> Add photos, PNG logos, lower thirds & B-roll
        │
        ▼
[Step 4: Audio Engineering] ───> Normalize dialogue to -12dB; duck music to -24dB
        │
        ▼
[Step 5: Color & Text]     ───> Apply color correction, titles, and light grading
        │
        ▼
[Step 6: Master Render]    ───> Export MP4/MOV with optimal bitrate for platform
```

### Step 1: Ingestion, Organization, and File Naming
Before touching the timeline, organize your media pool into dedicated bins:
- `01_A_ROLL`: Main speaking tracks, talking heads, or primary gameplay.
- `02_B_ROLL`: Cutaways, environment shots, and supporting b-roll clips.
- `03_GRAPHICS_IMAGES`: Logos, screenshots, still photos, and title cards.
- `04_AUDIO_MUSIC`: Voiceover stems, sound effects (SFX), and background music tracks.

Renaming clips with clear identifiers (e.g., `Scene1_Take3_Main` instead of `IMG_9482.MOV`) prevents timeline clutter and saves hours during complex cuts.

### Step 2: The Assembly Cut (Rough Cut)
Drop your primary A-roll onto **Video Track 1 (V1)** and **Audio Track 1 (A1)**. Play through the sequence using hotkeys (`J-K-L` for backward, pause, and fast-forward). Use the Razor/Split tool (`Cmd/Ctrl + B` or `C`) and **Ripple Delete** to remove stutters, false starts, and long pauses. Do not add music or visual effects during this stage—focus entirely on clear storytelling.

### Step 3: Layering Visual Overlays (B-Roll, Photos & Logos)
Once the narrative backbone is locked, introduce secondary elements on **Video Track 2 (V2)** and **Video Track 3 (V3)** to maintain visual momentum:
- Insert supporting b-roll over dialogue every 3 to 6 seconds to prevent viewer drop-off.
- Position screenshots, still pictures, and product callouts to illustrate complex concepts.

For advanced retention tactics, review our deep dive on [retention editing masterclass techniques](/blog/retention-editing-masterclass).

### Step 4: Audio Cleanup and Dialogue Balancing
Audio quality determines video retention more than resolution. Follow these industry-standard loudness benchmarks:
- **Dialogue / Voiceover:** Peaks between `-6 dB` and `-12 dB`.
- **Sound Effects (Whooshes, Pops):** Set between `-14 dB` and `-18 dB`.
- **Background Music:** Ducked under speech at `-22 dB` to `-28 dB`.

### Step 5: Color Correction and Titling
Color correction ensures visual consistency across different cameras and lighting setups:
1. **Balance Whites and Blacks:** Adjust lift, gamma, and gain so shadows sit at 0 IRE and highlights do not clip above 100 IRE.
2. **Skin Tone Uniformity:** Check vectorscopes to ensure human skin tones align with the 75-degree skin-tone line.
3. **Typography:** Add high-contrast titles using modern sans-serif fonts with subtle drop shadows or backing banners for readability on mobile screens.

### Step 6: Master Export Settings
When exporting your master file:
- **Format:** MP4 (MPEG-4) container for universal web compatibility.
- **Codec:** H.264 for standard uploads; H.265 (HEVC) or Apple ProRes for high-fidelity masters.
- **Bitrate:** 15–20 Mbps for 1080p at 30fps; 45–60 Mbps for 4K video editor exports.

## How to Add Photos, Images, and Logos to a Video

Adding still images, screen captures, and branding marks is essential for instructional videos, reaction formats, and professional social content.

```
Timeline Layering Architecture:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Track V3: [Transparent Logo Watermark (PNG)]  (Opacity 80%)
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Track V2: [Photo / B-Roll Image Callout]     (Transform: Scale 85%)
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Track V1: [Main A-Roll Speaker Footage]      (Base Layer)
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Method 1: How to Insert a Photo in a Video (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare the Image:** Use high-resolution PNG or JPEG files (minimum 1920x1080 resolution).
2. **Import Asset:** Drag the picture into your editor's project media pool.
3. **Place on Upper Track:** Place the photo directly on **Video Track 2 (V2)** above the primary footage on Track 1.
4. **Adjust Scale & Position:** Select the image clip on the timeline, open the **Transform / Inspector** window, and scale the image to fit your canvas (e.g., 70% scale positioned in the top-right corner for picture-in-picture mode).
5. **Apply Motion Transitions:** Add a 10-frame **Cross Dissolve** or **Smooth Push** transition at the in-point and out-point so the photo glides smoothly onto the screen rather than appearing abruptly.

### Method 2: Adding a Brand Logo and Watermark Overlay
Watermarks protect your intellectual property and build long-term brand recognition across TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram.

- **Logo Requirements:** Always use a 32-bit PNG file with an alpha channel (transparent background) or an SVG vector asset.
- **Positioning:** Place the logo in the upper-right or lower-right corner, respecting the **Action Safe Margin (10% inward from borders)** to avoid mobile interface UI buttons.
- **Opacity Settings:** Reduce layer opacity to `70% - 85%` so the watermark remains legible without obscuring central video details.
- **YouTube Watermark Specs:** For YouTube channel subscription buttons, upload a square `150x150 px` PNG under 1MB in YouTube Studio.

## How to Crop and Reframe Videos (Video Ko Crop Kaise Kare)

Cropping allows you to eliminate distracting frame edges, isolate focal subjects, or convert landscape clips into vertical 9:16 reels.

```
Landscape Source (16:9) ───[Smart Crop / Transform]───> Vertical Target (9:16)
┌───────────────────────┐                                 ┌─────────┐
│       ┌───────┐       │                                 │ ┌─────┐ │
│  BKG  │Subject│  BKG  │     =================>          │ │Subj │ │
│       └───────┘       │                                 │ └─────┘ │
└───────────────────────┘                                 └─────────┘
```

### 3 Ways to Crop Footage:
1. **Direct Canvas Cropping:** Change project timeline settings from `1920x1080 (16:9)` to `1080x1920 (9:16)`. Use the position slider to center the speaker.
2. **Edge Crop Effect (Crop Left / Right / Top / Bottom):** Apply the "Crop" effect from your effects panel to snip unwanted background elements or create cinematic widescreen letterboxing.
3. **Mobile Frame Cropping:** When using mobile video crop APK apps or CapCut, select **Format -> 9:16**, tap **Crop**, and drag the bounding box corners over your subject.

## Common Video Editing Mistakes and How to Fix Them

1. **Jump Cuts Without B-Roll Masking:** When cutting out pauses in a single-camera shot, the speaker's head snaps noticeably. Mask jump cuts by inserting a 2-second B-roll clip, zooming in the frame by 10–15% (creating an artificial second camera angle), or adding a relevant photo overlay.
2. **Loud, Distracting Background Music:** Never let your music track overpower spoken dialogue. Apply a **sidechain audio compressor (ducking)** to automatically lower the music volume whenever human speech is detected.
3. **Overusing Cheesy 3D Transitions:** Avoid random star wipes, 3D cube rotations, and spiral fades. Stick to invisible straight cuts, J-cuts (audio leads video), L-cuts (video leads audio), and occasional smooth whip pans.
4. **Ignoring Safe Margins:** When editing for mobile feeds (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts), keep essential text, subtitles, and logos at least 150px away from the bottom and right edges to prevent UI button overlap.
