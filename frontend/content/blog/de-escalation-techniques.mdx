Calm is contagious, and so is anger.

## Direct answer

To de-escalate, keep your voice low and slow, listen without interrupting, acknowledge feelings, avoid blame, offer options and suggest a break if needed. Focus on what you can do next.

## Steps

1. **Breathe** and keep a steady tone.
2. **Let them speak.**
3. **Acknowledge:** "I can see this is frustrating."
4. **Do not argue** about details yet.
5. **Ask what would help.**
6. **Offer a next step** or a pause.

## Safety

If you feel unsafe, leave and get help.
