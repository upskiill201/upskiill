They are testing how you think, not what you know.

## Direct answer

In a case interview, clarify the problem, structure your approach, analyse each part with the data given and finish with a clear recommendation. Think aloud, ask good questions and summarise regularly.

## Steps

1. **Clarify:** objective, context, constraints.
2. **Structure:** outline how you will analyse.
3. **Analyse:** calculations and insights.
4. **Recommend:** a clear answer with reasons and risks.

## Practice

- Read case examples.
- Practise mental maths.
- Rehearse out loud.
