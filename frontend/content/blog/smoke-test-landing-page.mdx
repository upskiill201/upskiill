## Direct answer

A smoke test is a simple landing page that describes your idea and asks for an action, such as an email sign-up or pre-order. Drive targeted traffic, measure conversion and compare with a target. Be honest about what exists.

## Steps

1. Write a clear headline and benefit.
2. Add one call to action.
3. Set a target, such as 100 visitors and 10 sign-ups.
4. Send traffic from your target audience.
5. Review results and talk to sign-ups.

## Cautions

- Do not mislead people.
- Low conversion may mean a weak message, not a weak idea. Test variations.
