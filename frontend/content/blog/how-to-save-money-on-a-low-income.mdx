"Save 20% of your income" is easy advice when you earn a lot. Here's what works when you don't.

## Direct answer

To save money on a low income, track where your money goes, set a small goal you can reach, cut spending you won't miss, move a small amount to savings the day you're paid, and keep a little buffer for emergencies. Consistency matters more than the amount. Even small savings stop surprises becoming debt.

## 12 ways

1. **Track a month** of spending.
2. **Pick a small goal:** a £/$ amount and a date.
3. **Pay yourself first,** even a tiny amount on payday. Automate it.
4. **Cancel unused subscriptions.**
5. **Plan meals** and cook more. See [how to budget for groceries](/blog/how-to-budget-for-groceries).
6. **Compare bills** and ask for better deals.
7. **Use free or cheaper options** for entertainment and transport.
8. **Pause on non-essentials.** See [how to stop impulse buying](/blog/how-to-stop-impulse-buying).
9. **Sell what you don't use.**
10. **Check for support you qualify for** where you live.
11. **Earn extra** where possible, and save part of it.
12. **Review monthly** and celebrate progress.
