Show what you know, and people notice.

## Direct answer

Share useful content on a platform your target employers use: lessons from projects, short tutorials, case studies and thoughtful comments. Be consistent, professional and helpful, and link to your portfolio.

## Ideas

- A short post on what you learned this week.
- A walkthrough of a project.
- A summary of a useful article with your take.
- A comment that adds value to others' posts.

## Tips

1. Pick one platform.
2. Share what helps others.
3. Be genuine.
4. Keep client or employer information private.
