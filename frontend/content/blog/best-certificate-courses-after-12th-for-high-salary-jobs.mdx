## Deep Dive: AutoCAD Course Fees, Duration, and Specializations

Computer-Aided Design (CAD) drafting is indispensable across civil engineering, architecture, mechanical manufacturing, and electrical schematics:

```
[AutoCAD Specialization Matrix]
                       │
  ┌────────────────────┼────────────────────┐
  │                    │                    │
  ▼                    ▼                    ▼
[Mechanical CAD]       [Civil / Architectural] [Electrical CAD]
  • 2D Part Drawings     • Floor Plans & Elevations • Wiring Schematics
  • Isometric Projections• Structural Cross-Sections • Circuit Layouts & Panels
  • GD&T Tolerances      • Building Bye-laws         • PLC Wiring Diagrams
```

### AutoCAD Course Fees & Duration Details:
- **Duration:** 4 to 8 weeks (40 to 60 total training hours).
- **Fees:** $150 – $500 (or ₹6,000 – ₹20,000 in India) for standard 2D drafting; $600 – $1,200 for combined 2D + 3D SolidWorks/CATIA packages.
- **Key Modules:** Coordinate systems ($X, Y, Z$), Geometric dimensioning, Layer management, Block attributes, and Plotting to PDF scale.

## PCB Design & Hardware Electronics Certification

For students with a passion for hardware, robotics, and electrical engineering, **PCB Design (Printed Circuit Board)** certification offers rapid job placement in EV (electric vehicle) and IoT (Internet of Things) manufacturing:
- **Software:** KiCad (Open Source) or Altium Designer.
- **Skills:** Schematic capture, component footprint creation, trace routing, ground planes, and Gerber file generation.
