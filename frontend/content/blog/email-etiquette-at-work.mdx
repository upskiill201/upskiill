Email is where most workplace reputations are made quietly.

## Direct answer

Write a clear subject line, lead with your main point, keep it short, be polite, check names and attachments, avoid unnecessary reply-all and end with a clear next step. Re-read before sending.

## 12 rules

1. Write a specific subject line.
2. Greet the person by name.
3. Put the point in the first line.
4. Keep paragraphs short.
5. Use bullets for lists.
6. Be polite, not wordy.
7. Check tone, since writing can sound colder than intended.
8. Attach files you mention.
9. Use reply-all sparingly.
10. Do not write angry emails. Wait.
11. End with a clear ask and date.
12. Proofread.

## Before and after

**Before:** "Hi, any update?"
**After:** "Hi Sam, could you share the draft by Thursday 3pm so I can review it before Friday's meeting?"
