You might feel fine about risk and still be unable to afford it.

## Direct answer

Risk tolerance is how comfortable you feel with investment losses. Risk capacity is how much loss you could afford financially. A secure income, a long time horizon and a large emergency fund raise capacity. When the two differ, choose investments that fit the lower one.

## Factors that affect capacity

- Income stability
- Time horizon
- Emergency fund size
- Debts and dependants
- How soon you need the money

## A quick example

Someone who feels fearless about risk but has no emergency fund and big debts has low capacity. Someone with plenty of savings who panics at small losses has low tolerance.
