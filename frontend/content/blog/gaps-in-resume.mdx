A gap is a fact, not a flaw.

## Direct answer

Explain a gap honestly and briefly, then pivot to what you learned, what you did and why you are ready now. Do not apologise. Add relevant activities such as courses, volunteering or projects.

## Script

"I took time out to care for a family member. During that time I completed a project management course, and I am now ready to return to full-time work."

## Resume tips

1. Use years rather than months if it helps.
2. Include courses, freelance work or volunteering.
3. Keep it honest.
