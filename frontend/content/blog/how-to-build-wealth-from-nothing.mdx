*This is general education, not personal financial advice.*

Wealth isn't a salary. It's what you keep and grow over time. The route is plain, and not instant.

## Direct answer

To build wealth from nothing: spend less than you earn, pay yourself first by saving automatically, clear expensive debt, build an emergency fund, grow your income and invest steadily for the long term. Time and consistency do most of the work, so small habits started early matter more than large gestures later.

## The steps

### 1. Know where your money goes
Track a month. See [how to budget for beginners](/blog/how-to-budget-for-beginners).

### 2. Pay yourself first
Move savings out as soon as you're paid, even a small amount.

### 3. Build an emergency fund
So surprises don't become debt. See [emergency fund for beginners](/blog/emergency-fund-for-beginners).

### 4. Clear expensive debt
High-interest debt eats wealth faster than most investments grow. See [how to budget to pay off debt](/blog/how-to-budget-to-pay-off-debt).

### 5. Grow the gap
Raise your income through skills, while keeping spending steady. See [lifestyle creep](/blog/lifestyle-creep).

### 6. Let compounding work
Growth on growth rewards time. Learn the risks: [what is investment risk](/blog/what-is-investment-risk).

### 7. Stay the course
Avoid scams and get-rich-quick schemes. See [common financial scams](/blog/common-financial-scams).

## By decade

| Decade | Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| 20s | Start the habit, build skills, avoid lifestyle creep |
