A search without a plan becomes a mood.

## Direct answer

A good job search strategy defines target roles and employers, prepares a strong resume and profile, applies selectively with tailored materials, uses networking and tracks progress weekly. Set small targets and review what is working.

## A weekly plan

| Activity | Target |
| :--- | :--- |
| Tailored applications | 5 |
| Networking messages | 5 |
| Follow-ups | 3 |
| Skill practice | 2 sessions |
| Review and adjust | 30 minutes |

## Steps

1. **Define** target roles and skills.
2. **Update** your resume and profile.
3. **List employers** and watch their openings.
4. **Tailor each application.**
5. **Network** for referrals.
6. **Track** in a spreadsheet.
