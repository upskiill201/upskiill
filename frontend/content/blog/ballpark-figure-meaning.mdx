Close enough to decide, not exact enough to sign.

## Direct answer

A ballpark figure is a rough estimate. When you give one, say it is approximate, offer a range and state your assumptions so nobody treats it as a firm quote.

## Example

"As a ballpark, the project could cost between 8,000 and 12,000 depending on scope. I will confirm after we define requirements."

## Tips

1. Use a range.
2. State what it depends on.
3. Promise a firmer figure by a date.
