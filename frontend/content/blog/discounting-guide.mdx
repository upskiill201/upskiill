Once you discount, you teach customers to wait for it.

## Direct answer

Discount with a purpose: clearing stock, rewarding volume or an introductory offer. Set limits, ask for something in return and avoid constant sales that teach customers to wait. If someone asks for a discount, consider adding value or changing the package instead.

## Alternatives to cutting price

- Add a bonus.
- Bundle products.
- Offer a smaller package.
- Give a payment plan.

## Script

"I cannot reduce the price, but I can include X at no extra cost."
