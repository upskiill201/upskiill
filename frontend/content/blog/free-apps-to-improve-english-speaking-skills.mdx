## 2. The Power of Audio Shadowing on Mobile

The single most effective technique for building native English cadence is **Audio Shadowing**:

1. Listen to a 5-second sentence spoken by a native speaker on the app.
2. Repeat the phrase immediately aloud, matching the exact rhythm, stress, and pauses.
3. Compare your recorded waveform against the original to identify missed consonants or vowel shifts.
