You can't make people motivated, but you can remove what drains motivation and add what builds it.

## Direct answer

To motivate employees, first fix what demotivates them (unclear work, unfair pay, overload, poor tools), then build purpose, autonomy and mastery, recognise good work specifically and fairly, keep workloads healthy and be honest in hard weeks. Motivation grows when people feel trusted, able and valued.

## Step 1: Fix the basics

Unfair pay, unclear roles and chaos kill motivation faster than any speech can fix. Ask what's getting in the way.

## Step 2: Build the drivers

| Driver | What it means | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Purpose | Why the work matters | Share how customers benefit |
| Autonomy | Control over how they work | Agree the goal, let them choose the method |
| Mastery | Getting better at something | Stretch tasks, training, feedback |
| Recognition | Being noticed fairly | Specific thanks in public or private |
| Progress | Seeing movement | Visible milestones |

## Recognition that works

- Be specific: "Your handover note saved us two hours."
- Be timely.
- Be fair: don't reward only the loudest.

## Workload and wellbeing

Overwork burns people out. Protect time off and avoid constant urgency.

## Hard weeks

Be honest about what's happening, share the plan and thank people. See [how to be a good leader](/blog/how-to-be-a-good-leader).
