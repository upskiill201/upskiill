A good icebreaker warms up the room without embarrassing anyone.

## Direct answer

An icebreaker is a quick activity at the start of a meeting that helps people connect. Choose low-pressure questions or short games, give people the option to pass and keep it brief.

## Ideas

**Quick questions**
- What was the best thing you ate this week?
- What is one thing you are looking forward to?
- Which skill would you love to learn?

**Activities**
- Two truths and a lie.
- One-word check-in.
- Show and tell: an object on your desk.

**Remote**
- Chat-box answer to a fun question.
- Share a photo of your view.

## Tips

1. Match the activity to the group.
2. Respect privacy.
3. Join in yourself.
