Facts persuade the head. Stories reach the heart.

## Direct answer

A founder story explains why you care and why you can solve the problem. Use a simple arc: meet a person, show their problem, describe the turning point, introduce your solution and share the vision. Keep it short and true.

## Structure

1. **A person:** "Maria runs a bakery."
2. **A problem:** "She loses hours to manual orders."
3. **Turning point:** "I watched her miss a big order."
4. **Solution:** "So we built a simple ordering tool."
5. **Vision:** "Every small shop can sell easily online."

## Tips

- Use specific details.
- Keep it honest.
- Move quickly to what you built.
