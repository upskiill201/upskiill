Introduce yourself in a way people remember.

## Direct answer

A personal brand statement is one or two sentences that say who you help, what you do and the result you create. Use a formula such as "I help [audience] to [result] by [method]" and adapt it for LinkedIn, interviews and networking.

## Examples

- "I help small shops sell online by building simple, fast websites."
- "I am a data analyst who turns messy spreadsheets into clear decisions for operations teams."

## Steps

1. List your strengths and the problems you solve.
2. Choose your audience.
3. Write the formula.
4. Cut jargon.
5. Test it out loud.
