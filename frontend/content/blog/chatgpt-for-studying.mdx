ChatGPT works best for studying when you tell it exactly how to behave.

## Direct answer

To use ChatGPT for studying, set it up as a tutor (with instructions that make it quiz you and give hints), paste in your notes or a topic, and practise: explain, answer, get feedback and repeat. Check key facts with your textbook, and follow your school's rules about AI.

## A tutor-style instruction

Paste this at the start of a chat, or into custom instructions:

> Act as a patient tutor. Ask me one question at a time. Give hints before answers. Tell me what I got right and wrong. If you're not sure of a fact, say so.

## Prompt templates

| Goal | Prompt |
| :--- | :--- |
| Understand | "Explain [topic] simply, with an everyday example." |
| Test | "Ask me five questions on [topic]. Wait for my answer each time." |
| Feedback | "Here's my answer: [answer]. What's missing or wrong?" |
| Plan | "I have [days] before my exam on [subject]. Make a daily revision plan." |
| Flashcards | "Turn these notes into 15 question-and-answer flashcards: [notes]." |
| Summaries | "Summarise this chapter into 8 key points." |

## Exam practice

- Ask for exam-style questions, then mark your own answers against the AI's feedback.
- Ask it to explain mistakes.
- Repeat weak topics a few days later.

## Limits

- It can be wrong or invent sources.
- It may not match your syllabus.
- Over-using it can weaken your own recall.

## Honest use

Check what your school allows. Use AI to learn, not to submit its work as yours.
