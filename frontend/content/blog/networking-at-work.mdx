Opportunities travel through people.

## Direct answer

To network at work, schedule short chats with colleagues in other teams, join cross-team projects, offer help and follow up. Be curious, listen and share useful information.

## Steps

1. List teams you work with or want to understand.
2. Invite people for a 20-minute chat.
3. Ask about their goals and challenges.
4. Offer something useful.
5. Keep in touch.

## Script

"I would love to learn more about what your team does. Could we have a 20-minute coffee chat?"
