## Direct answer

A pig butchering scam is a long-running fraud in which a scammer builds trust with a victim, often through a wrong-number message or dating app, and then guides them to invest in a fake platform, usually involving crypto. The app shows fake profits, but withdrawals are blocked or require more fees.

## How it unfolds

1. **Contact:** a random message or friendly chat.
2. **Trust:** daily messages for weeks.
3. **The tip:** "my uncle taught me an investment trick".
4. **A small win:** you invest a little and can withdraw.
5. **A big investment:** you add more.
6. **The trap:** withdrawals fail, fees appear, the platform disappears.

## Red flags

- A stranger talking about investing.
- A platform you cannot find in regulator lists.
- Pressure to deposit more.
- Fees to withdraw.

## What to do

Stop sending money, keep records and report it to your bank, platform and authorities.
