Search for "AI coding jobs" and you will find big promises. Here is what is actually known, and what is just hype.

## Direct answer

"AI coder" is not an official job title, so there is no official salary for it. AI coding tools are becoming part of ordinary software jobs. For a real reference, US Bureau of Labor Statistics data for 2025 puts state median wages for software developers between about $95,000 in Mississippi and about $174,000 in California. Pay depends on the role, location and experience, not on which tool you use.

## What the jobs look like

- **Software developer who uses AI tools:** the most common case. The tools speed up the work, but you are still hired for engineering judgement.
- **Roles building with AI:** AI engineer or automation engineer jobs, where you connect AI models into products.
- **Freelance and small projects:** quick builds and prototypes, where clear briefs and review matter.
- **Non-developer roles using AI to build tools:** analysts, operations and product people who automate their own work.

See the [AI automation engineer career guide](/blog/ai-automation-engineer-career-guide) for one route.

## What employers want

1. **Core skills:** reading and writing code, debugging, testing.
2. **Judgement:** spotting when AI output is wrong or unsafe.
3. **Communication:** writing clear briefs and explaining changes.
4. **Proof:** projects you can show and talk through.

Knowing a tool's name is not enough. Being able to explain why a change can be trusted is.

## Be careful with big claims

Some listings and videos promise six-figure pay for knowing one AI tool. Check who is making the claim, what the job really involves, and whether the pay is guaranteed or just a sales line. The BLS figures above are the safer starting point.
