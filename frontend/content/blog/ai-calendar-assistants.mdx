Scheduling is a task AI is well suited for, with limits.

## Direct answer

AI calendar assistants can find meeting times, protect time for focused work, reschedule when plans change and send reminders. They save effort on logistics, but you still decide priorities and should review what they book. Check permissions and data policies before connecting your calendar.

## What they do

- Suggest meeting times across calendars.
- Reserve focus time.
- Move tasks when meetings overrun.
- Summarise your day or week.

## Where to be careful

- Over-automation can clutter your calendar.
- Access to sensitive meetings and contacts.
- Time zone and holiday mistakes.

## A simple routine

1. Decide your top three priorities.
2. Let the tool schedule them.
3. Review each morning.
