## 1. Conquering Executive Dysfunction Through Micro-Habits

When everything feels like a massive chore, the brain shuts down in freeze mode. The secret is reducing the cognitive threshold until action becomes frictionless:

* **The 2-Minute Rule:** If a task takes less than 2 minutes (taking out the trash, making your bed, replying to a confirmation email), do it immediately.
* **Anchor to Existing Habits:** Pair a new habit with an automatic one (e.g., *"After I brew coffee, I will complete 15 minutes of skill practice on Teyro"*).
* **Protecting Focus:** Learn how stealth discipline builds unstoppable momentum in [the skill of being unseen](/blog/the-skill-of-being-unseen).

## 2. Personal Financial Autonomy (The 50/30/20 Framework)

You do not need an accounting degree to manage your money:

1. **50% Needs:** Rent, groceries, utilities, basic transportation.
2. **30% Wants:** Entertainment, dining out, hobbies.
3. **20% Savings & Debt Payoff:** Emergency fund (3 months of expenses) and retirement index funds.
