AI is a fast first-draft partner. The facts are still your job.

## Direct answer

To write reports faster with AI, start with the purpose and audience, ask for an outline, supply your own facts and numbers, draft section by section, then edit for accuracy, tone and clarity. Never let AI invent data, and check every figure.

## Workflow

1. **Define:** purpose, audience, length, decision needed.
2. **Outline:** "Suggest an outline for a one-page progress report."
3. **Supply facts:** paste your numbers and notes.
4. **Draft:** one section at a time.
5. **Edit:** simplify, cut and add your voice.
6. **Check:** numbers, names, dates and claims.

## Prompt example

"Using only the facts below, write a 150-word summary for senior managers. If something is missing, say so. Facts: ..."
