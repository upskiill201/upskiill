Launching is not just clicking publish. A short checklist saves embarrassing mistakes.

## Direct answer

To launch a website, finish and test it, choose a host (free options exist for simple sites), upload your files, optionally connect a domain name, check it on a real phone and laptop, then submit it to Google Search Console and share it. Do the checks before announcing it.

## Pre-launch checklist

- Every link works
- Contact details are right
- Spelling checked
- Images have alt text and aren't huge
- Each page has a clear title
- It looks right on a phone. See [what is responsive web design](/blog/what-is-responsive-web-design)
- Forms send to the right place

## Hosting options

| Option | Good for | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| GitHub Pages | Simple static sites from a repository | Free, needs a GitHub account |
| Netlify or Vercel | Static and modern sites | Free tiers with limits |
| Website builder hosting | Builder-made sites | Included, upgrades cost |
| Paid hosting | Larger or custom sites | Monthly fee |

Free plans and limits change, so check each provider's current terms.

## A domain name

A domain like yourname.com costs a yearly fee from a registrar. You connect it to your host by changing settings the host explains. It's optional at first.

## Launch steps

1. Upload or connect your files to the host.
2. Open the live address and test again.
3. Connect a domain if you have one.
4. Check it on a phone, not just your computer.
5. Add it to Google Search Console to be found.
6. Share with friends and ask them to try it.

## After launch

- Fix what people report.
- Check it monthly.
- Add content that visitors want.
