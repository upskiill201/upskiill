It is the invitation to make your case.

## Direct answer

Answer by connecting your strongest skills to the employer's needs, backing them with a specific result and showing your motivation. Use a simple structure: need, proof, enthusiasm.

## Template

"You need someone who can [key requirement]. In my last role I [result]. I am excited about this role because [reason]."

## Example

"You need someone who can improve customer retention. In my last role I redesigned our onboarding emails and increased retention by 12 percent in six months. I would love to bring that approach to your team."
