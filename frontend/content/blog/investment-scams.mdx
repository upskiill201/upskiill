If it sounds too good to be true, it usually is.

## Direct answer

Investment scams promise high or guaranteed returns with little risk, often through unsolicited calls, messages or social media ads. Check whether the firm is authorised by your financial regulator, never feel rushed and never invest in something you do not understand.

## 8 warning signs

1. Guaranteed returns.
2. High returns with no risk.
3. Pressure to act now.
4. Cold calls, messages or ads from strangers.
5. A seller who is not registered.
6. Vague strategy or secret system.
7. Requests to pay in crypto or gift cards.
8. Difficulty withdrawing.

## How to check

- Search the regulator's register for the firm and product.
- Look up the contact details yourself.
- Read independent reviews and warnings.
- Ask a trusted adviser.
