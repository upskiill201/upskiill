Both collapse. They just fail in different ways.

## Direct answer

In a pyramid scheme, you pay to join and earn mainly by recruiting others, and money flows up through levels. In a Ponzi scheme, you invest and are paid fake "returns" from newer investors' money. Both fail when new money slows and both are illegal in many countries.

## Key differences

| | Pyramid scheme | Ponzi scheme |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Promise | Earn by recruiting | Earn by investing |
| Money flow | Up the levels | From new to old investors |
| Role of products | Often token | Often vague investments |

## Warning signs

- Pay to join or buy a starter kit.
- Income depends on recruiting.
- Pressure to bring in friends and family.
- Vague answers about real product sales.
