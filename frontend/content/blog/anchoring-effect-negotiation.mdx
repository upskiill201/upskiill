The first number casts a long shadow.

## Direct answer

Anchoring is the tendency to be influenced by the first number mentioned. In negotiation, a well-researched first offer can pull the final result in your favour. If the other side anchors first, resist by recalling your own research and proposing your own figure with reasons.

## Using it

1. Research a fair range.
2. Open at the ambitious end, with a reason.
3. Stay credible. Extreme numbers can backfire.

## Responding to an anchor

- "That is higher than I expected. Based on X, I was thinking Y."
- Ask how they arrived at the number.
