```

                 ▼
[Week 3: Speed Ramping & Color Harmony]   ───> Practice slow-mo curves & cinematic LUTs
                 │
                 ▼
[Week 4: Social Formats & Showreel Master] ───> Assemble 60-second client-ready showreel
```

### The 4-Week Schedule:
- **Week 1 (Days 1–7):** Download DaVinci Resolve or CapCut. Practice assembling 1-minute talking head videos, removing all breathing pauses and stutters.
- **Week 2 (Days 8–14):** Layer background music, sound effect risers, and graphic image callouts on upper video tracks.
- **Week 3 (Days 15–21):** Shoot 60fps smartphone b-roll and practice speed ramping, time lapses, and basic color correction curves.
- **Week 4 (Days 22–30):** Edit three sample client videos (1 YouTube vlog, 1 TikTok reel, 1 product promo) and host them on a free portfolio site.
