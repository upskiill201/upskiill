The first number you say can shape the offer.

## Direct answer

Answer with a researched range and stay open to discussion. For example: "Based on my research and experience, I am looking for X to Y. I am open to discussing the total package." If you lack information, ask about the budget for the role.

## Prepare

1. Research typical pay for the role and location.
2. Know your minimum.
3. Consider benefits.

## Scripts

- "What is the budgeted range for this role?"
- "Based on market data, I am looking at X to Y."
