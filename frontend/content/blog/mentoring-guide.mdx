One conversation with the right person can save years.

## Direct answer

A mentor is someone with more experience who shares advice and perspective. To find one, identify people whose path you admire, ask for a short conversation about something specific and build the relationship over time. To be a good mentor, listen more than you advise and encourage the mentee to find their own answers.

## For mentees

1. **Know what you want** help with.
2. **Make a clear, small ask:** "Could I ask you three questions about moving into product?"
3. **Prepare** and respect their time.
4. **Follow up** on what you tried.
5. **Say thank you** and share results.

## For mentors

1. Listen first.
2. Share stories, not orders.
3. Ask questions that help the mentee think.
4. Be honest and kind.
5. Respect confidentiality.
