A good compliment is specific and true.

## Direct answer

Give compliments that are specific, sincere and about choices or effort, such as "Your presentation was clear and well organised." Accept compliments with a simple thank you. Avoid comments on appearance in professional settings.

## Examples

| Vague | Specific |
| :--- | :--- |
| Nice job. | You explained the data so clearly that the whole room understood it. |
| Good talk. | I liked the story you used to open. It made the problem real. |

## How to receive

- "Thank you, that means a lot."
- Do not deflect or argue.
