You cannot budget well until you know what you cannot cut.

## Direct answer

Essential expenses are the costs you must pay to live and work: housing, utilities, basic food, transport, health care, insurance and minimum debt payments. Anything beyond the basic version, such as premium plans or dining out, is usually a want.

## Typical essentials

- Rent or mortgage
- Electricity, water, heating
- Basic groceries
- Transport to work or school
- Health costs and necessary insurance
- Childcare
- Minimum payments on debts
- Basic phone and internet

## Be honest about the level

Food is essential, but takeaways are not. A phone is essential, but a premium plan might not be. Split each category into "basic" and "extra".

## Build your list

1. Go through three months of statements.
2. Mark each item essential, partly essential or non-essential.
3. Total the essentials. That is your minimum monthly figure.
