The thrill fades and the doubt arrives.

## Direct answer

Buyer's remorse is regret after buying, often because the purchase was impulsive, costly or not what you expected. If it hits, check the return window, return or resell the item if you can, and learn from what triggered it. To prevent it, wait before big purchases and compare options.

## What to do right now

1. **Check the return policy** and deadlines.
2. **Return or resell** if you can.
3. **If you keep it,** use it and look for what you like about it.
4. **Note the trigger:** sale, stress, peer pressure.
5. **Forgive yourself.** Everyone makes these mistakes.

## How to prevent it

- Use a waiting rule for non-essentials.
- Read honest reviews.
- Set a budget in advance.
- Buy fewer, better things.
