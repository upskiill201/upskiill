## Direct Answer: Niche Skills vs. General Skills at a Glance

> **Niche Skill Definition:** A rare, specialized technical or operational competency that addresses a specific high-value business bottleneck where demand vastly exceeds available talent supply.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 NICHE SKILLS VS. GENERAL SKILLS             │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  GENERAL SKILLS ──> High supply, commoditized, low pricing  │
│  NICHE SKILLS   ──> Scarce talent, mission-critical, high $ │
│                                                             │
│  ※ Example: General Web Developer ($65k) vs. Rust WebAssembly
│    Performance Engineer ($185k+).                           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Dimension | Generalist Skills | Niche Specialized Skills |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Talent Supply** | Massive pool of millions of applicants | Small global talent pool (thousands or hundreds) |
| **Pricing Power** | Fixed standard hourly wages | Value-based pricing & high retainer rates ($150–$350/hr) |
| **Recruiter Sourcing** | Filtered through high-volume ATS portals | Headhunted directly by executive recruiters |
| **Examples** | Basic HTML/CSS, General Data Entry, Virtual Assistant | Rust Systems, AI Quantization, Salesforce CPQ, Reverse Engineering |
