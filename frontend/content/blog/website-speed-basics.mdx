People leave slow sites.

## Direct answer

To make a site load faster, compress and resize images, lazy-load those below the fold, reduce and defer JavaScript, minimise third-party scripts, use browser caching and a CDN and choose fast hosting. Measure with Lighthouse before and after.

## 10 ways

1. Resize and compress images.
2. Use modern formats like WebP or AVIF.
3. Lazy-load below-the-fold images.
4. Defer or remove unused JavaScript.
5. Minimise third-party scripts.
6. Use a CDN.
7. Enable caching.
8. Use fewer fonts and load them efficiently.
9. Reserve space for images to avoid layout shift.
10. Choose reliable hosting.

## Measure it

Run Lighthouse in Chrome DevTools or use PageSpeed Insights. Focus on the largest improvements first.
