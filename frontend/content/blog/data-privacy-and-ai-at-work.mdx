The easiest way to cause a data incident is to paste it into a chatbot.

## Direct answer

Do not paste personal data about customers or colleagues, passwords and keys, confidential contracts, unreleased results or anything your organisation classes as confidential into AI tools unless they are approved for that use. Learn your policy, use approved tools and anonymise what you can.

## A quick traffic light

| | Examples |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Green** | public information, your own draft text with no names |
| **Amber** | internal documents: only in approved tools |
| **Red** | personal data, secrets, contracts, health or financial records |

## How to reduce risk

1. **Check the policy** and ask which tools are approved.
2. **Remove names and identifiers** before pasting.
3. **Use enterprise or business plans** with data controls when provided.
4. **Turn off training on your data** where the setting exists.
5. **Never paste credentials.**
6. **Ask when unsure.**
