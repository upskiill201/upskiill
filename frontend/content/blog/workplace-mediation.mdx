Sometimes a neutral third person helps people hear each other.

## Direct answer

Workplace mediation is a confidential process where a trained neutral mediator helps two parties talk through a dispute and reach their own agreement. It suits communication breakdowns and personality clashes, not serious misconduct.

## What happens

1. Separate conversations with each person.
2. A joint meeting with ground rules.
3. Each person speaks and listens.
4. The group explores solutions.
5. They agree a written plan.

## Benefits

- Faster than formal procedures.
- Preserves relationships.
- Gives people control.
