A pitch deck supports your story. It is not the story.

## Direct answer

A pitch deck typically covers the problem, your solution, the market, your business model, traction, the team and the ask. Keep slides simple, lead with the problem and make each slide support one idea.

## Typical slides

1. Title and one-liner
2. Problem
3. Solution
4. Product
5. Market
6. Business model
7. Traction
8. Competition
9. Team
10. The ask

## Tips

- One idea per slide.
- Use big, clear visuals.
- Tell a story with a beginning and end.
- Practise the talk.
