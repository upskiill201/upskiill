The goal is a conversation, not a performance.

## Direct answer

At a networking event, introduce yourself with your name, what you do and a short detail that invites a question, then ask about the other person. Listen, take a card or contact, and follow up within a couple of days.

## A simple formula

"Hi, I'm [name]. I [what you do], and I'm especially interested in [topic]. What brings you here?"

## Questions to ask

- What are you working on right now?
- How did you get into this field?
- What would make this event worthwhile for you?

## Follow up

"Great to meet you at [event]. I enjoyed hearing about [topic]. Here is the article I mentioned."
