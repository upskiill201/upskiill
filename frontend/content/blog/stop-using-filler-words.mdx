The pause feels longer to you than to your audience.

## Direct answer

To reduce filler words, slow down, prepare your key points and replace "um" with a silent pause. Record yourself to notice your patterns, practise out loud and have a friend signal when you use them. You will not remove them all, and that is fine.

## Steps

1. **Record** a minute of speech and count them.
2. **Slow your pace.**
3. **Plan your first sentence** of each point.
4. **Pause** instead of filling.
5. **Practise** in low-stakes settings.
6. **Review** progress weekly.

## Tips

- Know the next idea before finishing the current one.
- Breathe at the end of sentences.
- Do not obsess. Clear content matters more.
