Mindful spending is not about guilt. It is about knowing what your money is for.

## Direct answer

Mindful spending means pausing before you buy to check whether the purchase matches your values and your budget. You notice why you want it, how it will feel later and whether it supports your goals. The result is usually fewer regrets and more money for what matters.

## Five habits

1. **Know your values.** Write the three things that matter most to you.
2. **Pause before buying.** Take a breath, or wait a day.
3. **Ask three questions:** Do I need it? Do I want it enough? Does it fit my budget?
4. **Review monthly.** Which purchases gave real value? Which did not?
5. **Plan treats.** Include fun money so you are not tempted to binge.

## A quick test

Imagine looking at the item in a month. Will you be glad you spent the money?
