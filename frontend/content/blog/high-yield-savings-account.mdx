Your savings earn more where interest is higher. The difference compounds over time.

## Direct answer

A high-yield savings account pays a higher rate of interest than a standard savings account. It works like any savings account, but it often comes from online banks with lower costs. Compare the rate, any conditions, withdrawal rules and whether deposits are protected by a government-backed scheme.

## What to compare

- **Interest rate and whether it is variable.** Rates can change.
- **Introductory bonus rates** that drop after a period.
- **Minimum balance or deposit requirements.**
- **Withdrawal rules and fees.**
- **Deposit protection:** check the limit and the scheme in your country.
- **Ease of use:** transfers, app and customer support.

## Who it suits

It suits money you may need soon: emergency funds, sinking funds and short-term goals. It is not designed for long-term investing, where other products may offer higher growth but more risk.

## How to make it work

Set up an automatic transfer on payday so the account grows without effort. Review the rate every few months.
