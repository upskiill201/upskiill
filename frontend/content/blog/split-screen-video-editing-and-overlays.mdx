## Direct Answer: Split Screen & Overlays at a Glance (40–60 Word Summary)

Split screen video editing displays two or more independent video streams simultaneously by partitioning the screen canvas horizontally, vertically, or in a multi-box grid. Video overlays place secondary media layers on top of primary tracks using opacity sliders, blend modes (Screen, Multiply), or green screen chroma keying.

| Compositing Style | Timeline Tracks | Key Tool / Feature | Ideal Application | Difficulty |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Side-by-Side Split** | 2 Video Tracks | Transform (X-Position) + Crop | Before/After, Interviews, Music duets | Beginner |
| **Top & Bottom Split** | 2 Video Tracks | Transform (Y-Position) + Crop | TikTok gameplay + facecam reactions | Beginner |
| **Green Screen Keying**| 2 Video Tracks | Chroma Key / Ultra Key | Virtual sets, magical VFX, memes | Intermediate |
| **Blend Mode Overlay** | 2 Video Tracks | Screen / Add / Lighten Blends | Film grain, light leaks, flash transitions | Beginner |
| **Dynamic Blur Mask** | 1–2 Video Tracks | Gaussian Blur + Motion Tracking| Face blurring, privacy, aesthetic depth | Intermediate |

## How to Create a Split Screen Video (Mobile & Desktop)

Whether you are crafting a split-screen music video, a workout comparison, or a podcast dialogue, follow this universal multi-track setup:

```
Split Screen Composition Topologies:
1. Side-by-Side (50/50 Vertical Cut)     2. Top & Bottom (Mobile Stack)      3. Four-Way Quad Grid (2x2)
┌─────────────┬─────────────┐               ┌─────────────────────────────┐    ┌──────────────┬──────────────┐
│             │             │               │      Camera A (Reaction)    │    │   Cam 1      │    Cam 2     │
│   Video 1   │   Video 2   │               ├─────────────────────────────┤    ├──────────────┼──────────────┤
│  (Left 50%) │ (Right 50%) │               │      Camera B (Gameplay)    │    │   Cam 3      │    Cam 4     │
│             │             │               └─────────────────────────────┘    └──────────────┴──────────────┘
└─────────────┴─────────────┘
```

### Method 1: KineMaster Split Screen Workflow
1. Open KineMaster and start a project (16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for TikTok/Reels).
2. Tap **Media** and select your first video clip (Track 1).
3. Tap **Layer -> Media** to import your second video clip onto Track 2 directly beneath the first.
4. Select the layer clip and tap the **Split Screen** icon. Choose the **Left Split** or **Right Split** preset.
5. Tap and drag inside the preview window to reposition the subject inside their respective half.
6. Optional: Add a 2px white border frame or colored divider using a graphic solid layer between the clips.

### Method 2: How to Overlay Videos in InShot (PIP Feature)
1. In InShot, create a **Video** project and select your primary base clip.
2. Tap the **PIP (Picture-in-Picture)** icon on the toolbar.
3. Choose the secondary video or photo from your camera roll.
4. Tap **Crop** to trim the secondary clip to a 1:1 square or 9:16 slice.
5. Drag the corner handles to position it alongside your base video.
6. Use the **Mask** tool (split line, circle, or rounded rectangle) to create a custom geometric split edge.

## Master Overlay Tutorial: Blending Modes and Visual Textures

Overlays add cinematic depth, vintage atmosphere, and magical flair (such as dust particles, light leaks, and retro film burns) without requiring complex 3D rendering.

```
Visual Stacking Hierarchy:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Track V3: [Film Grain / Light Leak Overlay] (Blend: Screen)
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Track V2: [Floating Reaction Window / PIP]  (Scale: 40%)
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Track V1: [Primary Base Storyline]          (100% Scale)
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The 3 Core Blend Modes for Overlays:
1. **Screen Blend Mode (Black Removal):** When you download black-background visual assets (fireworks, lens flares, dust motes, fire flashes, or magic spell VFX), setting the layer blend mode to **Screen** instantly makes the black pixels 100% transparent while preserving the bright glowing highlights.
2. **Multiply Blend Mode (White Removal):** When using white-background textures (crumpled paper, sketched borders, dirty parchment), **Multiply** renders white pixels transparent and imprints the dark texture onto your underlying footage.
3. **Overlay / Soft Light (Contrast & Saturation):** Blends color gradients and cinematic color wash textures directly into your footage's highlights and shadows.

## Recording and Editing Green Screen Footage Like a Pro

Green screen (chroma keying) replaces a solid green or blue background with any background environment, UI mockup, or gameplay capture:

```
[Evenly Lit Subject on Green Backdrop]
                   │
                   ▼
[Import to Track V2 + Background to Track V1]
                   │
                   ▼
[Apply Chroma Key / Ultra Key Tool]
                   │
                   ▼
[Pick Background Color with Eyedropper]
                   │
                   ▼
[Tune Matte Cleanup: Pedestal + Clean Spill]
                   │
                   ▼
[Perfect Composited Subject in New Environment]
```

### Pro Tips for Flawless Green Screen Keying:
- **Zero Wrinkles and Flat Lighting:** Wrinkles and hot spots on the green fabric create uneven shades of green, forcing the chroma keyer to leave noisy fringes. Use two soft lights dedicated exclusively to illuminating the green backdrop.
- **Maintain Distance:** Position your actor at least 5 to 7 feet in front of the green screen to eliminate **green color spill** (green light bouncing onto the subject's hair, ears, and shoulders).
- **Spill Suppression:** In your software's chroma keyer settings, raise the **Spill Suppression / Despill** slider to neutralize any stray green reflections on the subject.

## How to Apply Blur Effects for Focus and Privacy

A blur video editor is essential for censoring sensitive information (passwords, phone numbers, faces) and creating cinematic depth of field.

### 2 Main Uses of Blur Effects:
1. **Subject Privacy / Face Blurring:** Apply a **Mosaic** or **Gaussian Blur** effect. Draw an elliptical mask over the person's face. Click the **Track Forward** button to automatically motion-track the mask across the timeline.
2. **Cinematic Split-Focus (Split Screen Flash):** When displaying two subjects in a split screen, apply a mild blur to the inactive listener while keeping the active speaker sharp, guiding the viewer's focal attention seamlessly.
