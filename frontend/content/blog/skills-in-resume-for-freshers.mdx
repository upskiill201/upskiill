When you have zero formal work experience, the **skills in resume for freshers** section is your single most important asset to capture recruiter attention and pass automated Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). With gamified micro-learning platforms like [Teyro](/onboarding/0), you can build verifiable coding, data, and business project skills in just 15 minutes of daily practice.
