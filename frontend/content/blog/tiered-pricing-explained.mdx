Different customers, different needs, different prices.

## Direct answer

Tiered pricing offers packages, often three, targeting different customer groups. The basic tier covers entry needs, the middle serves most customers and the top tier serves power users. Make the differences clear and base them on customer value.

## Design steps

1. Identify customer segments.
2. List features and their value to each.
3. Group into tiers.
4. Name tiers clearly.
5. Highlight the recommended tier.
6. Test and adjust.

## Mistakes

- Too many options.
- Differences that are unclear.
- Tiers based on cost to you rather than value to customers.
