Projects prove skills that resumes only claim.

## Direct answer

Choose a project that solves a real problem or relates to the job you want, finish it, document it with a README or case study and talk about what you learned. Two or three good projects are enough.

## Ideas by field

| Field | Project |
| :--- | :--- |
| Web | personal portfolio or small business site |
| Data | analysis of a public dataset |
| Marketing | content plan for a local business |
| Design | redesign of a confusing app |

## Present it

1. Explain the problem.
2. Show the result.
3. Describe your role and tools.
4. Share what you would improve.
