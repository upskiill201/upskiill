A few careful minutes before launch save hours of embarrassment after.

## Direct answer

Before launching, test your content, links, forms, mobile layout and speed, check titles and descriptions, enable HTTPS, set up analytics and backups, add a sitemap and test on real devices. After launch, submit the site to search engines and monitor for problems.

## Checklist

**Content**
1. Spelling and grammar checked.
2. Contact details correct.
3. Placeholder text removed.

**Function**
4. All links work.
5. Forms send and confirm.
6. 404 page exists.

**Design**
7. Mobile layout checked on a real phone.
8. Works in major browsers.
9. Text is readable and has good contrast.

**Technical**
10. HTTPS on and redirects set.
11. Favicon added.
12. Images compressed.
13. Speed tested.

**SEO**
14. Unique titles and descriptions.
15. Headings in order.
16. Alt text added.
17. Sitemap created.
18. Robots settings allow indexing.

**Tracking and safety**
19. Analytics installed.
20. Cookie notice if needed.
21. Backups set up.
22. Privacy policy added if you collect data.

**After launch**
23. Submit to Search Console.
24. Share it.
25. Monitor and fix.
