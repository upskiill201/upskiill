Busy readers read the top. Make it count.

## Direct answer

A work summary should give the conclusion, the key reasons and the next step in a few lines at the top. Write it last, then check that someone who reads only the summary could act.

## A three-line format

1. **What:** the conclusion or recommendation.
2. **Why:** two or three key reasons.
3. **Next:** what you need and by when.

## Example

"We should switch to the new supplier. It is 12 percent cheaper and delivers two days faster. Approval is needed by 30 June to avoid renewal fees."

## Tighten a draft

- Cut introductions.
- Delete repeated points.
- Replace long phrases with short ones.
