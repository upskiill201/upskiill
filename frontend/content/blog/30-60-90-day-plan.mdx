A simple structure for starting well.

## Direct answer

A 30-60-90 day plan sets goals for the first three months of a role: learn in the first 30 days, start contributing in the next 30 and take ownership by day 90. It helps new employees and managers agree expectations.

## Template

**Days 1 to 30: Learn**
- Meet the team and key stakeholders.
- Learn tools, processes and goals.
- Complete training.

**Days 31 to 60: Contribute**
- Take on small projects.
- Suggest improvements.
- Get feedback.

**Days 61 to 90: Own**
- Lead a project or area.
- Hit agreed targets.
- Plan the next quarter.

## Tips for managers

1. Agree the plan in week one.
2. Meet weekly.
3. Adjust as you learn.
