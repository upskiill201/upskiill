AI can be a patient tutor or a shortcut that leaves you knowing nothing. The difference is how you use it.

## Direct answer

To study with AI, use it as a tutor, not an answer machine: ask it to explain topics at your level, quiz you, check your answers and suggest what to revise. Try problems yourself first, ask for hints, put ideas into your own words and check important facts against trusted material.

## 6 ways that help

1. **Explain at your level.** "Explain photosynthesis to a 14-year-old, then give an example."
2. **Quiz me.** "Ask me five questions on this topic, one at a time. Tell me what I got wrong."
3. **Give feedback.** Paste your answer and ask what is missing.
4. **Make a plan.** "I have 10 days to revise. Create a plan with 40 minutes a day."
5. **Turn notes into flashcards.**
6. **Teach it back.** Explain the topic to the AI and ask for gaps.

## Prompts to try

- "Ask me to explain this concept, then correct me gently."
- "Give me a hint, not the answer."
- "Give me a harder question on this topic."

More prompt ideas: [ChatGPT for studying](/blog/chatgpt-for-studying).

## Mistakes

- Copying answers
- Trusting facts without checking
- Skipping practice questions
- Using AI when your school forbids it

## Study skills still matter

Spaced practice, testing yourself and sleep beat any tool. AI helps you do them.
