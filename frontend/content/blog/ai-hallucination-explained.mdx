A model does not know what it does not know. It still answers.

## Direct answer

An AI hallucination is a confident answer that is wrong or made up, such as a fake quote, citation or statistic. It happens because language models generate likely-sounding text instead of looking up verified facts. Reduce the risk by giving the model source material, asking for evidence and checking anything important yourself.

## Common examples

- Invented references or links.
- Wrong dates, numbers or names.
- Confident descriptions of things that do not exist.

## How to protect yourself

1. **Verify important facts** with trusted sources.
2. **Provide the source text** and ask the model to answer only from it.
3. **Ask it to say when it is unsure.**
4. **Check any citation** before using it.
5. **Use tools that retrieve real documents** for current information.
