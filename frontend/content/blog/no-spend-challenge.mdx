A no spend challenge is a reset for your habits. It shows you how much of your spending is automatic.

## Direct answer

A no spend challenge means you stop non-essential spending for a set time, such as a week or a month. You still pay for essentials like rent, bills, basic groceries and transport. The goal is to save money and notice what you normally buy without thinking.

## Set your rules first

1. **Choose the length:** start with a week if a month feels too big.
2. **Write your essentials list:** housing, utilities, basic food, health, commuting, debt minimums.
3. **Decide the exceptions:** for example, one planned social event.
4. **Tell someone** for accountability.

## Tips for staying on track

- Unsubscribe from promotional emails and delete shopping apps.
- Use up what is in the fridge, freezer and cupboards.
- Plan free activities: walks, library, home workouts, cooking with friends.
- Keep a list of things you want, and review it at the end.

## After the challenge

Do not return to old habits. Review what you missed, what you did not, and decide which spending to keep. Move the saved money into savings straight away.
