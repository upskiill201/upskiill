A raise should improve your life. If it just raises your bills, it does not.

## Direct answer

Lifestyle inflation means your spending grows as your income grows, so you never get ahead. A higher salary leads to a bigger home, nicer car and more subscriptions, and savings stay flat. To avoid it, set aside part of each raise before you start spending the rest.

## How it happens

- Small upgrades that feel normal: better food, newer gadgets.
- Spending to match friends or coworkers.
- Treating a raise as "extra money" to spend.

## A simple rule

When your income rises, split the increase. For example, save half and use half to enjoy. Automate the savings part on the same day your first pay rise lands.

## Spend on what you value

Upgrades are fine when they add real value. Ask: "Would I choose this if I had to save for it?"
