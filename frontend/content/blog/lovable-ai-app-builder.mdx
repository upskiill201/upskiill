Prompt, preview, refine. That is the loop.

## Direct answer

Lovable is an AI app builder that creates web apps from plain-language prompts, shows a live preview and lets you refine them through conversation. It is useful for prototypes and simple apps. As with other AI builders, review the output, protect your data and check current features and pricing on its website, since they change.

## A typical workflow

1. **Describe the app:** who it is for and what it does.
2. **Review the preview.**
3. **Refine in small steps:** "Add a login page", "Show a list of tasks".
4. **Connect a backend** if you need data.
5. **Test the main flows.**
6. **Deploy** and share.

## Tips for better results

- Be specific about screens, data and behaviour.
- Add one feature at a time.
- Keep a list of what works so you can restore it.
- Check mobile layout.

## Limits

- Complex logic can need manual coding.
- Generated code needs review for security and quality.
