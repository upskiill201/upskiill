Notes are for learning, not for copying.

## Direct answer

The best note-taking method depends on the material. Cornell notes work for lectures and review. Outlines suit structured topics. Mind maps show relationships. Charts compare facts. Whatever you choose, process the ideas in your own words and review your notes soon after.

## Methods

| Method | Best for |
| :--- | :--- |
| Cornell | lectures and exam revision |
| Outline | structured, hierarchical topics |
| Mind map | brainstorming and overviews |
| Charting | comparing facts or categories |
| Zettelkasten | connecting ideas over time |

## Habits that improve any method

1. Write in your own words.
2. Add questions as you go.
3. Review within 24 hours.
4. Turn notes into flashcards or practice questions.
