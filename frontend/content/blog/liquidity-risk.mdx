Having wealth on paper is not the same as having cash.

## Direct answer

Liquidity risk is the risk that you cannot turn an asset into cash quickly without losing value. Cash is highly liquid. Property and private investments are less so. Keep money you may need soon in liquid accounts and be wary of products that lock your money away.

## Examples

- Selling a house can take months.
- Some funds restrict withdrawals during stress.
- Collectibles can be hard to sell at a fair price.

## How to plan

1. **Hold an emergency fund** in easy-access savings.
2. **Match investments to timelines.**
3. **Check withdrawal rules** and fees before investing.
4. **Do not rely on illiquid assets** for short-term needs.
