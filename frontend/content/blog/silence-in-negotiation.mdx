## Direct answer

Silence is a negotiation tool. After making an offer or hearing one, pause for a few seconds. It shows confidence, gives you time to think and often leads the other side to clarify or improve their proposal.

## When to use it

- After stating your number.
- After a surprising offer.
- When you need time to think.

## Tips

1. Breathe and relax your face.
2. Count to five.
3. Keep eye contact.
4. If needed, say "Let me think about that."
