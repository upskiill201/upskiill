## Direct answer

To move the needle means to make a noticeable, measurable difference, as when a change raises sales or satisfaction. It is a way to ask whether something is worth the effort. In plain English, say "make a real difference".

## Examples

- "Will this campaign move the needle on sign-ups?"
- "Those small fixes will not move the needle."

## Plain alternatives

| Jargon | Plain |
| :--- | :--- |
| Move the needle | Make a real difference |
| Needle movers | Biggest drivers |
