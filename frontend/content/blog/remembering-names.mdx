Names are the sweetest sound, as the saying goes, and the easiest to forget.

## Direct answer

To remember names, focus on the person when you meet, repeat the name back, use it in the conversation, link it to a feature or image and write it down afterwards. If you forget, simply ask again.

## 7 techniques

1. **Pay attention** when they say it.
2. **Repeat it:** "Nice to meet you, Chidi."
3. **Use it** once or twice in the chat.
4. **Associate** with an image or a person you know.
5. **Ask for the spelling** if unusual.
6. **Write it down** soon after.
7. **Review** before meeting again.

## If you forget

"I am sorry, I have forgotten your name. Could you remind me?" People appreciate honesty.
