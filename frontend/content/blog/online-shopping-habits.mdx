One click makes spending painless. That is exactly the problem.

## Direct answer

To spend less online, add friction: remove saved cards and one-click checkout, delete shopping apps, unsubscribe from promotions, and use the cart as a wish list that you revisit after at least a day. Plan your purchases and set a monthly limit.

## Habits that help

1. **Remove stored payment details.**
2. **Log out of shopping sites.**
3. **Use a list,** not browsing, to shop.
4. **Wait 24 hours** before checkout.
5. **Check total cost** including shipping and returns.
6. **Read reviews** with a critical eye.
7. **Track monthly online spending** in a note.
8. **Set a monthly limit** and stop when you reach it.

## Watch out for

- "Free shipping over" thresholds that make you add more.
- Subscription traps after free trials.
- Sale emails that arrive at pay time.
