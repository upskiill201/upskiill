If they cannot find you, they cannot call you.

## Direct answer

To be found by recruiters, complete your profile with clear titles, keywords and skills, add a professional photo, share results and set your preferences to show you are open to work. Reply professionally and ask for role details.

## Checklist

- [ ] Clear headline
- [ ] Skills listed
- [ ] Achievements with numbers
- [ ] Location and preferences
- [ ] Updated resume

## Replying

"Thank you for reaching out. I would be glad to learn more. Could you share the role description and salary range?"
