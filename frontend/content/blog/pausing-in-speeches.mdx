Silence is a tool, not an accident.

## Direct answer

Use pauses before and after important points, between ideas and after questions. A one to two second pause gives listeners time to think and makes you sound more confident. Practise by marking pauses in your notes and rehearsing aloud.

## Where to pause

- After an important sentence.
- Before a surprising fact.
- After a question.
- When changing topic.

## Drill

1. Read a paragraph aloud.
2. Mark pauses with slashes.
3. Count one-two in your head at each slash.
4. Record and listen.
