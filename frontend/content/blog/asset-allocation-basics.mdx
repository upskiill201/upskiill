How you split your money often matters more than which individual investments you pick.

## Direct answer

Asset allocation is the mix of asset classes in your portfolio, such as shares, bonds and cash. A higher share of shares generally means higher potential growth and more volatility. A higher share of bonds and cash means more stability. Your mix should fit your goals, timeline and comfort with risk.

## Factors that shape it

- **Time horizon:** longer horizons can ride out more volatility.
- **Risk tolerance:** how you feel about losses.
- **Risk capacity:** how much loss you can afford.
- **Goals:** growth, income or preservation.

## A gradual shift

Some investors move toward safer assets as they approach a goal. Some funds automate this.
