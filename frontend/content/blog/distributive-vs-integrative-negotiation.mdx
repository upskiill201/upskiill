Is the pie fixed, or can you grow it?

## Direct answer

Distributive negotiation divides a fixed amount, such as a price. Integrative negotiation adds issues and options so both sides gain, such as trading price for delivery time. Most negotiations include both: expand the pie, then divide it.

## Comparison

| | Distributive | Integrative |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Aim | claim value | create value |
| Issues | one, often price | many |
| Approach | positions | interests |
| Relationship | may be short-term | often ongoing |

## Example

A supplier wants faster payment and you want a lower price. Agree on quicker payment in exchange for a discount.
