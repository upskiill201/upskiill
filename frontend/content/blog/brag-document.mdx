You will forget most of what you did. Write it down.

## Direct answer

A brag document is a running list of your achievements, with dates and results. Update it weekly or monthly and use it to prepare for performance reviews, ask for raises and update your resume.

## What to include

- What you delivered and the result.
- Positive feedback.
- Skills you learned.
- Problems you solved.
- Help you gave others.

## Simple format

| Date | What I did | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 12 May | Built onboarding checklist | New hires productive two days faster |
