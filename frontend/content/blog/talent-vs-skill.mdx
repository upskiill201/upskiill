## 1. The Neuroscience of Skill Acquisition: Myelin and Neuroplasticity

Whenever you practice a skill with intense focus, your brain wraps the relevant neural circuits in a fatty insulating substance called **myelin**:

* **The Myelin Effect:** The more myelin wraps around a nerve pathway, the faster and more accurately electrical signals travel.
* **Why Practice Beats Talent:** A "talented" beginner has slightly more efficient initial wiring, but a disciplined learner who logs daily reps develops thick myelin insulation that makes execution lightning-fast and automatic.

## 2. The Deliberate Practice Framework (Dr. Anders Ericsson)

Mindless repetition does not build skill. Elite performers use **deliberate practice**:

1. **Specific Micro-Goals:** Instead of "practicing coding," practice "mastering binary search algorithms in Python" (see [Duolingo for Python](/blog/duolingo-for-python)).
2. **Immediate Feedback:** Knowing within seconds whether your solution worked.
3. **Operating at the Edge of Comfort:** Pushing just beyond your current competence level (where growth happens).

## 3. Carol Dweck's Growth Mindset vs. Fixed Mindset

* **Fixed Mindset:** Believing intelligence and talent are static traits ("I'm just bad at business").
* **Growth Mindset:** Believing that intellectual abilities can be developed through effort and strategy (explore [how to learn business skills](/blog/how-to-learn-business-skills)).
