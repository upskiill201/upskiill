Ice cream sales and sunburn rise together. Ice cream does not cause sunburn.

## Direct answer

Correlation shows that two variables move together. Causation means that changing one produces a change in the other. A third factor, a confounder, can cause both, as hot weather drives both ice cream sales and sunburn. Randomised experiments are the best way to show cause.

## Why correlations mislead

- **Confounders:** a hidden driver.
- **Coincidence:** random patterns in many variables.
- **Reverse causation:** the effect may cause the "cause".

## How to be careful

1. Ask what else could explain the link.
2. Check whether the cause came before the effect.
3. Look for experiments or natural experiments.
4. Be cautious with language: "is associated with", not "causes".
