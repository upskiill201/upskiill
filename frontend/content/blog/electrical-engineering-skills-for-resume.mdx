## 1. Hardware Design, Schematic Capture & PCB Layout

Modern hardware teams want engineers who understand real-world manufacturing constraints:

* **High-Speed Routing:** Managing differential pair trace lengths and controlled impedance for USB/Ethernet signals.
* **Design for Manufacturing (DFM):** Generating clean Gerber files, Bill of Materials (BOM), and pick-and-place files for PCB assembly houses.

---

## 2. Embedded Systems & Python Automation

Hardware is incomplete without firmware and automated testing:

* **Embedded C/C++:** Writing efficient interrupt service routines (ISRs) on ARM microcontrollers.
* **Python for Lab Automation:** Writing Python scripts (via PyVISA) to automate oscilloscope waveform captures and multimeter logging (learn in [Duolingo for Python](/blog/duolingo-for-python) and [easy Python games](/blog/easy-python-games)).

---

## 3. Laboratory Diagnostics & Safety Standards

* **Test Instrumentation:** Operating digital storage oscilloscopes (DSOs), logic analyzers, and spectrum analyzers to debug bus timing errors.
* **Regulatory Compliance:** Adhering to FCC Part 15 (EMI/EMC emissions), CE safety marks, and UL isolation standards.
