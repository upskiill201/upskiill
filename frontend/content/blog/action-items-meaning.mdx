Meetings without action items are conversations.

## Direct answer

An action item is a specific task assigned during a meeting, with an owner and a deadline. Write them with a verb, a clear outcome and a date, such as "Sam to send the revised budget to finance by Friday."

## Template

**Who:** Sam
**What:** Send revised budget to finance
**By when:** Friday

## Tips

1. One owner per item.
2. Be specific.
3. Share the list right after the meeting.
4. Review at the next meeting.
