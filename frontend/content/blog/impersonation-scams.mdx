## Direct answer

In an impersonation scam, fraudsters pretend to be your bank, a government agency, a tech support team, a delivery service or a relative. They create urgency and ask you to share codes, move money or install software. Hang up, then contact the real organisation through a number you look up yourself.

## Common versions

- **Bank fraud team** asking you to move money to a "safe account".
- **Tax or government officials** threatening penalties.
- **Tech support** claiming your computer is infected.
- **Family emergency** from a "relative" with a new number.

## The routine

1. **Stop** and do not act under pressure.
2. **Do not share** passwords, codes or PINs.
3. **Hang up** and look up the real number.
4. **Call back** using that number.
5. **Report** the attempt.

## Agree a family code word

A secret word helps verify urgent calls or messages from relatives.
