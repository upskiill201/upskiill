## 1. Financial Literacy & Unit Economics

If you cannot read a balance sheet, you cannot make strategic business decisions. Every business lives or dies by cash flow.

* **Core Metrics:** Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Lifetime Value (LTV), Gross Margin, Burn Rate, and Net Profit.
* **The Rule of Unit Economics:** If it costs you $50 to acquire a customer who only spends $30, no amount of scale will make your business profitable.

---

## 2. Sales, Persuasion & High-Stakes Negotiation

Sales is the lifeblood of any organization. Learning to listen actively, uncover customer pain points, and present solutions persuasively is the highest-leverage skill in business.

| Sales Technique | Psychological Principle | Practical Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Active Discovery** | People buy for their reasons, not yours | Ask open-ended questions about their bottlenecks |
| **Value Anchoring** | Price is relative to perceived ROI | Compare your cost to the cost of inaction |
| **Risk Reversal** | Fear of loss exceeds desire for gain | Offer ironclad guarantees or milestone billing |
