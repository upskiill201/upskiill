If you are the same as everyone else, price becomes the only reason to choose you.

## Direct answer

A unique selling point is a specific reason customers should pick you over alternatives. Find it by listing what customers value, checking what competitors say and choosing something true, specific and important to your audience.

## Steps

1. Ask customers why they chose you.
2. List competitors' claims.
3. Find the gap.
4. Make sure it is true and provable.
5. State it simply.

## Examples

- "Free shipping and returns, always."
- "Same-day repairs or your money back."
