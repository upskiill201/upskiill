Fixed costs set the floor of your budget. Lowering them is the biggest long-term win.

## Direct answer

Fixed expenses stay about the same every month: rent, loan payments, insurance, subscriptions with set prices. They are hard to change in the short term but lowering them gives lasting savings. Review contracts, compare providers and, when it makes sense, change big costs such as housing and transport.

## Common examples

- Rent or mortgage
- Car payments and insurance
- Phone and internet plans
- Student loans
- Gym or software subscriptions

## How to lower them

1. **Renegotiate or switch** insurance, phone and internet.
2. **Cancel unused subscriptions.**
3. **Refinance or consolidate** debt if it lowers the total cost. Check terms carefully.
4. **Consider housing changes,** such as a roommate or a cheaper area.
5. **Choose cheaper transport** where possible.

## When to do it

Review fixed costs when contracts renew and at least once a year.
