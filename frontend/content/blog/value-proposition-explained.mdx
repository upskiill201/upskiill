If customers cannot say why you matter, you do not have a value proposition yet.

## Direct answer

A value proposition is a short statement of who you help, what problem you solve, the benefit and why you are different. A simple formula is: "For [audience] who [need], we [do X] so that [benefit], unlike [alternative]." Test it with real customers.

## Examples

- "For busy parents who struggle to plan meals, our app creates a weekly plan in two minutes, so dinner is never a scramble."
- "For freelancers who dislike chasing payments, we send invoices and reminders automatically."

## How to write one

1. Describe the customer and their problem.
2. State the main benefit.
3. Say what makes you different.
4. Use plain words.
5. Test and refine.

## Mistakes

- Listing features instead of benefits.
- Vague claims such as "best quality".
- Too many ideas at once.
