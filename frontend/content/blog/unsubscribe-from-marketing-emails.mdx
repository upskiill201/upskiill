You cannot spend on a sale you never saw.

## Direct answer

To cut marketing triggers, unsubscribe from promotional emails, turn off shopping app notifications and unfollow brands that make you want things. Use filters to send promotions to a separate folder you check only when you plan a purchase.

## Step by step

1. **Search your inbox** for "unsubscribe" and clear a batch each day.
2. **Click unsubscribe** at the bottom of each email.
3. **Create a filter** that sends promotional emails to a folder.
4. **Turn off push notifications** from shopping apps.
5. **Delete shopping apps** or log out so you must sign in each time.
6. **Unfollow or mute** brand and influencer accounts that trigger spending.
7. **Use a separate email** for receipts and sign-ups.

## Keep the useful ones

Keep alerts for a few things you really need, such as price drops on a planned purchase.
