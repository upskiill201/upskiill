Cutting everything makes you miserable. Cutting what you do not care about frees money for what you do.

## Direct answer

Conscious spending means you decide in advance how much goes to essentials, savings and investments, and guilt-free spending, and then you spend your fun money without worrying. You cut ruthlessly on things you do not value so you can spend on what you do.

## A simple structure

1. **Fixed costs:** housing, bills, debt minimums.
2. **Savings and investments:** automated.
3. **Guilt-free spending:** whatever remains, for things you love.

## Make it work

- Automate savings first.
- Pick two or three areas where you will happily spend more.
- Cut one or two areas you do not care about.
- Review every month.
