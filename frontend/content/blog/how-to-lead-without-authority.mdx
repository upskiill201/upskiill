You don't need a title to lead. Many teams rely on people who step up without being asked.

## Direct answer

To lead without authority, build credibility by being reliable, find the goals you share with other people, make clear and easy requests, run meetings you don't own so they help everyone, follow up on what you promise, and give credit generously. Influence comes from trust and usefulness, not rank.

## 8 ways

1. **Be reliable.** Do what you say, on time.
2. **Know what others care about.** Link your request to their goals.
3. **Make asks small and clear.** "Can you review this section by Thursday?"
4. **Build relationships before you need them.**
5. **Share a goal.** "We both want fewer late deliveries."
6. **Bring solutions,** not just problems.
7. **Run a good meeting,** even if it's not yours: agenda, notes, owners.
8. **Follow up and share credit.**

## Scripts

- "I've noticed the handover causes delays. Could we try a shared checklist for two weeks?"
- "I'd like your view before I propose it to the director."
- "Thanks to Priya's work on the data, we cut the time in half."

## When you meet resistance

Ask what worries them, adjust the plan and offer a small trial.
