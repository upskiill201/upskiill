## 1. Emotional Resilience & Coping with Stress

Student life in 2026 is filled with digital overwhelm, social media comparison, and intense examination pressure:

* **Distress Tolerance:** Understanding that feeling stressed before a test is a normal physiological arousal signal rather than a catastrophe.
* **The 4-7-8 Breathing Technique:** Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7 seconds, exhale for 8 seconds to reset the nervous system.

---

## 2. Critical Thinking and Information Literacy

In an age of deepfakes and AI hallucinations, students must learn to question what they see online:

1. **Source Verification:** Always checking the primary author and funding behind internet claims (see [ATL skills guide](/blog/atl-skills-guide)).
2. **First Principles Analysis:** Breaking complex social and academic problems down to foundational truths.
