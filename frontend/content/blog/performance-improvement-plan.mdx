A PIP should be a fair chance to improve, not a paper trail.

## Direct answer

A performance improvement plan lists specific performance problems, what success looks like, the support the employee will receive and a timeline with review dates. Managers should be clear, fair and supportive. Employees should ask questions, document progress and seek advice if needed.

## For managers

1. **Check expectations were clear** before this point.
2. **Give specific examples** and measurable goals.
3. **Offer support:** training, coaching, resources.
4. **Set review dates** and meet regularly.
5. **Document fairly** and follow your HR process.

## For employees

- Ask for specifics and what success means.
- Request the support offered.
- Keep notes and evidence of progress.
- Consider speaking to HR or a trusted adviser, and know your rights where you live.
