Most mistakes happen before the talking starts.

## Direct answer

Common negotiation mistakes include poor preparation, accepting the first offer, focusing only on price, talking too much, making threats, getting emotional and failing to get the deal in writing. Avoid them by preparing, listening and staying focused on interests.

## 10 mistakes and fixes

1. **No preparation:** know goals, limits and alternatives.
2. **Accepting too fast:** check against your BATNA.
3. **Only haggling on price:** explore other terms.
4. **Talking too much:** ask and listen.
5. **Ignoring their interests:** ask why.
6. **Bluffing:** be honest.
7. **Rushing:** take time.
8. **Getting emotional:** pause.
9. **Giving without getting:** trade.
10. **No written agreement:** confirm it.
