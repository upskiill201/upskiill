Crashes are scary, and they are part of investing history.

## Direct answer

A market crash is a sharp, sudden drop in prices. If you have a long time horizon and a plan, many experts suggest avoiding panic selling, keeping your emergency fund separate and continuing regular contributions if you can. If you need the money soon, your plan should have protected it already.

## What to do

1. **Pause before acting.**
2. **Check your plan and timeline.**
3. **Do not sell in a panic.**
4. **Keep contributing** if your finances allow.
5. **Avoid borrowing to invest.**
6. **Speak to a qualified adviser** if you are unsure.

## What not to do

- Check prices every hour.
- Follow social media tips to "buy the dip" without a plan.
- Pull money you need for essentials.
