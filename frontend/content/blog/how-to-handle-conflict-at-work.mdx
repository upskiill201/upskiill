Conflict at work doesn't disappear when you ignore it. It gets quieter, then bigger. A few simple steps keep it manageable.

## Direct answer

To handle conflict at work: pause before reacting, speak privately, listen to the other person's side, describe the issue and its effect without blaming, find what each of you needs, and agree a specific next step. If it involves harassment, discrimination or safety, go to HR or your manager promptly.

## Step by step

1. **Cool down.** Don't reply when you're angry.
2. **Choose the moment.** Private, calm, not in front of others.
3. **Start with the goal.** "I'd like us to work well together."
4. **Describe, don't accuse.** "In the last two meetings the report was late, which delayed the team."
5. **Listen.** Ask, "How do you see it?" and summarise what you heard.
6. **Find the real issue:** priorities, a misunderstanding, workload, or style.
7. **Agree an action.** Who does what by when.
8. **Follow up.**

## Scripts

- "Can we talk about the handover? I think we may have misunderstood each other."
- "I want to understand your view. What's making this hard?"
- "I'm not comfortable with how that was said. Can we agree to discuss concerns privately?"

## Choosing how to respond

| Situation | Approach |
| :--- | :--- |
| Small misunderstanding | Quick, friendly check-in |
| Repeated behaviour | A direct, private conversation |
| A big disagreement | A structured talk or a mediator |
| Harassment or safety | Report it formally |

## For managers

- Act early.
- Talk to each person separately first.
- Focus on behaviour and impact, not personality.
- Agree clear next steps and check back.
