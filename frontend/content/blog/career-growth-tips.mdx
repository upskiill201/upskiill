Career growth is a collection of small choices. Here are ten that add up.

## Direct answer

To grow your career, do your current job well, learn the skills your next role needs, take on stretch work, build relationships, ask for feedback, keep a record of results and review your plan every few months. Growth comes from visible results and clear communication, not from time served.

## 10 tips

1. **Be excellent at today's job.** It earns trust.
2. **Know what's next.** Read job posts for the role you want and note the skills.
3. **Learn deliberately.** One skill at a time, applied at work.
4. **Take stretch work.** Volunteer for projects slightly above your level.
5. **Find a mentor.** Ask for specific advice. See [how to network for a job](/blog/how-to-network-for-a-job) for how to ask.
6. **Ask for feedback** regularly and act on it.
7. **Keep a win list.** Results, thanks, numbers.
8. **Be visible.** Share progress with your manager and team.
9. **Help others.** Teaching and mentoring build your reputation.
10. **Review every quarter.** Adjust the plan.

## For students

Start with internships, projects and volunteering. See [how to get a job with no experience](/blog/how-to-get-a-job-with-no-experience).

## When it's time to move

If you have outgrown the role and there's no path, use your win list in your next search. [How to ask for a promotion](/blog/how-to-ask-for-a-promotion) comes first if you want to stay.
