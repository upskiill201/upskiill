There is no free lunch in investing.

## Direct answer

Risk and return are linked. Investments with higher possible returns generally carry greater risk of losing money or of big swings in value. Lower-risk options like cash tend to grow more slowly. The right balance depends on your goals, timeframe and comfort with losses.

## A simple spectrum

| Lower risk, lower expected return | Higher risk, higher expected return |
| :--- | :--- |
| Cash and savings accounts | Individual company shares |
| Government bonds | Emerging market investments |
| Short-term deposits | Speculative assets |

## How to choose

1. **Define the goal** and when you need the money.
2. **Consider how you would feel** if the value dropped 20 percent.
3. **Match risk to time:** money needed soon belongs in lower-risk places.
4. **Diversify** to avoid concentrated risk.
