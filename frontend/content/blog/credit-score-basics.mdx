Lenders use your score to decide whether to lend and at what price.

## Direct answer

A credit score is a number based on your credit history that lenders use to judge risk. Scoring systems differ by country, but common factors are payment history, how much of your limit you use, the age of your accounts, new applications and the types of credit. Paying on time and keeping balances low helps most.

## Habits that help

1. **Pay at least the minimum on time,** ideally in full.
2. **Keep balances low** compared with your limits.
3. **Avoid applying for lots of credit** in a short time.
4. **Keep older accounts** open where it makes sense.
5. **Check your credit report** for errors and dispute them.

## What does not help

Quick-fix schemes and companies that promise to "repair" your credit for large fees.
