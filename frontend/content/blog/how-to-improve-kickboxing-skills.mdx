## 1. Perfecting Hip Rotation: The Secret to Explosive Power

In kickboxing, power is generated from the ground up through your legs and hips, not your arms:

* **The Pivot:** On a lead roundhouse kick, your rear support foot must pivot at least 90° to 180° outward. This opens your hip joint and allows your pelvis to rotate completely over.
* **The Whip Effect:** Keep the kicking leg loose like a baseball bat until the millisecond of impact, then tense your core and shin.

## 2. Seamless Punch-to-Kick Combination Flows

A single isolated strike is easy for an opponent to block. High-level kickboxers use punches to blind their opponent's guard before landing heavy kicks to the body or legs:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│             3 ESSENTIAL KICKBOXING COMBINATIONS             │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Combo 1 (The Classic) ──> Jab (1) + Cross (2) + Lead Hook (3) + Rear Low Kick
│  Combo 2 (Inside Cut)  ──> Cross (2) + Lead Body Hook + Lead Low Kick
│  Combo 3 (Distance)    ──> Teep (Front Kick) + Fake Teep + Rear Cross (2)
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. Shin Conditioning and Defensive Checking

Checking a kick is far safer than absorbing it on your thighs or forearms:

1. **The 45-Degree Rule:** Lift your lead knee outward at a 45-degree angle toward the incoming kick.
2. **Knee-to-Elbow Connection:** Bring your lead knee all the way up to touch your lead elbow, closing the gap so punches cannot sneak through.
