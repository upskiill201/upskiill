## The Passive Income Mindset

### Create Once, Earn Forever

The core principle of passive income: you invest time upfront to build an asset, then that asset generates revenue with minimal ongoing effort. Teyro's mission-based format makes this realistic:

- **Build a course in 1–2 weeks** (vs. months for video production)
- **Earn from day one** — Publish, share, and start earning within days
- **Low maintenance** — Update missions occasionally; no re-recording needed
- **Scalable** — 100 learners and 10,000 learners require the same effort from you

### Why Most "Passive Income" Isn't Actually Passive

Most passive income advice misses a critical truth: the "passive" part only comes AFTER significant "active" work. Teyro shortens the active phase dramatically:

- No video production (record once, upload, done)
- No complex editing (missions are text-based with interactive elements)
- No separate hosting setup (Teyro hosts everything)
- No marketing infrastructure (Teyro's catalog promotes your course)

The upfront work is genuinely 1–2 weeks, not 3–6 months. That's what makes it achievable.

## Building a Sustainable Income Stream

### The Catalog Strategy

Successful Teyro creators build catalogs, not single courses:

- **Course 1** — Your strongest topic (establish credibility)
- **Course 2** — Adjacent topic (cross-sell to existing learners)
- **Course 3** — Beginner version of advanced topic (expand audience)
- **Course 4+** — Fill gaps based on learner requests and Q&A data

Each new course benefits from your existing audience. Your Teyro profile becomes a hub learners return to.

Teyro's revenue model rewards courses that learners actually complete. This means your incentives align with your learners' success:
