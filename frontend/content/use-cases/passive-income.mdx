## Is course income really passive?

Honestly: partly. Once lessons exist, they can be sold again and again without you. But three things decide whether they *do* sell, and none of them run themselves:

| What drives revenue | Who does the work |
| :--- | :--- |
| **People finding the course** | You — content, social, email, search |
| **People trusting it** | You — a clear promise, a good first lesson |
| **People finishing and recommending it** | Shared — Teyro's habit loop, your answers and updates |

Anyone promising income "while you sleep" from day one is selling something.

## What does Teyro take off your plate?

- **Bringing learners back.** Streaks, weekly leagues, daily quests and chests give learners a reason to open your course every day.
- **Try before buying.** Paid courses give learners the first two lessons free, so good lessons sell themselves.
- **Knowing what to fix.** [Analytics](/features/analytics-dashboard) show where learners quit and which questions they miss.
- **Getting paid.** An itemised ledger and [payouts](/features/payouts) to a bank account or mobile money.

## What stays with you?

Writing a course worth finishing, answering questions in your course community, updating lessons as tools change, and telling people it exists.
