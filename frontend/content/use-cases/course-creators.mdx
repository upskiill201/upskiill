The #1 reason experts never create courses isn't lack of knowledge — it's production complexity:

- **Recording** — Equipment, lighting, audio, screen capture
- **Editing** — Cutting mistakes, adding graphics, pacing
- **Uploading** — Platform setup, course structure, pricing
- **Marketing** — Driving traffic, managing reviews, adjusting pricing

Teyro eliminates 80% of this. You don't record videos — you build missions. Each mission takes 2–5 minutes to create (vs. 15–30 minutes of equivalent video). A 10-mission course takes a fraction of the time.

### The Expertise Trap

Experts often overestimate what beginners need. Teyro's mission templates provide structure that helps creators organize knowledge into beginner-friendly progressions. You focus on *what* to teach; Teyro handles *how* to teach it.

## How Course Creation Works on Teyro

### Step 1: Choose Your Track

Select the skill or topic for your course. Teyro provides templates for popular categories (coding, business, design, productivity, AI) but you can create any subject.

### Step 2: Build Missions

Instead of recording lectures, create individual missions:

- **Learn mission** — Introduce a concept interactively (3–5 min)
- **Apply mission** — Give learners a real problem to solve (5–8 min)
- **Reflect mission** — Self-assessment to cement understanding (2–3 min)
- **Challenge mission** — Push with a harder problem (3–5 min)

Each mission type serves a different learning purpose, creating a complete learning cycle without video production.

### Step 3: Organize into a Track

Arrange missions in logical order. Teyro suggests prerequisite chains based on your track selection. You can customize the order, add prerequisites, and set difficulty levels.

### Step 4: Publish and Share

One click publishes your track to the Teyro catalog. Share it anywhere — your learners access it instantly. Teyro handles hosting, updates, and learner management.
