## What problem does Teyro solve for creators?

Completion. A course that people buy and abandon earns once and builds no reputation. Teyro's learner app is designed to bring people back every day, and your course inherits all of it.

## What do you get?

| For your learners | For you |
| :--- | :--- |
| Four-step lessons with instant feedback | [Studio](/features/course-publishing) to build and publish |
| Streaks, leagues, quests and chests | [Analytics](/features/analytics-dashboard): opens, quits, first-try misses |
| A course community after two lessons | Admin of your course community |
| First two lessons free on paid courses | Free or paid plans, coupons, [payouts](/features/payouts) |

## Who is it a good fit for?

Creators who teach **coding or AI** and care whether learners finish. If you teach another subject, Teyro isn't the right home yet.

## How do I start?

Head to [teyro.app/teach](/teach) to set up your creator profile and open Studio. Before you do, [how to turn expertise into an online course](/blog/how-to-turn-expertise-into-an-online-course) is worth a read.
