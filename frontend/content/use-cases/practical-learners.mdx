Practical learners need skills that transfer to real-world situations — not certificates that hang on a wall. Research shows that **applied practice** (solving real problems in context) produces 4× better skill transfer than passive instruction alone. Teyro structures every lesson around real scenarios: you don't watch a lecture about communication — you practice a real negotiation. You don't read about coding — you build a working feature.

## The Practical Learner's Journey

### Week 1: Foundation

You start with core concepts through interactive missions. No videos to watch — just direct instruction with immediate application. By the end of week 1, you can do the basic tasks in your skill area.

### Week 2: Application

Missions shift to real-world scenarios. You solve problems that mirror actual work situations. Feedback becomes more nuanced, pointing out both strengths and areas to improve.

### Week 3: Complexity

Challenges combine multiple skills. You analyze, decide, execute, and evaluate — just like in real work. This is where transferable skills form.

### Week 4: Mastery Check

A final challenge tests whether you can handle the full scope of a real task. Pass it, and you've demonstrated practical competence — not just knowledge.
