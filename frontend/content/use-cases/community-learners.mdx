## Why learn with other people?

Solo learning is quiet, and quiet is easy to walk away from. Other people add the small pushes that keep you going: a question answered, a win noticed, a friend's streak that is one day longer than yours.

## What does Teyro give you?

| Layer | What it is |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Course community** | Questions, wins and help from classmates and the creator — [joined after two lessons](/features/community-qa) |
| **Friends** | Follow people, see their streaks and profiles |
| **Weekly league** | Race real learners from Bronze to Diamond — [how leagues work](/features/leagues-leaderboards) |

## Why join after two lessons, not straight away?

So everyone in the community has actually started. It keeps the conversation about the course, not about whether to begin.

## What if I'm shy?

Reading counts. Many learners get most of the value from seeing other people's questions answered. When you're ready, answering someone else's question is one of the best ways to learn — see [a skill-learning app with community](/blog/skill-learning-app-with-community).
