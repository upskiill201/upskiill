## Reply bank (for the first hour of comments)
- **"Is the proof real?"** → It hasn't been peer reviewed yet. About 42% of the main results have a Lean (computer-checked) proof, and OpenAI itself says some of the unchecked ones "could have issues".
- **"What model is it?"** → OpenAI hasn't named it or released it. That's part of the problem: nobody outside can rerun it.
- **"What's the Unique Games Conjecture?"** → It's a 2002 conjecture about how well computers can approximately solve certain optimisation problems. If it's true, getting "close enough" on many of them is as hard as solving them exactly.
- **"Where do I learn this stuff?"** → Teyro: short daily lessons on using AI tools, building agents and automations. Head to teyro.app.
