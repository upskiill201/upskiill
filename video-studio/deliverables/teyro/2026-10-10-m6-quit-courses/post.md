- **"3%? Source?"** → Harvard and MIT's edX data, published in *Science* (2019): 3.13% of all participants finished in 2017–18. Paid "verified" learners finished far more often (46%), which shows commitment matters.
- **"The Duolingo stat is just correlation"** → Fair, and we label it that way: committed people build streaks too. The point is that the streak gives you something to protect every day.
- **"Gamification is a gimmick"** → It can be, if it's only points. That's why every Teyro lesson makes you write or fix real code with instant feedback; the game just gets you back tomorrow.
- **"Is it free?"** → Free to start.
