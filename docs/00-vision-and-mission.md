# Teyro Vision School — Summary

*Understand the Mission. See the Future. Build the Company.*

## What Teyro Is

Teyro is Duolingo for skills — a learning app that makes building real, practical skills as fun, engaging, and habit-forming as social media and mobile games.

More formally: Teyro is an AI-powered personalized learning platform built to solve online learning's core failure — people start courses with real intent, then lose motivation and quietly stop, long before the skill becomes useful. Teyro applies the same engagement, habit-building, and feedback design that makes social media, games, and short-form video so compelling to real, practical skill-building — without turning learning into meaningless entertainment.

This isn't a guess — it's grounded in discovery conversations with 500+ people across different markets about why they stop learning online.

## Who Teyro Is For

Teyro is two-sided:

- **Learners** — self-directed people who want to actually finish what they start learning, not just start it. They need structure, habit-building, and social/gamified accountability to stick with a skill long enough for it to matter.
- **Creators** — people who want to publish and sell courses on a platform engineered around learner retention and completion, not just content hosting. The creator ecosystem isn't a bolt-on; it's core to the business engine.

## Why Teyro Exists

Founder's belief: online learning is broken at the root — built like a textbook with a login page. Nobody keeps showing up for something that feels like a chore. Teyro exists to prove that practical learning can be rigorous *and* genuinely rewarding — those two things aren't in conflict, they just haven't been built together yet.

## The Product Experience

Every lesson runs through a four-phase architecture:

**Learn → Apply → Reflect → Deepen**

Underneath sits a full gamification layer — streaks, XP, levels, quests, achievements, leaderboards, daily/weekly rewards — plus **Tey**, Teyro's AI mascot and companion, who encourages and keeps learners company throughout. The inspiration is Duolingo, but built toward a more premium feel.

## The Vision — Today, Next, Long Term

- **Today:** Build a great learning experience and prove people will actually return and make progress.
- **Next:** Grow the learner experience, the creator ecosystem, AI capabilities, gamification, and a learning marketplace.
- **Long term:** Become the place where people discover skills, learn them, practice them, build lasting learning habits, and access creators and learning experiences from all over the world — less a library of courses, more a living learning experience with a personality and rhythm of its own.

## How Teyro Makes Money

| Revenue Engine | How It Works |
|---|---|
| Learner access | Pay-per-course, monthly — unlocks full access for that month. Broader premium subscription tier planned for the future. |
| Course marketplace | Creators publish and sell courses; current model discussed is a 70% creator / 30% Teyro revenue share. |
| Creator Studio | A premium creator offering with additional tools and value. |
| Advertising | Non-aggressive ads for free/lower-tier users over time — never at the expense of the learning experience. |

**The flywheel:** more great creators → more useful learning → more learners → more engagement → more creator earnings → more creators → a stronger marketplace → more revenue → a better product → more learners.

## Market & Competition

Teyro competes for learning time and attention broadly — not just against other course platforms.

| Category | What They Teach Us | Where Teyro Can Be Different |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube / short-form content | Discovery, convenience, attention | Turn attention into structured progress and real skill-building |
| Games | Habit, feedback, rewards | Apply proven game principles to learning that actually matters |
| Course platforms | Depth and creator expertise | Make the learning journey far more engaging and habit-forming |
| Traditional education | Structure, credentials, community | Make practical learning more accessible, flexible, and engaging |
