# Creator Course Guide — How to Build a Course on Teyro

> **Audience:** Course creators. Everything below reflects what the Course Builder actually asks for today (Sept 2026) — no aspirational features.
> **Core structure:** Course → Modules → Lessons. Every lesson runs through the 4-phase moat: **Learn → Apply → Reflect → Deepen**.

---

## Before You Start

You don't need a finished script or edited video to begin. You need:
- A working title and a one-sentence idea of the transformation you're teaching.
- At least one module's worth of content mapped out (even rough notes).
- A thumbnail image, or the willingness to make one before you submit for review.

You can save a draft and come back — nothing is lost, and nothing goes live until you explicitly submit it for review and then publish it.

---

## Step 1 — Start a Course (the 4-question wizard)

From your creator dashboard, "New Course" opens a short wizard:

1. **Course Type** — choose **Interactive Course** (the only type available today; "Practice Exam Engine" is coming soon).
2. **Working Title** — up to 60 characters. You can change this later; it's just a starting point.
3. **Category** — pick the closest fit (Development, Business, Finance & Accounting, IT & Software, Design, Marketing, Health & Fitness, Music, Teaching & Academics, Photography & Video, Lifestyle) or "I don't know yet."
4. **Weekly time commitment** — how much time you expect to invest (0–2h, 2–4h, 5h+, or undecided). This is informational, not a deadline.

Finishing the wizard creates your course and drops you into the **Course Builder**.

---

## Step 2 — Course Setup

This is the info page for your course — what learners see before they enroll.

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Title | Required, up to 80 characters |
| Subtitle | Required, up to 120 characters — one line on what learners will achieve |
| Category / Subcategory | Required. Subcategory options change based on the category you pick |
| Level | Required — Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced |
| Language | Required — English, French, Spanish, German, or Portuguese |
| Short description | Required, up to 160 characters (used in cards/search) |
| Full description | Required, rich text, minimum 50 characters |
| What Learners Will Achieve (outcomes) | 1–10 bullet points; aim for at least 3 |
| Skills taught | Free-tag list; aim for at least 3 |
| Prerequisites | Optional, up to 300 characters |
| Thumbnail | **Required to publish.** JPG/PNG/WebP, max 5MB, 1280×720 (16:9) recommended |
| Preview/trailer video | Optional — picked from a video you've already uploaded to one of your lessons, not uploaded separately |
| Price | See [Pricing](#step-5--pricing) below |

**Tip:** the outcomes and skills fields directly drive your course card and search relevance — don't leave them thin just to get past the checklist.

---

## Step 3 — Build Your Curriculum (Modules)

A **Module** is a named group of lessons (think "Week 1" or "Part 2: Fundamentals"). For each module you set:

- **Title** (required)
- **Description/goal** (optional — a line on what this module covers)

Modules can be reordered by drag-and-drop, duplicated, or deleted. Add lessons underneath each module — a course needs **at least one module, and every module needs at least one lesson**, or it can't be submitted for review.

---

## Step 4 — Build Each Lesson (Learn → Apply → Reflect → Deepen)

Every lesson has its own builder with metadata plus four tabs. Lesson-level fields:

- **Title** (required, up to 100 characters)
- **Short description** (up to 300 characters)
- **Free preview** toggle — lets non-enrolled learners try this lesson
- Duration and XP are calculated automatically (duration from your uploaded media; XP defaults to 10 and can be tuned by us for balance)

### 1. Learn — teach the concept
Pick one content type per lesson:
- **Video** — drag-and-drop upload, MP4/MOV/MKV/WebM, max 2GB. Large files upload in resumable chunks — if your connection drops, it picks up where it left off.
- **Text** — rich text editor.
- **Audio** — same upload pipeline as video, max 500MB.

Also capture a short "What You Will Learn" summary here.

### 2. Apply — practice
Today this is a **multiple-choice quiz**:
- Optional scenario/context (up to 500 characters)
- Up to 50 questions, each with answer options, a correct answer, and an explanation (you can also flag what a wrong answer's misconception is)
- Set a difficulty level, a passing score (50–100%), and whether retries are allowed

A lesson can't be published without at least one complete question (text, correct answer, 2+ options).

### 3. Reflect — reinforce
- A required prompt (rich text)
- Type: **Open** (free write) or **Guided** (a specific set of questions) — ratings and audio/video reflections are on our roadmap but not available yet
- Configure: starter prompts, minimum word count, whether it's required, peer visibility, comments, attachments

### 4. Deepen — go further
- Collection title (required) and optional description
- Up to **10 resources** — see [Lesson Resources](#lesson-resources) below
- Optional "Learning Path Suggestions" and a "Recommended Next Step" (continue, practice, project, or explore more)

**Publishing a lesson** requires Learn, Apply, and Reflect to each be marked complete. Deepen is optional. You can also save a lesson as a draft, or schedule it, without meeting these requirements yet.

---

## Lesson Resources

Attach downloadable files or links to any lesson (mainly from the Deepen tab):
- **Types:** PDF, PPTX, Figma link, external link, or video
- **File limits:** documents/zips/spreadsheets/slides up to 100MB
- Add a title, optional description, and category (Reference, Template, Exercise, Supplemental)
- Toggle whether a resource is required to complete, tracked, downloadable, and whether links open in a new tab
- Max 10 resources per lesson

## Step 5 — Pricing

Pricing is set **once, in USD**, as your course's base value:

- **Free** — price is $0.
- **Paid** — you set one number (the "Base Value," suggested starting point $30). Teyro automatically builds a 3-tier subscription ladder from it:
  - **Weekly** — roughly base ÷ 10 (minimum $0.99)
  - **Monthly** — discounted off the weekly rate (minimum $2.99) — shown as the default/best-for-learning option
  - **Yearly** — ~20% off your base value (minimum $9.99) — shown as best value

You don't set these tiers individually — just your base value, and the ladder is generated for you.

**Coupons:** you can create discount codes for your course (percentage or fixed-dollar off), schedule when they're active, cap total redemptions, and scope them to specific plans. Coupons currently discount a learner's **first payment only**, not every renewal.

**Earnings:** you keep 70–80% of net revenue depending on your creator tier, tracked per-sale with a support reference ID you can quote if a learner has a billing issue.

---

## Step 6 — Submit for Review

Before you can submit, the builder checks:
- Title is at least 6 characters, description at least 50
- Thumbnail is set
- At least one module, and every module has at least one lesson
- **Every lesson in the course is fully published** (not just saved as a draft)

Once you submit, the course is locked for editing while our team reviews it. You'll get one of three outcomes:
- **Approved** — ready to publish
- **Changes requested** — you'll get feedback and the course reopens for editing
- **Rejected** — if it was already live, it's taken down immediately

## Step 7 — Publish

Once approved, hit **Publish** — this is a separate action from submitting for review. Your course goes live in the catalog.

**Heads up:** editing *any* content in an approved/published course (a lesson, the description, anything substantive) automatically pulls it back into Draft/review status. This keeps what learners see always in sync with what we've reviewed — plan bigger content updates as a batch rather than lots of small live edits.

---

## Quick Checklist

- [ ] Title, subtitle, category, level, language, descriptions filled in
- [ ] Thumbnail uploaded (1280×720, ≤5MB)
- [ ] At least 3 outcomes, at least 3 skills
- [ ] At least one module, each with at least one lesson
- [ ] Every lesson: Learn content uploaded, Apply quiz with ≥1 valid question, Reflect prompt written
- [ ] Pricing set (free or a base value)
- [ ] Submitted for review → approved → published
