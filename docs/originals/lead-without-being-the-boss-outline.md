# How to Lead Without Being the Boss — Outline

> Leadership & Management track, course 2 (docs/originals/leadership-track-plan.md). Short Original: 5 chapters × 10 lessons.
> **Storyline: "Remi's Six-Week Fix".** Lead: Remi. Countdown: Week 1 → Week 6, and a number on the map (refunds: 21 days → 5).

## The story

Remi (22, Nairobi) finished his Synergix internship (Corporate Jargon) and got the job: junior analyst in Strategy & Operations. In his first month, the CEO, Mr Kamau, has a problem. When a delivery goes wrong, business customers wait **21 days** for a refund, and the biggest one, Mama Mboga Fresh, is threatening to leave. Brian gives the problem to Remi: "Get it under five days. You have six weeks. Kamau wants to see it."

Remi has no team. The fix needs five people from five teams, none of whom report to him and all of whom are busy:

- **Wanjiku** (Finance): owns refund approvals. Calm, exact, worried about audits.
- **Felix** (IT): can change the system, but his queue is nine weeks long.
- **Emeka** (Logistics, Lagos, by video): knows why deliveries fail; forgets he's on mute.
- **Pauline** (Marketing): owns what customers are told. Pitched with Remi in his internship.
- **Njeri** (warehouse supervisor, new): 20 years on the floor, has seen five head-office "projects" come and go.

Allies: **Zanele** (his friend, the spreadsheet genius), **Brian** (sponsor, not boss of the others), **Mrs Achieng** (unwritten rules).

Remi's arc: brilliant at understanding problems, bad at bringing people with him. He learns to lead through credibility, generosity, shared goals and follow-through.

## Chapters

**Ch1 · Week 1 · "Nobody works for me"**: power without a title, credibility, the listening tour, a stakeholder map, the first ask. Challenge: only Zanele comes to the kick-off.

**Ch2 · Week 2 · "What's in it for them?"**: currencies, asking for help, giving first, interests vs positions, remote influence (Emeka). Challenge: Wanjiku says no.

**Ch3 · Week 3 · "Make it our idea"**: shared goal, co-creation, pre-meetings, running a meeting you don't own, credit, commitments. Challenge: Pauline and Wanjiku clash in the workshop.

**Ch4 · Week 5 · "When it stalls"**: following up without nagging, disagreeing with a senior person, feedback to a peer, when and how to escalate, staying consistent, givers' boundaries, small wins. Challenge: Felix's change gets cancelled.

**Ch5 · Week 6 · "Show, don't tell"**: trust equation, persuading the CEO, making others shine, influence vs manipulation, keeping it going after you leave. Final challenge: the review with Mr Kamau, where 21 days became 4.
