# How to Motivate a Team — Outline

> Leadership & Management track (built fourth, 2026-10-16). Short Original: 5 chapters × 10 lessons.
> **Storyline: "Miracle's Rollout".** Lead: Miracle. Countdown: Week 1 → Week 8, when 300 clinics go live.

## The story

Eighteen months ago Miracle (24, from Buea) chose the Lagos health-tech startup, Afiya, over the bank job (How to Make People Listen to You). She's a developer. Then the lead of the **Clinic Success** team (the people who onboard clinics onto Afiya's app and answer their calls) quits. Folake, the COO, asks Miracle to step in: "Eight weeks until 300 clinics go live. And honestly, that team is the least motivated in the company."

- **Bisi**: first support hire, 4 years in. Warm, very funny, quietly cynical after three managers in two years.
- **Chidi**: top performer, bored, has a bank job offer in his inbox.
- **Halima**: newest, a former nurse from Kano. Brilliant with clinics, unsure she belongs in tech.
- **Emmanuel** (narrated): does the minimum, jokes the most.
- **Folake** (COO): fast, demanding, fair; thinks motivation is "a pizza problem".

Miracle's arc: from "how do I get them to work harder?" to "what's getting in their way, and what makes this work worth doing?"

## Chapters

**Ch1 · Week 1 · "Nobody wants to be here"**: motivation myths, what you assume about people, hygiene first, the three needs, stay interviews. Challenge: Chidi shows Miracle the bank offer.

**Ch2 · Week 2 · "Why does this matter?"**: task significance, the job characteristics model, meaningful goals, connecting work to impact. Challenge: Folake wants a public ticket leaderboard.

**Ch3 · Week 4 · "Let them run with it"**: rewards and intrinsic motivation, autonomy, mastery, progress, check-ins instead of micromanaging, confidence. Challenge: Chidi's idea fails in public.

**Ch4 · Week 6 · "Fair and seen"**: fairness, recognition, workload and burnout, belonging, fair rotas. Challenge: Folake offers a bonus to the top ticket-closer.

**Ch5 · Week 8 · "Rollout"**: motivation under pressure, team confidence, a hard week run humanely, celebrating, the leader's own energy. Final challenge: rollout week, and Chidi's decision.
