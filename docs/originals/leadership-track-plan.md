# Leadership & Management — Track Plan

> A new Teyro Originals category: **Leadership & Management** (founder, 2026-10-16).
> 16 short courses, each **5 chapters × 10 lessons** (`format: 'short'`: Checkpoint at lesson 5, Chapter Challenge at lesson 10).
> Built one at a time. After each course: checker, type-checks, preview, local commit, and a report. Pushed to staging only after the founder's go-ahead.

## How every course in this track works

- **One simple, addictive storyline per course.** One lead, one goal, a countdown on the map (founder: "easy to follow"). No frame devices.
- **Lives move forward** (CAST.md rule 2). The shared cast grows into leaders: Lucía runs a team, Remi leads without a title, Ada hires, Arjun mentors.
- **Bus-friendly.** 10–12-minute lessons, tap-only exercises on a phone, and a story beat in every lesson, so a learner can stop at any stop and pick up at the next.
- **Research first.** Every claim is sourced in the lesson `notes`, with hedging where evidence is thin (no "60% of new managers fail" folklore).
