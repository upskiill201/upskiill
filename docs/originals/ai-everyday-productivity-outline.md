How it differs from the other AI Originals: **AI at Work** is Ada's job, **Study Smarter with AI** is Kofi's schoolwork, **The AI Stack** is building AI. This one is ordinary life: a party, a flat, a complaint, a language, a week where everything breaks.

## Storyline: Lucía's Impossible Month

Lucía (Mexico City, newly promoted marketing lead at Nopal) writes four things on the fridge on a Sunday night:

1. **Mamá and Papá's 35th anniversary**, in Oaxaca on Day 30. She volunteered. 60 guests, MX$40,000.
2. **Move out.** Her landlord is selling; she has until Day 28.
3. **Portuguese.** Carmen wants her to open the São Paulo distributor meeting in Portuguese.
4. **Her actual job.**

She has never used AI for anything but a joke. **Ada** (Lagos), whom Lucía mentored through her negotiations, returns the favour by voice note: she uses AI every day at work. **Marta** (Lucía's best work friend) is the sceptic. **Tomás** pastes everything into AI and trusts every word: the cautionary tale. **Sofía** quietly checks sources. **Carmen** sets the São Paulo deadline. Narrated only: Mamá (Rosa), Papá (Ernesto), her brother Mateo (helps by sending memes), Doña Lupe (the Oaxaca venue owner), the landlord Señor Ibarra.

**Running device:** Lucía's prompt cards, a deck of prompts that worked, v1–v5 (lesson 9 of every chapter, Tap to explore). The learner builds their own kit alongside.

## Facts used (author notes carry the sources)

- AI chat tools generate text by predicting likely next words from patterns in training data; they can state false things confidently ("hallucinations").
- Tools have a training cut-off; some can search the web and show links, which still need opening and checking.
- Bastani et al. (2024, "Generative AI Can Harm Learning", ~1,000 high-school maths students in Turkey): unrestricted chat access raised practice scores but students did worse on the later unassisted exam than students without it; a tutor-style version that gave hints instead of answers largely removed the harm.
- Retrieval practice (testing effect): Roediger & Karpicke (2006). Spacing: Cepeda et al. (2006 meta-analysis).
- Privacy: tool providers may store chats and, depending on settings, use them to improve models; don't paste passwords, ID numbers, bank details or other people's private information.
