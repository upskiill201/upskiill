# Teyro Platform - Product Definition

## Users
- African and global learners seeking high-quality, affordable online education
- Professionals looking to upskill in tech, business, and creative fields
- Career changers and students wanting practical, project-based learning
- Instructors and experts wanting to share knowledge and earn revenue

## Product Purpose
Teyro is a next-generation online learning platform that helps people build real skills through project-based learning, AI guidance, mentorship, and real-world practice. Unlike traditional online courses that are boring and ineffective, Teyro makes learning engaging, practical, and results-focused.

## Brand Positioning
Teyro is positioned as a premium, modern alternative to platforms like Udemy and Coursera, specifically designed for the global market with a focus on the African context. We're "Duolingo for learning skills" - making consistent, daily learning achievable through gamified, habit-forming systems.

## Anti-references
- NOT like traditional online course platforms (Udemy, Coursera) that focus on passive watching
- NOT like generic EdTech platforms with boring, lecture-heavy content
- NOT like platforms with low completion rates and ineffective learning methods
- NOT like platforms that don't help users stay consistent or build real skills

## Strategic Principles
1. **Project-Based Learning First** - Learning by doing, not watching
2. **AI-Powered Guidance** - Personalized help, not generic content
3. **Mentorship Integration** - Human connection, not isolation
4. **Habit Formation** - Consistent learning, not binge-watching
5. **Global Accessibility** - Works for African and emerging markets
6. **Results-Oriented** - Build real skills, get real outcomes
